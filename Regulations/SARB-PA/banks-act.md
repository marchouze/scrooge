# Banks Act 94 of 1990

**Curator:** Mira · **Status:** POPULATED · **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06

## Citation

- **Title:** Banks Act, 1990 (Act No. 94 of 1990).
- **Promulgated:** 1990; **as amended** through subsequent Banking Acts and the FSR Act of 2017.
- **Authority:** South African Reserve Bank Prudential Authority (PA).
- **Companion:** Regulations Relating to Banks (Government Gazette 35950, 12 December 2012, as amended).
- **Source:** [resbank.co.za](https://www.resbank.co.za) — Prudential Authority publications.

## Scope and applicability

The Banks Act applies to every entity registered as a bank in the Republic of South Africa. Once licensed, the bank is bound by:

- The Act itself (capital, reserves, returns, governance, disclosure, sanctions).
- The Regulations Relating to Banks (operational detail, BA-return forms, capital and liquidity formulas, large-exposures limits).
- Directives, circulars, and guidance notes issued by the PA from time to time.
- The FSR Act framework (Twin Peaks) — the PA is the prudential regulator under the Act.

The bank is **pre-licence** as of 2026-05-06; the licence application is forthcoming. Obligations are designed-against today; activation follows licensing.

## Key obligations

### Capital

- **s.70 + Regs Ch.6** — Maintain capital and reserve funds at not less than the minima prescribed (CET1, AT1, T2). Includes Pillar 2A add-ons set by PA. Capital conservation buffer + countercyclical buffer where required.
- **Regs** — Submit BA 100 (capital and reserve funds) to PA on the prescribed cadence.
- **s.79** — Capital instrument issuance and redemption requires PA notification / approval.

### Liquidity

- **Regs Ch.6** — Maintain LCR ≥ 100%; report via BA 325.
- **Regs Ch.6** — Maintain NSFR ≥ 100%; report via BA 326.
- **PA guidance** — Intraday liquidity monitoring per BCBS 248.

### Large exposures

- **Reg 24(6)–(8) + Directive 3 of 2022** — Single-name and connected-counterparty exposures capped per the regulatory ceiling; reported via the BA 200-series credit-risk return family. *(NB: BA 330 is the IRRBB repricing-gap return, not the large-exposures return — see `Regulations/SARB-PA/large-exposures.md` §7.)*

### Risk management

- **s.60A** — Establish risk management and compliance functions with prescribed independence and access.
- **PA practice** — Submit annual ICAAP and ILAAP; engage with PA on outcomes.

### Governance

- **s.60** — Designate Chief Executive Officer; ensure board governance per fit-and-proper standards.
- **s.60B** — Conduct and conduct-related supervision (interfaces with FSR Act / FSCA).
- **PA fit-and-proper standards** — All designated persons (CEO, CRO, CFO, CCO, CISO, etc.) must be fit-and-proper.
- **Joint Standard 2 of 2024** — Cyber-resilience accountability (named officer).

### Reporting

- **Regs** — BA 100, 200, 300, 325, 326, 330 + supporting returns at prescribed cadence.
- **s.75** — Annual financial statements, audited.
- **s.75A** — Disclosure requirements (Pillar 3 equivalents per Regs).

### Audit

- **s.61** — External auditor appointed; auditor independence; auditor reporting duties to PA.
- **s.62** — Internal audit function (interfaces with IIA IPPF and BCBS 223).

### Restricted activities

- **s.11–14** — Activities restricted to licensed banks.
- **s.74** — Subordination provisions; depositor preference.

### Resolution / failure

- **PA framework** — Recovery and resolution planning (interfaces with BCBS Key Attributes).
