## 2. Fit-and-proper requirements the steady-state holder must satisfy

The fit-and-proper requirements below are the categories the steady-state holder (me, when human-Saskia is appointed) must satisfy. Citations are to the controlling FAIS instruments. Where the institutional-only / wholesale posture creates a scope question, the row carries `[citation: TBC]` rather than asserting a counsel-only answer; counsel confirms at the licence-application gate.

### 2.1 Honesty, integrity, good standing

- **Citation.** FAIS Act s.8(1)(a); Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017, Chapter 2 (Honesty, Integrity and Good Standing) `[citation: TBC — exact 2017 Determination chapter and paragraph references to be reconciled against the version in force at the licence-application gate; FSCA has revised the Determination since first issuance]`.
- **Form of evidence.** Background checks (criminal record check; civil judgement check; insolvency check); financial-services regulatory history (no debarment, no withdrawal, no suspension); declaration of any disciplinary findings by professional bodies; sworn affidavit on undisclosed matters.
- **Owner.** The seat-holder. Coordinated by Owen (CoSec, secretariat for fit-and-proper file structure) and Zara (CCO, fit-and-proper policy line).

### 2.2 Competence — knowledge, skills, qualifications

- **Citation.** FAIS Act s.8(1)(b); Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017, Chapter 3 (Competence) `[citation: TBC — chapter / paragraph reference per current Determination]`.
- **Form of evidence.** Recognised qualification (FSCA-approved qualification list for FSP Categories the bank holds — for an institutional-only wholesale dealer, this is most likely Categories I and II `[citation: TBC]`); regulatory examinations (RE 1 for Key Individuals; RE 5 for Representatives where applicable) `[citation: TBC — RE-exam regime to be confirmed against the version in force at the licence-application gate; FSCA has revised the regulatory-examination structure since first issuance]`; experience requirements (minimum years in financial services / asset class); continuous professional development (CPD) hours per the relevant cycle.
- **Substitution surface.** The bank's institutional-only / wholesale posture may scope out of some product-specific competence requirements (the heavy retail-product competence regime is unlikely to apply); this is itself a counsel question — see Part 2 of this work, the PAX dispatch.
- **Owner.** Seat-holder. Substantive review by Zara at appointment; counsel review at licence-application gate.

### 2.3 Operational ability

- **Citation.** FAIS Act s.8(1)(c); Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017, Chapter 4 (Operational Ability) `[citation: TBC]`.
- **Form of evidence.** The bank itself meets the operational-ability standard for the FSP at the entity level; the KI is responsible for ensuring the bank maintains the standard. Evidence aggregates from the operational-resilience framework (Devon-owned; `ORG-PR-18`), the cyber-resilience framework (Rashida-owned; `ORG-CY-02`), the change-management framework (Atlas-owned), and the records-management framework (Owen-owned; `ORG-FC-05`).
- **Owner.** Devon (operational resilience) + Rashida (cyber resilience) + Atlas (change management) at the substrate layer; Saskia (KI) at the named-accountability layer.

### 2.4 Financial soundness

- **Citation.** FAIS Act s.8(1)(d); Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017, Chapter 5 (Financial Soundness) `[citation: TBC]`.
- **Form of evidence.** At the entity level, the bank meets the FSP financial-soundness standard via its banking-licence capital adequacy regime (Camille-owned; `ORG-PR-01` through `ORG-PR-05`); the KI personally is required to be of sound financial standing (no current insolvency, no recent sequestration `[citation: TBC]`).
- **Owner.** Camille at the entity layer; seat-holder personally for personal-soundness disclosure.

### 2.5 Continuity and oversight obligations

- **Citation.** FAIS Act s.8 read with the General Code of Conduct (Board Notice 80 of 2003 as amended) `[citation: TBC — current version of the General Code to be confirmed at the licence-application gate]`; FSCA conduct standards.
- **Form of evidence.** The KI has substantive oversight of the FSP's representatives, advice processes, suitability records, complaints handling, and conflict-of-interest management; designation of an alternate KI is recommended-practice where workload or continuity requires it `[citation: TBC — alternate-KI designation guidance to be confirmed]`.
- **Substitution surface.** The bank's institutional-only / wholesale posture may not generate "advice" in the FAIS sense (counterparties are likely classed as institutional / professional and may be exempt from advice-record requirements under FAIS subordinate legislation `[citation: TBC]`); if so, the KI's oversight load is materially smaller than for a retail FSP. This is the heart of the Part 2 PAX research dispatch.
