### 2.2 Required attributes — must-haves

The named human CRO must demonstrably satisfy each of the following at appointment, with the evidence preserved for SARB pre-application engagement:

1. **Senior risk-function track record at a SARB-licensed bank or equivalent jurisdiction** — minimum 10 years at deputy-CRO level or above, including at least one full ICAAP / ILAAP cycle as an accountable signatory or equivalent. [citation: BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks, Principle 6 — operative experience requirement; `ORG-GV-10`.]
2. **Three-lines-of-defence independence** — no first-line accountabilities (revenue-generating, customer-facing, P&L-owning) within the immediate prior 24 months. [citation: BCBS CG Principles 6; JS 1 of 2024 operational-independence reading per `ORG-GV-CRO-INDEPENDENCE`, register row pending Mira.]
3. **PA fit-and-proper qualification** — meets the SARB Prudential Authority's continuous fit-and-proper standards: honesty and integrity, competence and capability, financial soundness, no disqualifying convictions or regulatory findings. [citation: `ORG-HR-11`; `ORG-GV-11`; PA Fit-and-Proper Standards reference [TBC — exact gazette / standard cite pending Mira register].]
4. **JS 1 of 2024 fit-and-proper specifically** — risk-function operational independence assessable; cyber-risk-aware (the CRO carries the risk-appetite line on cyber per `ORG-CY-01`, even though Rashida-as-CISO is the named accountable executive for the cyber programme itself). [citation: `ORG-CY-01`; JS 1 of 2024 §20 governance-and-accountability framing.]
5. **Risk-taxonomy fluency across the bank's product perimeter** — institutional global-markets trading bank: market risk (FRTB/D352/D457 idiom), credit risk (BCBS standardised + IFRS 9 ECL), counterparty-credit (SA-CCR), liquidity (LCR/NSFR/IRRBB), operational and operational-resilience risk, model risk (SR 11-7 / SS 1/23 idiom), climate-related risk (PA Guidance Note 1 of 2024). [citation: `ORG-PR-04` through `ORG-PR-22`; `ORG-CY-08`; `ORG-AC-02`.]
6. **Direct access to the Board / BRC, in writing in the appointment letter** — non-negotiable. [citation: `ORG-GV-10`; BCBS CG Principle 6.]
7. **No disqualifying conflicts** — no current directorship, advisory, or material shareholding in any counterparty the bank intends to onboard in the institutional-only wholesale dealer franchise. Conflicts register entry at appointment per `Procedures/by-policy/conflicts-declaration.md`.
8. **English working proficiency at regulator-engagement standard** — supervisory letters, SREP-equivalent dialogue, ICAAP / ILAAP authorship.

### 2.3 Strongly-preferred attributes

- Prior named-CRO accountability at a SARB-licensed or comparable jurisdiction's licensed bank (i.e. has carried the seat once already, not stepping up for the first time). [Rationale: licence-application credibility; SARB pre-application engagement reads strongly on this.]
- AI-substrate-aware — the bank's risk function is engineered into autonomous agents under Principle 7. The human CRO must be willing to govern (rather than rebuild) the engineering line, on the engineering-vs-governance split CLAUDE.md formalises.
- Familiarity with event-sourced architectures and the implication for risk reporting (Principle 1: every risk number is a query over the event log, not a stored balance). The CRO does not need to engineer this; the CRO needs to *trust and govern* it.
- Climate-risk fluency at PA Guidance Note 1 of 2024 depth.

### 2.4 Disqualifying attributes

- Active first-line role within prior 24 months (per §2.2 #2).
- Any unresolved supervisory finding personally addressed at the candidate (PA, FSCA, foreign equivalent).
- Any conflict in §2.2 #7 that cannot be resolved by appointment.
- Inability or unwillingness to sign ICAAP / ILAAP as a named accountable officer.

## 3. Mandate boundary — CRO seat versus engineering line

The CRO seat is the **governance** seat. The engineering line — Rohan (risk engineer), Nadia (independent-validation engineer, peer-in-second-line) — continues under the CRO's eventual oversight as the operating substrate that produces the measurements, validations, and projections the CRO governs. The split mirrors the engineering-vs-governance architecture CLAUDE.md formalises (Principle 7 corollary).

| Boundary | Governance CRO (human, licence-day) | Engineering line (Rohan, Nadia, agents) |
|---|---|---|
| RAS / RMF authorship and sign-off | Owns; signs to BRC | Drafts, measures, supports |
| ICAAP / ILAAP signature | Signs (with CFO co-sign) | Builds the engine; populates the inputs |
| Appetite-breach disposition | Decides (per RAS §6 tier framework) | Detects, surfaces, recommends |
| Model-risk approvals | Approves within validated envelope | Builds models (Rohan); validates independently (Nadia) |
| BRC papers | Approves for tabling, presents | Generates from event log (Anya substrate) |
| Supervisory engagement on risk | Owns the dialogue | Provides the underlying numbers and methodology |
| Three-lines-of-defence integrity | Owns the second-line discipline | Operates within the second-line discipline; Vera (third line) tests |

The engineering line's reporting through the CRO is unchanged from the present arrangement (Rohan → Helena; Nadia → Helena, functionally independent of Rohan). The human CRO replaces Helena-the-interim-governance-shell, not Helena-the-coordinated-engineering-line; Helena's `/Team/Helena.md` operating spec continues to describe the engineering-coordination function the CRO governs.

## 4. Helena interim posture — handover at human-CRO appointment

Until the human CRO is appointed, Helena is the **interim governance shell**: signs ICAAP / ILAAP placeholders for the build-phase rehearsal cycle (with Marc-as-CEO co-sign per `ORG-GV-17` interim posture), operates the BRC-pre-Board through the Interim Risk Forum, and engages Mira and Senna on the obligations-register and cyber-risk lines.

3. The supervisory-correspondence intake (Owen secretariat) re-routes the CRO-addressed mail to the human CRO; Helena retains the engineering coordination intake.
4. The conflicts register entry for Helena's BRC-chair-and-CRO dual role (per `/Team/Helena.md` §15) closes; the human CRO inherits a clean conflicts posture.
5. The Helena `/Team/Helena.md` operating spec is amended (touch-time upgrade) to reflect the engineering-coordination-only scope, removing the interim-governance-shell language.
6. Helena's substrate-gap inventory (Helena spec §16) transfers ownership of the *governance* gaps (BRC-paper generator, supervisory-correspondence register) to the human CRO; the *engineering* gaps (risk-appetite monitoring projection, ICAAP / ILAAP engine) remain with Rohan + Atlas as before.

The CRO-as-Helena interim posture is the bank's **build-phase shell**, not a permanent arrangement. The CEO decision converts the interim into a hard appointment cadence: the named human CRO is the seat SARB sees at licence application.
