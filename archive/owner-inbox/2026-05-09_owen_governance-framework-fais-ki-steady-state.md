## Unchanged-6-human-composition rationale

The licence-day human roster set by D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM (CEO modify, 2026-05-08) is **six separate humans + Marc + external audit firm**:

1. Independent Chair (NED).
2. NED #2.
3. NED #3.
4. Company Secretary.
5. Triple-hatted Compliance Lead (MLRO + FIC Compliance Officer + POPIA Information Officer).
6. Chief Risk Officer (human seat).

The FAIS Key Individual designation under FAIS Act 37 of 2002 s.8 is a **regulatory designation carried by an existing seat-holder**, not a separate seat on the licence-day roster. Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance) carries this designation as part of her standing-agent role in the governance fleet — the human FAIS KI appointment crystallises when Saskia's fit-and-proper file (Saskia's PR #45 Gate (b)) completes.

Marc-interim (CEO + executive director + Public Officer + interim Banks Act CFO + interim FAIS KI) covers the FAIS KI accountability until that point. The interim arrangement does not enlarge the licence-day roster; it parks the designation on the CEO until the named agent's fit-and-proper file is in place.

This is why the FAIS KI confirmation does **not** change the headline composition: 6 + Marc + audit firm.
