Obligations register

A consolidated, filterable register of every obligation the bank carries under applicable regulation, mapped to where it is fulfilled in the bank's policy stack. The operational expression of Principle 2.

How to use: filter by domain, status, or fulfilment owner; or search by text (citation, requirement, policy, owner). Click an obligation row's source citation to read the underlying analysis (where the instrument file is populated). The register is a projection over the per-instrument analyses; reproducible at any as-of date (P1).
