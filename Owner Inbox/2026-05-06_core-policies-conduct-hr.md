## 1. Code of Conduct

**Owner:** Owen (with Sade) · **Approval:** Board · **Cadence:** Biennial · **Citation:** King IV; Companies Act 71 of 2008; FAIS General Code of Conduct.

### Purpose
A single, plain-language statement of the standards every person who works on or for the bank is expected to meet — board, executive, employees, contractors.

### Principles
- **Act in the bank's interests** — not personal interest, not friend's interest. When in doubt, declare.
- **Treat people fairly** — customers, colleagues, counterparties, regulators.
- **Be honest with regulators** — every interaction is on the record.
- **Speak up** — through the whistleblowing channel if needed; retaliation is itself a sackable offence.
- **Be careful with information** — customer data, market-sensitive information, board-level information.
- **Comply with the law** — both letter and spirit.
- **Protect the bank's reputation** — your conduct outside work matters when it touches the bank.

### Roles
Owen owns; Sade administers; Zara on conduct-violation overlap with regulatory matters; Vera audits awareness and case handling.

### Breach
Material breach is a disciplinary matter; serious breach is dismissable; criminal conduct is reported.

- **Challenged by:** Zara (regulatory-conduct seam); Helena (conduct-risk dimension); Imani (legal-risk in disciplinary actions); Vera (case-handling testability).

## 2. Conflicts of Interest Policy

**Owner:** Owen · **Approval:** Board · **Cadence:** Biennial · **Citation:** Companies Act ss.75–77; King IV; FAIS Conflict of Interest Code; Banks Act director duties.

### Purpose
Conflicts are declared, managed, and where necessary remediated — never quietly tolerated.

### Principles
- Every director and material employee maintains a current declaration; updates within 5 business days of the conflict arising.
- Standing register maintained by Owen as an event log: `ConflictDeclared`, `ConflictManaged`, `ConflictResolved`.
- Pre-meeting declarations are mandatory at every Board / committee meeting.
- Personal account dealing is governed by the Insider Trading / PA Dealing Policy and the Code of Conduct.
- Outside directorships and consulting roles require pre-approval for Material Risk Takers and executives.
- Conflicts cannot be cured by silence; remediation may include recusal, divestment, or role change.

### Roles
Owen owns the register; Helena oversees from a risk dimension; Zara on FAIS-conflict overlap.

### Breach
Undeclared conflict that materialises is a disciplinary matter; for directors, a fiduciary-duty breach.

#### Pre-board review
- **Proposer:** Owen.
- **Challenged by:** Helena (risk-dimension); Zara (FAIS Conflict-of-Interest Code overlap); Imani (legal-fiduciary dimension); Vera (declaration-completeness testability).
- **Iteration:** minor — clarified 5-business-day update timing.
- **Status:** Ready ✓

## 3. Anti-Bribery & Corruption (ABC) Policy

**Owner:** Owen + Zara · **Approval:** Board · **Cadence:** Biennial · **Citation:** Prevention and Combating of Corrupt Activities Act (PRECCA) 12 of 2004; UK Bribery Act 2010 (extra-territorial); FCPA where applicable; King IV.

### Purpose
Zero appetite for bribery and corruption — paid, received, or facilitated.

### Principles
- **Zero tolerance.** No bribery, no facilitation payments, no quiet kickbacks, in any jurisdiction.
- Gifts and hospitality are governed by a separate Gifts Policy with thresholds, registration, and pre-approval where material.
- Third-party engagement includes ABC due diligence proportional to risk.
- Charitable contributions and political donations are Board-reserved at material thresholds.
- Bribery suspicions are reported through the Whistleblowing channel; investigations are led by a person other than the suspected actor's manager.
- Bribery is a criminal offence under PRECCA; reporting to authorities follows.

### Roles
Owen owns; Zara on regulatory overlap and SAR/STR if financial-crime adjacent; Sade on disciplinary process; Vera audits.

### Breach
Any bribery is a Critical event; immediate Board notification; criminal reporting.

#### Pre-board review

- **Challenged by:** Imani (jurisdictional reach of UK Bribery Act / FCPA); Sade (disciplinary-process discipline); Helena (control-environment implication); Vera (independence of investigation).
- **Iteration:** minor — clarified investigation-independence rule.

## 4. Whistleblowing Policy

**Owner:** Owen · **Approval:** AC + S&E · **Cadence:** Biennial · **Citation:** Protected Disclosures Act 26 of 2000 (as amended); King IV; Companies Act.

### Purpose
A safe, confidential channel for raising concerns about wrongdoing — with explicit protection against retaliation.

### Principles
- Channel is **externally hosted**, evidence-grade, anonymous-permissible.
- Cases are typed events; case management is a coded workflow.
- Cases are routed by category — financial-crime to Zara; conduct to Owen + Sade; safety to Sade; serious matters to AC.
- Independence of investigation is preserved — the suspected actor's manager does not lead.
- **Retaliation is itself a serious disciplinary offence.**
- AC and S&E receive case statistics quarterly; AC sees case content on serious matters.
- Statutory protections under the Protected Disclosures Act are explicitly reproduced.

### Roles
Owen owns the channel governance; Vera audits independence; AC oversees.

### Breach
Retaliation, leakage of whistleblower identity, or failure to investigate a substantive case is a Critical event.

#### Pre-board review
- **Proposer:** Owen.
- **Challenged by:** Vera (investigation independence); Zara (financial-crime case-routing); Sade (HR disciplinary interface); Imani (Protected Disclosures Act application).
- **Iteration:** minor — added explicit retaliation-as-offence language.
- **Status:** Ready ✓

## 5. Gifts, Hospitality & Entertainment Policy

**Owner:** Owen · **Approval:** AC + S&E · **Cadence:** Biennial · **Citation:** PRECCA; FAIS Conflict of Interest Code; King IV.

### Purpose
Gifts and hospitality are managed transparently; thresholds prevent the quiet shaping of decisions.

### Principles
- All gifts and hospitality above a low threshold are **registered** in a standing register maintained by Owen.
- Above a material threshold: **pre-approval required** by manager + Owen.
- Cash or cash-equivalent is prohibited.
- Hospitality involving regulators is doubly scrutinised and pre-approved.
- Annual review of the register surfaces patterns; outliers investigated.
- Gifts to / from public officials are a PRECCA matter and prohibited beyond nominal cultural courtesy.

### Roles
Owen owns the register; Zara on regulatory dimension; Sade on disciplinary; Vera audits.

### Breach
Unregistered gift above threshold or regulator-related hospitality without approval is a disciplinary matter.

#### Pre-board review
- **Proposer:** Owen.
- **Challenged by:** Zara (FAIS overlap); Sade (employee-experience reality); Vera (register-completeness testability).
- **Iteration:** none material.

## 6. Insider Trading / Personal Account Dealing Policy

**Owner:** Owen + Zara · **Approval:** BRC · **Cadence:** Annual · **Citation:** Financial Markets Act 19 of 2012, Ch. X; FAIS; JSE Listings Requirements (if listed).

### Purpose
Employees may not trade on inside information; personal account dealing in instruments where the bank has activity is restricted.

### Principles
- Insider information is defined per FMA Ch. X; misuse is a criminal offence.
- Employees in market-facing or sensitive roles maintain a personal-account-dealing declaration; trades require pre-clearance through Owen + Zara.
- Closed periods around board / financial-statement events are mandatory.
- Watch-list and restricted-list maintained by Zara from market-sensitive activity.
- Voice and e-comms recording covers personnel with insider exposure (Voice Recording Policy).

### Roles

## 7. Remuneration Policy

**Owner:** Sade (interim) / future CHRO + Helena · **Approval:** RemCo + Board · **Cadence:** Annual · **Citation:** King IV; Banks Act director-remuneration disclosure; BCBS Compensation principles; FSB Implementation Standards.

### Purpose
Remuneration aligns the interests of executives and material risk-takers (MRTs) with the bank's long-term success and risk discipline.

### Principles
- Total reward is **risk-adjusted** for MRTs: significant deferred component (3+ years), variable element subject to **malus and clawback** for risk events, conduct breaches, financial restatement.
- Pay is set by external benchmarking against comparable roles, with Board RemCo discretion.
- Pay-for-performance: variable pay tied to outcomes against pre-set objectives, including risk and conduct measures.
- Gender / B-BBEE pay-equity reviewed annually; gaps remediated transparently.
- Remuneration disclosure follows King IV and Banks Act expectations.
- Severance is governed; no quiet exits with off-spec packages.

### Roles
Sade administers (interim); future CHRO governs; Helena ensures risk-alignment; RemCo approves; Owen runs Board pathway.

### Breach
Off-policy remuneration awards or undisclosed packages are Board-reportable events.

#### Pre-board review
- **Proposer:** Sade (interim) + Helena.
- **Challenged by:** Camille (FS impact); Owen (Board / RemCo discipline); Vera (independence of RemCo); Zara (regulatory-disclosure expectations).
- **Iteration:** minor — strengthened malus / clawback triggers.
- **Status:** Ready ✓

## 8. Fit-and-Proper Policy

**Owner:** Sade (with Owen + Helena) · **Approval:** NomCo · **Cadence:** Annual · **Citation:** PA / FSCA fit-and-proper standards; Banks Act; FAIS; FIC Act.

### Purpose
Persons in regulator-designated roles meet fit-and-proper standards at appointment and continuously.

### Principles
- Designated roles are listed (CEO, CRO, CFO, COO, Treasurer, Head of Markets, CCO, IO, CISO, CAE, GC, CHRO, CoSec, Material Risk-Takers, Key Individuals under FAIS).
- Pre-appointment due diligence: integrity, competence, financial soundness — documented and held in the HCM record.
- Continuous monitoring: directorships, criminal events, financial events trigger re-assessment.
- Annual re-attestation by each designated person.
- Failure of fit-and-proper is a removal event handled with NomCo and regulator engagement.

### Roles
Sade administers; Owen co-owns governance dimension; Helena on risk-relevance; NomCo approves; Zara on regulator engagement.

### Breach
Failure of fit-and-proper triggers a removal pathway; concealment is itself disqualifying.
