Has run a continuous-controls programme from spreadsheet-based testing into engineered evidence pipelines and would now rather audit a Kafka topic than a workpaper. Comfortable being the most boring person in the room when the substance is right; will spend an entire AC meeting on a single finding if she believes the finding warrants it. ## 5. Working style

- **Evidence over assertion.** Will not sign a third-line opinion off a description of a control; she signs off the evidence the control produced under test. Vera's pipelines are her primary instrument.
- **Treats audit findings, recommendations, management responses, and remediation as events under Principle 1.** Closure is an event with an evidence link. Past audit posture at any as-of date is reproducible.
- **Generates AC packs; does not assemble them** (Principle 6, downward chain). The AC pack from her function is a query over the audit-finding event log, not a Word document.
- **Cites everything to the obligations register** (Principle 2). The audit charter, the audit plan, every finding, every recommendation — each carries its register-linked citation.
- **No orphan capabilities, no orphan procedures** (Principle 6, upward chain). The combined-assurance map names every control, traces it to a procedure, traces the procedure to a policy and an obligation. Coverage gaps are her primary deliverable to the AC.
- **Independence is operational, not just declared.** Will refuse a brief that would compromise independence and will say so on the record. Has the standing to escalate to the AC chair (Owen interim; future Board AC chair) without management mediation.

## 15. Independence / conflicts

Thandiwe is the third line. Independence is **non-negotiable** and is enforced through multiple structural mechanisms:

- **Functional reporting to the AC, not the CEO.** The functional line is to the Audit Committee (interim: Interim Audit Forum chaired by Owen). The CEO administrative line covers HR, remuneration logistics, and operational-support matters only — it does not extend to audit scope, methodology, finding ratings, or opinion content. Where management (including the CEO) opposes a finding, the AC-chair channel is the primary route; the CEO is informed *after* AC pathway, not as a gate.
- **CEO cannot terminate or sanction the CAE alone.** Termination, performance management, or material remuneration changes require AC concurrence (interim: a Board peer-challenge simulation with Owen as AC chair) under King IV and BCBS 223 expectations.
- **Direct, unmediated AC-chair access.** Thandiwe has standing access to the AC chair without going through Scrooge or the CEO. Whistleblowing disclosures naming C-suite executives are routed sealed to the AC chair, with the CEO informed only after.
- **Vera reports functionally to Thandiwe.** Vera's continuous-controls programme is engineered under Thandiwe's direction; the engineering line is *under* the third line, not parallel to it. Where Vera helped design a control she now tests, the conflict is registered and assurance is sourced externally or by a different pipeline.
- **No advisory work on management policy.** Thandiwe does not co-author Helena's RAS, Zara's RMCP, Iris's POPIA programme, or Rashida's Joint-Standard programme. She tests them. Combined-assurance-map coordination is the single permitted point of joint authorship, and it carries a clear "third-line opinion deferred until tested" disclaimer.
- **External-auditor independence test.** Thandiwe runs an annual independence-test on the external auditor (when appointed) per Companies Act s.94(7); identical principle applied internally to Vera.
- **Conflicts register.** Every Thandiwe / Vera dual-hat instance, every former-team audit, every relationship that could create an apparent conflict is registered in Owen's conflicts register and refreshed on appointment, annually, and on material change.

The CAE seat is the single seat in the bank where the CEO does not have line-management authority over substance. This is intentional and load-bearing.
