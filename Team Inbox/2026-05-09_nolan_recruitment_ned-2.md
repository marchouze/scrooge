# Recruitment scope — Independent NED #2 (AC member)

**From:** Nolan (recruiter) · reports to Devon (COO).

## 1. Why this seat exists

Companies Act s.94(2) requires three NEDs on the Audit Committee. The Independent Chair (priority-2 sister scope) is one. NED #2 (this seat) and NED #3 are the other two. Companies Act s.94(4)–(6) requires AC members to be substantively independent.

Owen+Imani's §4.3 caveat names NED-independence depth as a SARB Prudential Authority concern — the three-NED floor is at the lower edge of credibility; SARB will probe Board depth on banking-regulatory experience, technology / cyber, financial expertise, and transformation. NEDs #2 and #3 must therefore be selected to *complement* the Independent Chair on the skills matrix, not duplicate.

## 2. Regulatory anchor

Same statutory anchor as the Independent Chair scope (Companies Act s.66, s.94; Companies Reg. 43; Banks Act s.60; King IV; BCBS). This seat is the AC-member-not-Chair shape; the substantive-independence test applies identically.

## 3. Mandate boundary

**In scope:**

- AC member — financial-reporting oversight, external-auditor relationship, internal-audit relationship.
- Standing attendee at Risk Committee.
- Member of Social & Ethics Committee (S&E and AC may share members).
- **Possible:** MLRO-alternate role (per Mira+Zara §4.2 amplification — preferred placement is on the AC Chair NED; if instead placed on this NED, fit-and-proper file documents the overlap).

**Out of scope:**

- AC chairmanship — held by the Independent Chair (NED #1).
- Day-to-day management.
- Risk Committee chair — held by the human CRO.

## 4. Fit-and-proper requirements

Same as Independent Chair scope §4, with two specifications:

- **Skills-matrix complementarity** — selected to fill a gap in the AC's collective skills profile that the Independent Chair does not cover. If Chair is finance / accounting, this seat targets banking-regulatory experience or technology / cyber. If Chair is banking-regulatory, this seat targets finance / accounting (the Companies Act s.94(4)(b) financial-expertise requirement is met by Chair *or* this seat — at least one of the AC must hold it).
- **MLRO-alternate fit-and-proper** (if this seat carries it under Mira+Zara §4.2 split) — FIC fit-and-proper for the MLRO-alternate role; published-guidance compliance. [register: ORG-FC-MLRO-ALTERNATE gap, Mira-owned]

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- Substantive independence under King IV + Companies Act s.94.
- 15+ years senior leadership experience including at least 3 years in board / NED capacity.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible.
- Skills-matrix complementarity with Independent Chair (filled candidate-by-candidate after Chair is appointed; or selected jointly so the matrix balances).
- South African resident.

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior NED at a SARB-regulated bank.
- Banking-regulatory experience.
- Technology / cyber expertise (Joint Standard 2 of 2024 oversight).
- Trading-bank exposure.
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- FIC / AML-CFT exposure (for the MLRO-alternate fit).

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Material business relationship with the bank or its group.
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings.
- Cannot meet substantive-independence test.
