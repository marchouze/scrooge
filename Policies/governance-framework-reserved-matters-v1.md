# Governance Framework — Reserved Matters v1

> **Status:** IN FORCE (policy layer). The Board's reserved matters define the boundary between Board-level decisions and matters delegated to management. Binds from adoption.
>
> **Author:** Owen (Company Secretary, governance).
>
> **Build-phase note:** During the build phase the CEO wears the Board and shareholder seats. Reserved matters are held at CEO level until a full Board is constituted. At first NED appointment, this register is presented to the Board for formal adoption.

## 1. Board-reserved matters

The following decisions are reserved for the Board and may not be delegated to management:

### 1.1 Strategy and capital
- Approval of the annual strategy plan and business plan.
- Approval of the Risk Appetite Statement (RAS) and any material amendment.
- Approval of ICAAP and ILAAP documents.
- Capital raise, debt issuance, or material change in capital structure.

### 1.2 Governance and policy
- Approval of the Risk Management and Compliance Policy (RMCP).
- Approval of material policies (including those listed in Regulation 39(17)(b)).
- Approval of the cyber-risk framework (Joint Standard 2 of 2024 §6).
- Appointment and removal of the CEO, CRO, CCO, CAE, and CFO.
- Approval of the internal audit charter and plan.

### 1.3 Financial reporting
- Approval of annual financial statements.
- Approval of dividend declaration.
- Approval of going-concern assessment.

### 1.4 Regulatory
- Any material submission to the Prudential Authority or FSCA.
- Approval of recovery plan updates.
- Any response to a regulatory direction or enforcement action.

---

## 2. Management delegations

All matters not listed in §1 are delegated to the CEO and, by further sub-delegation, to the relevant governance seat. Sub-delegations are recorded in the bank's delegation-of-authority schedule (maintained by Owen).

---

## 3. Build-phase governance

During the build phase, all reserved matters are decided by the CEO (Marc), recorded as `Decision` events in the event store per CLAUDE.md §Operating procedures. When a full Board is constituted, decision authority migrates to the Board; prior CEO decisions remain valid.
