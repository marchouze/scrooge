## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-220-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` (the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`).
- **BA 220 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba220-contract.json` (30 cells), loaded + validated via `return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA220")`. Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA220`.

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements — the bought-in binding (Phase C batch 2)

Every BA 220 data line **is** a bought-in asset, so the form binds a small, defining attribute set on `prd:bank:credit:loan` (the acquired asset arises from a defaulted credit facility):

| Attribute | Required | Why | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assetBoughtInFlag` | **true** | a BA 220 line exists iff an asset was bought-in / acquired — the form-defining attribute (excludes hash-total / control cells) | SARB reg 23; Banks Act s.78 |
| `boughtInDate` | **true** | the "Date bought in/acquired" cell cannot populate without the acquisition date; the holding period runs against the s.78 disposal deadline | SARB reg 23; Banks Act s.78 |
| `boughtInAssetType` | false | the Companies vs ImmovableAssets sub-form carries the type; the individual cell does not need it to populate | SARB reg 23; Banks Act s.78 |

The NPA gate binds on `assetBoughtInFlag` + `boughtInDate`. The same **honest scope gap** noted in PROC-FIN-BA-200 §2.1 applies.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6)

For each of the 30 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA220")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:** all BA 220 cells are `direct` — fold from the **`ba220-assets-bought-in-fold`** projection over the acquired-assets sub-ledger (historic cost, market value, NAV, credit facilities, attributable profit/loss; the company-identity + date + percentage-interest reference inputs). Values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
3. **Honours status:** all BA 220 cells are `status: licence-day-data`. The acquired-assets sub-ledger exists as substrate, but the bank **holds no bought-in assets pre-licence-day**; there is nothing to report yet. The product-attribute `dataRequirements` bind now.

> **Currency (P5).** Bought-in-asset amounts are reported in the functional currency, resolved from reference data, never hard-coded.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-220-specific hard gate)

**Before** four-eyes review, the generated BA 220 instance MUST validate against `BA220_v20022026.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 220 upload XML (the summary + Companies + ImmovableAssets sub-form envelopes; `Number (14,2)` currency wrappers; `Date` values).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA220.zip → BA220_v20022026.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is not eligible for review or submission.

In addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract`.

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-220-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Sub-form roll-up** | The `BA220` summary totals (Companies-Total, ImmovableAssets-Total, Total) equal the sum of the per-asset lines in the `BA220_Companies` and `BA220_ImmovableAssets` sub-forms. A summary that does not equal the sum of its sub-form lines is a control failure. |
| **Hashtotal integrity** | Each `Hashtotal*` control cell equals the SARB-defined hash over its cell range; a hashtotal mismatch fails the form-integrity check. |
| **Carrying-value consistency** | Each bought-in asset's market value / NAV reconciles to the IFRS carrying value of the corresponding acquired-asset GL position (no BA 220 figure without a backing ledger position). |
| **Disposal-deadline tracking** | Every asset's `boughtInDate` is tracked against the Banks Act s.78 disposal deadline; an asset held beyond the deadline is flagged (legal/credit escalation), never silently reported. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every populated cell traces to its `ba220-assets-bought-in-fold` source; no value without a source (P1/P2). |
