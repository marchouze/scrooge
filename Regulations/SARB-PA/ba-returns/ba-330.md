## Purpose of BA 330

Form BA 330 is the prudential return through which the bank reports its **interest-rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB)** to the Prudential Authority. IRRBB is a **Pillar 2** risk: D2/2023 §1.3 states that *"The Pillar 2 Interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) framework requires banks to assess, and stress test their overall interest rate risk exposure according to specified methods and take corrective measures should this exposure be deemed excessive."*

The return is built around three linked measurement views, each reported on both a **contractual (static)** basis and a **behavioural** basis:

1. the **repricing gap** schedule (the structural backbone — D2/2023 §2.1.1; lines 1–22 contractual, lines 23–44 behavioural);
2. the **net-interest-income (NII / ΔNII)** earnings-perspective sensitivity (D2/2023 lines 45–66); and
3. the **economic-value-of-equity (EVE / ΔEVE)** sensitivity, including the supervisory **maximum-loss / outlier** measure against Tier 1 capital (D2/2023 lines 67–99).

## The repricing-gap structure

D2/2023 §2.1.1 directs: *"A bank shall construct the relevant required repricing gap by classifying all relevant interest rate-sensitive assets and liabilities in time buckets according to their repricing characteristics or maturity dates."* The repricing gap:

- *"(a) measures the bank's exposure to risk by focusing on changes to future profitability within a given time horizon, eventually affecting future levels of the bank's equity;"*
- *"(b) is derived from a static balance sheet; and"*
- *"(c) assumes that the derived potential rate movements will occur through a parallel rate shock to all maturities along the yield curve."* (D2/2023 §2.1.1(a)–(c).)

**Column instructions (time buckets).** D2/2023 §2.1.2 prescribes time-bucket columns by repricing tenor. Column 1 = **Overnight** (*"items profiled to reprice/reset within the next business day or maturity date if maturing within the next day"*); the intervening columns are the repricing time bands; column 10 = **Non-rate-sensitive items** (*"assets, liabilities or items held in the bank's banking book in relation to which the value is not sensitive to or influenced by changes in interest rates"*).

**Line-item instructions (asset / liability / derivative classification).** The form classifies each banking-book item by its rate behaviour (verbatim line-item descriptions, D2/2023 §2.1.2 tables):

| Line | Line-item | Substance |
|---|---|---|
| 2 / 8 | **Variable-rate items** (asset / liability) | prime-linked, central-bank main reference rate, and other overnight-resetting items, profiled to the overnight (next-business-day) reset/maturity. |
| 3 / 9 | **Fixed-rate items** | fixed-rate items profiled to contractual maturity or next reset date. *(Inflation-linked bonds are classified as fixed-rate — they reprice at the next coupon date and at maturity; D2/2023 §1.7, §2.2.6.1.)* |
| 4 / 10 | **Benchmark-rate items** | reference-rate-linked (e.g. Jibar) items. |
| 5 / 11 | **Discretionary-rate items** | items whose rate may vary at the bank's discretion; on the liability side, non-maturity deposits subject to pass-through and profiling assumptions (contractually repricing/withdrawable overnight → full balance in the overnight bucket). |
| 6 / 12 | **Other assets / liabilities (non-rate-sensitive)** | items not sensitive to rate changes (e.g. deferred-tax asset, suspended-interest portions of stage-3 IFRS-9 exposures, capital and reserve funds). |
| 13 | **Net funding to/(from) trading book** | the bank's internal lending between trading and banking books (offsetting positions reported in the non-rate-sensitive column). |
| 14 | **Net funding to/(from) foreign branches** | legal-entity-basis net position vs foreign branches. |
| 17–19 | **Swaps and FRAs** | pay-fixed/receive-floating, receive-fixed/pay-floating, and basis swaps — reported as notional in the relevant fixed-leg (maturity) and floating-leg (next-reprice) buckets, with prescribed long/short equivalence (e.g. receive-floating/pay-fixed ≡ long floating-rate instrument to next fixing + short fixed-rate instrument to swap maturity). FRAs/futures reported as long/short positions. |
| 20 | **Other derivative instruments** | options/swaptions managing banking-book IRR, reported on a delta-equivalent / settlement-and-maturity basis. |
| 22 | **Cumulative gap (incl. derivatives)** | the running cumulative repricing gap per time band. |
| 23–44 | **Behavioural static repricing gap** | the same line-item logic re-applied with the bank's **behavioural assumptions** (NMD decay, prepayment, etc.). |

## The IRRBB framework — ΔNII and ΔEVE under prescribed rate-shock scenarios

BA 330 carries **both** of the BCBS d368 IRRBB perspectives — the earnings perspective (**ΔNII**) and the economic-value perspective (**ΔEVE**).

### ΔNII (net-interest-income / earnings perspective)

D2/2023 lines 45–66 capture the **contractual** and **behavioural** sensitivity of NII to interest-rate movements:

- **Lines 45–48 — parallel rate shock up/down**, *"calculated as scenario less base"*, reported both **excluding** (lines 45–46) and **including** (lines 47–48) the impact of interest-rate swaps/FRAs.
- **Lines 49–50 — impact of a parallel rate shock on Tier 1 capital and reserve funds**: the rate-change impact on NII expressed as a percentage of the bank's Tier 1 capital and reserve funds (line item 77, column 1, form **BA 700**).
- **Lines 51–52 — impact on forecast NII**: the impact expressed as a percentage of the bank's **forecast NII for the twelve months following the reporting month** (the 12-month forward earnings horizon, consistent with d368).
- **Line 53 — adverse correlated risk shock**: expected NII impact over 12 months of an unchanged prime rate but an adverse ±100 bps (or further currency-specific) move in the call rate / applicable benchmark.
- **Lines 54–55 — bank-specific rate shock (ZAR adverse correlated)**: NII impact from the bank's own internally-formulated adverse-correlated scenario (assumptions/yield-curve shifts submitted to the PA on written request).
- **Lines 56–66 — behavioural NII sensitivity**: the same line-item logic with behavioural assumptions applied.

This corresponds to the BCBS d368 earnings measure: ΔNII over a **forward-looking rolling 12-month horizon** on a **constant balance-sheet** assumption under an instantaneous shock (d368 Principle 8, Definitions (ii)).

### ΔEVE (economic-value-of-equity perspective)

D2/2023 lines 67–93 capture the **contractual** and **behavioural** EVE sensitivity:

- **Lines 67–69 / 77–79 — total rate-sensitive assets, total rate-sensitive liabilities, net gap** (contractual and behavioural).
- **Lines 70 / 80 — Base EVE**: D2/2023 line 70 directs the EVE methodology verbatim — *"The starting point for an EVE sensitivity calculation is the static interest-rate gap report … the gaps in each time bucket are transformed into a net present value (NPV) by discounting using discount factors based on the level of interest rates … The NPVs for each time bucket are then added to create a high-level estimate of the bank's economic value."* The tenor bucket used for each product is the **interest-rate repricing tenor, not its expected-life tenor** (line 70(c)). Base EVE is reported both **including** and **excluding** derivatives.
- **Lines 71–76 / 81–86 — EVE sensitivity per shock scenario**: the impact of each prescribed shock scenario relative to base EVE, per time bucket, contractual (71–76) and behavioural (81–86).
- **Lines 87–93 — discount curves**: mid-point curve rates per **regulation 39(4)** of the Regulations; the **base curve is the Jibar swap curve (ZAR reporting)**; the >25Y term structure uses the 30Y mid-point. For material foreign-currency reporting the PA does not prescribe the base curve — banks report their internal risk-management curves.

### The six prescribed shock scenarios

The ΔEVE measure is computed under the **six prescribed interest-rate shock scenarios** — D2/2023 line 94 cites the bank's *"maximum loss amount derived from the 6 prescribed scenarios prespecified in **regulation 30(5)(a)(iii)(H)(i)** of the Regulations"*. These are the canonical BCBS d368 Annex 2 set, referenced at d368 Principle 4 (*"the six prescribed interest rate shock scenarios set out in Annex 2"*) and listed in the d368 Principle 8 disclosure template (d368 §IV / Annex 2):

1. **Parallel up**
2. **Parallel down**
3. **Steepener** (short rates down, long rates up)
4. **Flattener** (short rates up, long rates down)
5. **Short-rate up**
6. **Short-rate down**

## The supervisory outlier test

D2/2023 lines 94–99 implement the IRRBB **supervisory outlier / materiality test**:

- **Lines 94 / 97 — Maximum loss**: the maximum loss from the 6 prescribed scenarios (per Reg 30(5)(a)(iii)(H)(i)), reported both including and excluding derivatives.
- **Lines 95 / 98 — Tier 1 capital**: the bank's Tier 1 capital and reserve funds (BA 700, line 77, column 1).
- **Lines 96 / 99 — Maximum (loss) % of Tier 1 capital**: maximum loss ÷ Tier 1 capital.

This is the SARB transposition of **BCBS d368 Principle 12**: *"Supervisors must publish their criteria for identifying outlier banks. Banks identified as outliers must be considered as potentially having undue IRRBB."* The d368 outlier/materiality test compares *"the bank's ∆EVE … with 15% of its Tier 1 capital, under a set of prescribed interest rate shock scenarios"* (d368 §I / Principle 12). A bank whose worst-case ΔEVE exceeds **15% of Tier 1 capital** is an outlier and is subject to supervisory review, mitigation actions, and/or additional capital (d368 Principle 12).

## CSRBB (credit-spread risk in the banking book)

CSRBB is a **related** risk that banks must monitor and assess within their interest-rate-risk management framework (it is distinct from, not a sub-type of, IRRBB). BCBS d368 §2 para 10 defines it: *"CSRBB refers to any kind of asset/liability spread risk of credit-risky instruments that is not explained by IRRBB and by the expected credit/jump to default risk."* d368 Principle 1 directs that *"banks should monitor and assess CSRBB."*

In South Africa, CSRBB is being introduced via **SARB PA Prudential Communication 15 of 2024 (CSRBB field testing)** — register row `ORG-PR-34`. PC 15/2024 follows a proposed CSRBB Directive issued for consultation (December 2023); it runs a field-testing exercise (Jan–Mar 2025, extendable), with a proposed CSRBB reporting template (Annexure A), submission by the 25th working day of the following month, via the Umoja platform (PC 15/2024 §3–§4). CSRBB is **not** carried on form BA 330 itself today — it is a separate field-testing return — but it is documented here because it is part of the same IRRBB risk-management framework.

## Material foreign-currency reporting

D2/2023 line 103 requires **material foreign-currency reporting**: where a single foreign-currency exposure **equals or exceeds 5% of the bank's total assets/liabilities**, the bank applies the corresponding line-item instructions to that currency **without converting to ZAR**. This is the Principle-5 (multi-currency) hook on the IRRBB return — material currencies are reported in their own currency, ZAR is the presentation currency for the consolidated view.

## Binding obligations on the bank (summary)

1. **Construct the repricing gap** by classifying all rate-sensitive banking-book assets and liabilities into time buckets by repricing/maturity (D2/2023 §2.1.1).
2. **Report both contractual (static) and behavioural** repricing gap, NII sensitivity and EVE sensitivity (D2/2023 lines 1–93).
3. **Compute ΔNII** over a 12-month forward horizon under parallel and adverse-correlated shocks, expressed against Tier 1 capital and forecast NII (D2/2023 lines 45–66).
4. **Compute ΔEVE** by NPV-discounting each repricing bucket's net cashflow at base vs shocked rates, using the Jibar swap discount curve (Reg 39(4)), under the six prescribed shock scenarios (D2/2023 lines 67–93; Reg 30(5)(a)(iii)(H)(i)).
5. **Apply the supervisory outlier test** — worst-case ΔEVE as a percentage of Tier 1 capital; a bank exceeding 15% of Tier 1 is an outlier subject to review (D2/2023 lines 94–99; d368 Principle 12).
6. **Report material foreign currencies** (≥ 5% of total assets/liabilities) without ZAR conversion (D2/2023 line 103).
7. **Submit form BA 330** to the PA on the prescribed cadence (D5/2025 §2.1.15; cadence counsel-gated), with CEO + auditor acknowledgement (D2/2023 §3).
8. **Monitor and assess CSRBB** within the IRRBB framework and participate in PA CSRBB field testing (d368 Principle 1; PC 15/2024).
