# Role brief — Chief Audit Executive (CAE)

**Author:** PAX
**Date:** 2026-05-06
**For:** Nolan
**Authorising decision:** A2 (CEO approval, 2026-05-06).

## 1. Role title and one-line purpose

**Chief Audit Executive (CAE)** — heads the third line of defence; runs Internal Audit; reports functionally to the Audit Committee and administratively to the CEO; named accountable for independent assurance.

## 2. Why this role exists

Vera is the internal-audit / continuous-assurance *engineer* — she builds the continuous-controls evidence pipelines. The named CAE designation is a governance accountability separate from engineering. The Banks Act, BCBS Internal Audit Function in Banks (BCBS 223 / 268), the Companies Act on Audit Committees, the IIA International Professional Practices Framework (IPPF), and King IV all expect a named CAE with operational independence from management and direct access to the Audit Committee. Helena's recommended order placed CAE first because Vera's third-line independence is currently propped up by a dotted line; that arrangement is regulator-credible only as an interim.

The CEO has confirmed (2026-05-06, A2) that this is the next governance hire.

## 3. Scope of work (priority order)

1. **Internal audit charter and mandate.** Authoring; sign-off by the Audit Committee; communicating organisationally.
2. **Risk-based audit plan.** Annual plan derived from the bank's risk taxonomy and the RAS / RAF; approved by the AC; revised at least quarterly.
3. **Continuous-controls assurance programme.** Vera's evidence pipelines feed the CAE's quarterly opinions to the AC; the CAE oversees Vera's engineering; the engineering reports to the CAE.
4. **Audit-Committee secretariat support (alongside Owen).** AC pack content from a third-line perspective; engagement with the External Auditor; coordination on combined assurance.
5. **External Auditor relationship.** Engagement with the external auditor, joint planning, response to findings.
6. **Investigations.** Independent investigation of escalated whistleblowing or financial-crime matters that should sit outside the second line.
7. **Combined assurance map.** Coordinating with Helena, Zara, Iris, Senna so that first / second / third lines do not duplicate or under-cover.
8. **Quality assurance and improvement programme (QAIP).** Per IIA IPPF — internal QA, external QA at least every five years.
9. **Regulator engagement on assurance.** Named contact for PA / FSCA on internal-audit matters where they engage directly.
10. **Special engagements.** Pre-implementation reviews on material change; ICAAP / ILAAP independent challenge contributions where warranted.

## 4. Direct reports (subject to CEO confirmation)

## 10. Principle alignment

**P1 — Events as source of truth.** Audit findings, recommendations, management responses, and remediation are events. Closure is an event with evidence link. Past audit posture at any as-of date is reproducible.

**P2 — Traceability.** Every audit finding cites the policy / standard / regulation it tests against. The audit charter is itself register-linked.

**P3 — Cloud-native, no manual.** AC packs, third-line opinions, evidence registers, follow-up trackers — generated, not assembled. Vera's pipelines are the operational substrate.

**P4 — Security by design.** Auditor access is purpose-bound, audit-trail itself audited; investigations file integrity attested.

**P5 — Multi-everything.** Audit plan covers per-entity, per-jurisdiction risks. New entities and jurisdictions enter the plan as register changes.

**P6 — Single source of truth.** Third-line opinion is a *summary* of the evidence stack; never authored independently of the data.
