## Architecture notes

### BA 350 — Principle 1 compliance

The BA 350 subscriber uses the P1-compliant events-first path
(`generateBa350MarketRiskFromEvents`): FX positions are folded from
`FxTradeExecuted` primary events, NOT from the trial balance. IR general /
IR specific / equity / commodity sub-charges remain caller-supplied
placeholders (zero by default) — these are substrate gaps surfaced in the
output `placeholders` field, roadmap-tracked under D-MARKETS-CAPITAL-TIME-SHAPE.

### BA 600 — Build-phase posture

The BA 600 subscriber uses caller-supplied gross-income rows (rehearsal-grade;
empty acceptable → zero capital). The correct P1-compliant path would fold
gross income from `RevenueRecognitionEmitted` events; that event stream is a
substrate gap noted in `period-close-subscriber.ts` as `// TODO`.
