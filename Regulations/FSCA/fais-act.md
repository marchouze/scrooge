## Scope and applicability

### Who the Act regulates

The Act regulates persons who render "financial advisory and intermediary services" (together, "financial services") to clients as a regular feature of their business. The key gateway concept is the definition of "financial services provider" in s.1(1): any person, other than a representative, who as a regular feature of their business (a) furnishes advice, (b) furnishes advice and renders any intermediary service, or (c) renders an intermediary service.

### Applicability to Hoz Securities Limited

**Entity in scope: Hoz Securities Limited** (FSP / ODP licence). Hoz Bank Limited does not independently hold an FSP licence; the bank-exemption carve-out under s.1(3)(b)(i) — which exempts a bank or mutual bank acting as conduit between a client and another product supplier — does NOT cover the full scope of OTC derivative advice and intermediary services Hoz Securities renders. Posture A (pursue FSP licence) is confirmed per CEO decision `D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY`.

**Products in scope:** JSE bonds and equities, OTC interest-rate derivatives (IRS, CCS, FRAs), FX spot/forward/options. All are "financial products" within s.1(1) definition paras (a)(i)–(v), (e) (foreign currency denominated investment instruments). OTC derivatives advice and intermediary services require both Category I (advice) and Category II (intermediary services, discretionary management of derivative portfolios) authorisation. Category IIA (discretionary FSP) may also apply; external counsel ratifies at licence-application gate.

**Counterparties:** institutional-only (asset managers, hedge funds, corporate treasuries, other banks). These are "clients" under s.1(1) and are classified as institutional / professional counterparties — the suitability and disclosure obligations still apply but are calibrated to institutional-counterparty assumptions under the General Code of Conduct.

**Licence pursuit:** Categories I and II FSP authorisation for Hoz Securities Limited. External counsel confirms exact categories at licence-application gate.

**FAIS Key Individual:** Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance) is the steady-state KI under s.8 + Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017. Marc is interim KI until Saskia's fit-and-proper file completes.

### Bank-exemption carve-out — why it does NOT apply

> s.1(3)(b)(i): "intermediary service does not include— (i) the rendering by a bank or mutual bank of a service contemplated in paragraph (b)(ii) of the definition of 'intermediary service' where the bank or mutual bank acts merely as a conduit between a client and another product supplier;"

The conduit exemption applies only where the bank "acts merely as a conduit." Hoz Securities provides independent OTC derivative structuring, advice, and execution — not mere conduit services. The Securities entity is separately licensed, and the posture is: pursue FSP licence; no exemption claimed. Register entry: `ORG-FAIS-KI` (Domain P, Posture A confirmed).

## Chapter II — Authorisation of Financial Services Providers (ss.7–12)

### s.7 — Authorisation of financial services providers

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.7(1)):** "With effect from a date determined by the Minister by notice in the *Gazette*, a person may not act or offer to act as a financial services provider unless such person has been issued with a licence under section 8."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.7(2)):** "Subject to section 40, a transaction concluded on or after the date contemplated in subsection (1) between a product supplier and any client by virtue of any financial service rendered to the client by a person not authorised as a financial services provider, or by any other person acting on behalf of such unauthorised person, is not unenforceable between the product supplier and the client merely by reason of such lack of authorisation."

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED:** No person may render financial services without an FSP licence. The prohibition is absolute from the date the Minister has determined (the Act's commencement for financial services was phased).

### s.8 — Application for authorisation

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.8(1)):** "An application for an authorisation referred to in section 7(1), including an application by an applicant not domiciled in the Republic, must be submitted to the registrar in the form and manner determined by the registrar by notice in the *Gazette*, and be accompanied by information to satisfy the registrar that the applicant complies with the requirements for fit and proper financial services providers or categories of providers, determined by the registrar by notice in the *Gazette*, after consultation with the Advisory Committee, in respect of— (a) personal character qualities of honesty and integrity; (b) the competence and operational ability of the applicant to fulfil the responsibilities imposed by this Act; and (c) the applicant's financial soundness; Provided that where the applicant is a partnership, a trust or a corporate or unincorporated body, the applicant must, in addition, so satisfy the registrar that any key individual in respect of the applicant complies with the said requirements in respect of— (i) personal character qualities of honesty and integrity; and (ii) competence and operational ability, to the extent required in order for such key individual to fulfil the responsibilities imposed on the key individual by this Act."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.8(4)(b)(i)–(iii)):** "Conditions and restrictions contemplated in paragraph (a) may include a condition that where after the date of granting of the licence— (i) any key individual in respect of the licensee's business is replaced by a new key individual; or (ii) any new key individual is appointed or assumes office; or (iii) any change occurs in the personal circumstances of a key individual which affects the fit and proper requirements mentioned in subsection (1) and renders or may render such person to be no longer a fit and proper person, no such person may be permitted to take part in the conduct or management or oversight of the licensee's business in relation to the rendering of financial services, unless such person has on application been approved by the registrar in the manner and in accordance with a procedure determined, after consultation with the Advisory Committee, by the registrar by notice in the *Gazette*."

## Chapter III — Representatives of Authorised FSPs (ss.13–14)

### s.13 — Qualifications of representatives and duties of authorised FSPs

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.13(1)):** "A person may not— (a) carry on business by rendering financial services to clients for or on behalf of any person who— (i) is not authorised as a financial services provider; and (ii) is not exempted from the application of this Act relating to the rendering of a financial service; or (b) act as a representative of an authorised financial services provider, unless such person— (i) is able to provide confirmation, certified by the provider, to clients— (aa) that a service contract or other mandatory agreement, to represent the provider, exists; and (bb) that the provider accepts responsibility for those activities of the representative performed within the scope of, or in the course of implementing, any such contract or agreement: and (ii) if debarred as contemplated in section 14, complies with the requirements determined by the registrar, after consultation with the Advisory Committee, by notice in the *Gazette*, for the reappointment of a debarred person as a representative."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.13(2)):** "An authorised financial services provider must— (a) at all times be satisfied that the provider's representatives, and key individuals of such representatives, are, when rendering a financial service on behalf of the provider, competent to act, taking into consideration requirements similar to those contemplated in paragraphs (a) and (b) of section 8(1) and subsection (1)(b)(ii) of this section, where applicable; and (b) take such steps as may be reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that representatives comply with any applicable code of conduct as well as with other applicable laws on conduct of business."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.13(3)):** "The authorised financial services provider must maintain a register of representatives, and key individuals of such representatives, which must be regularly updated and be available to the registrar for reference or inspection purposes."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.13(4)(a)–(b)):** "Such register must— (a) contain every representative's or key individual's name and business address, and state whether the representative acts for the provider as employee or as mandatory; and (b) specify the categories in which such representatives are competent to render financial services."

**OBLIGATIONS TRIGGERED:**

1. Representatives must be fit-and-proper and competent for the categories in which they render financial services.
2. FSP must maintain a register of representatives and their KIs — updated regularly and available to FSCA.
3. FSP must ensure representatives comply with applicable codes of conduct.

**APPLICATION TO HOZ:** Hoz Securities must maintain a FAIS representatives register covering all persons rendering financial services on its behalf (Saskia and any other representatives). The register must record: name, business address, employee/mandatory status, product categories authorised. ### s.16 — Principles of code of conduct

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.16(1)):** "A code of conduct must be drafted in such a manner as to ensure that the clients being rendered financial services will be able to make informed decisions, that their reasonable financial needs regarding financial products will be appropriately and suitably satisfied and that for those purposes authorised financial services providers, and their representatives, are obliged by the provisions of such code to— (a) act honestly and fairly, and with due skill, care and diligence, in the interests of clients and the integrity of the financial services industry; (b) have and employ effectively the resources, procedures and appropriate technological systems for the proper performance of professional activities; (c) seek from clients appropriate and available information regarding their financial situations, financial product experience and objectives in connection with the financial service required; (d) act with circumspection and treat clients fairly in a situation of conflicting interests; and (e) comply with all applicable statutory or common law requirements applicable to the conduct of business."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.16(2)):** "A code of conduct must in particular contain provisions relating to— (a) the making of adequate disclosures of relevant material information, including disclosures of actual or potential own interests, in relation to dealings with clients; (b) adequate and appropriate record-keeping; (c) avoidance of fraudulent and misleading advertising, canvassing and marketing; (d) proper safe-keeping, separation and protection of funds and transaction documentation of clients; (e) where appropriate, suitable guarantees or professional indemnity or fidelity insurance cover, and mechanisms for adjustments of such guarantees or cover by the registrar in any particular case; and (f) any other matter which is necessary or expedient to be regulated in such code for the better achievement of the objects of this Act."

**OBLIGATIONS TRIGGERED:** These principles define the minimum content that the General Code of Conduct must contain. The principles in s.16(1)(a)–(e) are the statutory conduct obligations that underpin the General Code's specific provisions.

**APPLICATION TO HOZ:** The s.16 principles are the statutory basis for the General Code obligations analysed in the General Code section below. Hoz Securities' FAIS Policy, TCF Policy, and conduct-of-business procedures must collectively discharge all s.16(1) and s.16(2) principles.

### s.19 — Accounting and audit requirements

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.19(1)(a)–(b)):** "Except to the extent exempted by the registrar, an authorised financial services provider must, in respect of the business carried on by the provider as authorised under the provider's licence— (a) maintain full and proper accounting records on a continual basis, brought up to date monthly; and (b) annually prepare, in respect of the relevant financial year of the provider, financial statements reflecting, with suitable particulars, the financial position of the business as at the last day of the financial year in question, and the results of the operations and cash flow information for the period then ended."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.19(2)(a)):** "An authorised financial services provider must cause the statements referred to in subsection (1)(b) to be audited and reported on by an external auditor approved by the registrar, in order to produce— (i) an audited balance sheet, including such notes thereon or documents attached thereto as may be necessary; (ii) an audited income statement, including such notes thereon or documents attached thereto as may be necessary; and (iii) an audited statement of the source and application of funds."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.19(4)):** "Despite anything to the contrary contained in any law, the auditor of an authorised financial services provider must report to and inform the registrar in writing of any irregularity or suspected irregularity in the conduct or the affairs of the authorised financial services provider concerned of which the auditor became aware in performing functions as auditor and which, in the opinion of the auditor, is material."

## Chapter VI, Part I — Ombud for Financial Services Providers (ss.20–32)

### Overview

Chapter VI, Part I (ss.20–32) establishes the Office of the Ombud for Financial Services Providers (FAIS Ombud). The Ombud handles client complaints against FSPs and representatives.

### s.27 — Receipt of complaints, prescription, jurisdiction and investigation

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.27(3)(a)(i)–(ii)):** "The following jurisdictional provisions apply to the Ombud in respect of the investigation of complaints: (a) (i) The Ombud must decline to investigate any complaint which relates to an act or omission which occurred on or after the date of commencement of this Act but on a date more than three years before the date of receipt of such complaint by the Office. (ii) Where the complainant was unaware of the occurrence of the act or omission contemplated in subparagraph (i), the period of three years commences on the date on which the complainant became aware or ought reasonably to have become aware of such occurrence, whichever occurs first."

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED / APPLICATION TO HOZ:** The 3-year prescription period means Hoz Securities' complaint-handling procedures must capture all complaints and the date of the underlying act. `ORG-FAIS-RK-COMPLAINT-HANDLING` substrate must record the date of the underlying act (not just the complaint receipt date) to enable accurate Ombud jurisdiction assessment. Reconciles with s.18 records.

### s.31 — Penalties (Ombud-related)

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.31):** "Any person who— (a) commits any act in respect of the Ombud or an investigation by the Ombud which, if committed in respect of a court of law, would have constituted contempt of court, is guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to any penalty which may be imposed on a conviction of contempt of court; or (b) (i) anticipates a determination of the Ombud in any manner calculated to influence the determination; or (ii) wilfully interrupts any proceedings conducted by the Ombud, is guilty of an offence and liable on conviction to a fine or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year."

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED:** No interference with Ombud proceedings; full cooperation.

**APPLICATION TO HOZ:** Hoz Securities must cooperate fully with Ombud investigations. Internal escalation procedures (Zara + Owen) must include a Ombud-response protocol: acknowledge complaint within 5 business days; provide substantive response within Ombud's specified timeframe; do not attempt to influence Ombud determinations.

## Chapter VI, Part II — Other Enforcement Measures (ss.33–39)

### s.33 — Civil remedies

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.33(1)):** "The registrar may, when satisfied on the basis of available facts and information that a person has contravened or not complied with any provision of this Act, or is likely so to contravene or not to comply, apply to a Court for an order restraining such person from continuing to commit any such act or omission or from committing it in future, and requiring the person to take such remedial steps as the Court deems necessary to rectify the consequences of the act or omission, including consequences which prejudiced or may prejudice any client."

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED / APPLICATION TO HOZ:** Civil remedies can be sought by FSCA against Hoz Securities for non-compliance. The remedial-step obligation means post-breach remediation is mandatory. Vera's continuous-controls assertions and Zara's compliance monitoring are the operational controls that minimise this risk.

### s.34 — Undesirable practices

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.34(1)):** "Subject to subsections (2) and (3), the registrar may, after consultation with the Advisory Committee, by notice in the *Gazette* declare a particular business practice to be undesirable for all or a category of authorised financial services providers, or any such provider."

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.34(2)(a)(i)–(iv)):** "The following principles must guide the registrar in considering whether or not a declaration contemplated in subsection (1) should be made: (a) That the practice concerned, directly or indirectly, has or is likely to have the effect of— (i) harming the relations between authorised financial services providers or any category of such providers, or any such provider, and clients or the general public; (ii) unreasonably prejudicing any client; (iii) deceiving any client; or (iv) unfairly affecting any client"

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED / APPLICATION TO HOZ:** FSCA may declare specific business practices undesirable. ### s.36 — Offences and penalties

> **STATUTORY TEXT (s.36):** "Any person who— (a) contravenes or fails to comply with a provision of section 7(1), 8(8), 13(1), 14(1), 18, 19(2) or 34(4) or (6); or (b) in any application in terms of this Act, deliberately makes a misleading, false or deceptive statement, or conceals any material fact, is guilty of an offence and is on conviction liable to a fine not exceeding R1 000 000 or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding 10 years, or to both such fine and such imprisonment."

**OBLIGATION TRIGGERED:** Criminal offence exposure for: (a) operating without licence (s.7(1)); (b) failure to display/maintain licence (s.8(8)); (c) unauthorised representatives operating (s.13(1)); (d) failure to debar (s.14(1)); (e) failure to maintain records (s.18); (f) failure to have audited financial statements (s.19(2)); (g) carrying on undesirable practice after declaration (s.34(4)/(6)); and (h) false or misleading statements in any FSCA application.

**APPLICATION TO HOZ:** Maximum exposure: R1 000 000 fine and/or 10 years imprisonment per offence. The eight triggering provisions (s.7(1), s.8(8), s.13(1), s.14(1), s.18, s.19(2), s.34(4)/(6)) are the core compliance obligations whose breach constitutes a criminal offence. ### Structure

The Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements (BN 194 of 2017, as amended) sets out the detailed fit-and-proper criteria that the FSCA applies when assessing FSP licence applications and ongoing compliance. It operates within the framework of s.8(1)(a)–(c) of the Act (personal character/honesty/integrity; competence and operational ability; financial soundness).

### Honesty, integrity, and good standing

> `[TEXT NOT RETRIEVED — manual verification required]`

**Substance:** No person with a criminal conviction for dishonesty, fraud, financial crime, or similar offence may be a KI or representative. Any pending or concluded regulatory sanction, debarment, or adverse finding in any jurisdiction must be disclosed. The honesty and integrity requirement is assessed on an ongoing basis — a KI who receives a criminal charge must notify the FSP immediately.

**Application to Hoz:** Saskia (KI, steady-state) and Marc (KI, interim) must make full disclosure of any prior convictions, regulatory sanctions, or adverse findings in any jurisdiction at application. ### Qualifications — Category I and II

> `[TEXT NOT RETRIEVED — manual verification required for exact qualification schedule]`

**Substance (BN 194 of 2017 structure):** The Determination establishes product categories and sub-categories; for each sub-category, it specifies minimum qualification levels (NQF level) for KIs and representatives. Categories I and II for OTC derivatives and equities/bonds require:

- **Category I (advice only):** Minimum NQF Level 5 qualification (recognised by SAQA) in the relevant financial product sub-category, or equivalent recognised qualification. For equity and derivative products: relevant undergraduate degree or professional qualification in finance, economics, or related field; or industry qualification such as CFA, FRM, or equivalent.
- **Category II (discretionary management / intermediary services):** Higher experience and/or qualification threshold than Category I for the same product sub-categories. Typically requires at least NQF Level 6 or industry equivalent for derivative product categories.
- **Category IIA (discretionary FSP — if applicable):** Similar to Category II with additional operational-ability requirements for discretionary portfolio management.

**Application to Hoz:** Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance) must demonstrate: (a) qualification at or above the minimum NQF level for Category I and II across the product sub-categories in Hoz Securities' intended product schedule (equities, bonds, OTC IRS/CCS/FRA, FX spot/forward/options); (b) relevant industry qualifications (CFA, FRM, or equivalent are typically accepted). ### KI vs representative distinction

| Criterion | KI requirement | Representative requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Responsible for managing and overseeing FSP rendering of financial services (s.1(1) "key individual" definition) | Renders financial services on behalf of the FSP (s.1(1) "representative" definition) |
| Fit-and-proper assessment | Full assessment at licence application; ongoing ongoing; FSCA approval required for any KI change (s.8(4)(b)) | Initial competence check by FSP (s.13(2)(a)); FSP responsible for maintaining representative fit-and-proper |
| Qualifications | Per BN 194 of 2017 schedule by Category — typically higher threshold | Per BN 194 of 2017 schedule by Category — same schedule, lower minimum in some sub-categories |
| Experience | Minimum years per BN 194 of 2017; KI typically higher threshold | Minimum years per BN 194 of 2017; representative threshold equal to or below KI |
| CPD | Mandatory per BN 194 of 2017; reported to FSCA | Mandatory per BN 194 of 2017; managed by FSP |
| Honesty/integrity | Full FSCA assessment | FSP verification; disclosed to FSCA if debarred (s.14) |
| Register | FSCA's central register of KIs | FSP's s.13(3) register; submitted to FSCA on request |

**Application to Hoz:** The Hoz Securities licence structure has: (1) Saskia as the sole KI (steady-state); (2) Marc as interim KI until Saskia's file completes; (3) any trading staff rendering financial services on behalf of Hoz Securities are representatives and must be on the s.13(3) register. At the build-phase, no representatives are engaged (no live client interactions); the register activates at licence-day with the first licensed representative.
