## 1. Mandate boundary

**In scope (governance seat — single hat):**

- **Independent NED** — fiduciary duties under Companies Act s.76; King IV independent definition [citation: Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.66; King IV Principle 7].
- **Audit Committee member** — Companies Act s.94 requires the AC to comprise at least three NEDs; this seat is one of the two AC members other than the AC Chair (seat #1) [citation: Companies Act s.94; Companies Reg. 43; King IV Principle 8].
- **Banks Act fit-and-proper director** [citation: Banks Act 94 of 1990 s.60; PA Fit-and-Proper Standards `ORG-HR-11`].
- **Where the seat brings the financial-expertise hat** — at least one AC member should be a designated CA(SA) or hold equivalent financial-reporting credentials sufficient to discharge AC duties under Companies Reg. 43.

**Out of scope:**

- Chair / AC Chair functions (seat #1 holds).
- Executive accountability (Marc, CRO, Compliance Lead hold).
- Day-to-day risk function — human CRO holds.
- AML/CFT primary or alternate accountability — Compliance Lead is principal, AC Chair (seat #1) is alternate.

## 6. Conflict-of-interest map

Same structure as Chair §6, with AC-specific additions:

- **Audit-firm overlap (binding):** if the candidate's most-recent (≤5 years) employer is a Big-4 audit firm, that firm is disqualified as the bank's external auditor under independence rules. The conflict is solvable by audit-firm choice — but the conflicts register flagged at appointment, and Camille's audit-firm RFP must avoid the candidate's prior firm.
- **Cross-directorship at a direct competitor's AC** — the AC sees pricing-sensitive financial detail; cross-directorship is disqualifying.
- **Prior-employer restraint clauses** — typically light for retired-partner candidates; heavier for currently-active candidates.
- **JSE-listed-company-NED concurrent role conflicts** — if the candidate sits on a listed-co AC and the listed-co becomes a counterparty of the bank, the cross-AC information-flow is a concern; conflicts register.
