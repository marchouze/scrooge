## How this pack is structured

Six sub-decisions, one per seat. Each sub-decision shows:
- **Recommendation** — the role-brief's bottom line in one line.
- **Sequencing implication** — what depends on this resolving (within the six and to outside agents).
- **CEO-direction items** — only the questions the role-brief explicitly asks back. Generic "approve as drafted" answers are the default; substantive direction is expected only on the items listed.

Read top-to-bottom; resolve in one pass. Four cross-cutting questions appear once at the bottom — they apply to multiple seats and resolving them once is enough.

> **Default if no decision.** Each role-brief carries its own default-if-no-decision (the recommendation as drafted). No-action on this pack means no-action on Nolan's active search; the seats remain spec'd-but-not-sourced.

## Cross-cutting CEO-direction items (resolve once; applies to all six)

These four questions show up in multiple sub-decisions. Resolving them once unblocks all six searches.

### Q1 — Compensation ceilings

PAX gives indicative comp bands per role anchored to comparator-class shape; Camille (CFO, governance) sets actual numbers. CEO direction needed: any **hard ceilings** beyond the comparator band (e.g. "no package above R5m all-in for any seat", "Chair caps at R2.5× base-NED, no exceptions", or "comp is whatever it takes for the right candidate"). Default-if-no-decision: comparator-class bands, Camille framing pass.

### Q2 — Geography flexibility

Default for SA-licensed bank seats is SA-resident or willing-to-relocate. CEO direction needed: any seats where **international candidates with SA experience** are acceptable (most likely the Chair; possibly NED #2 on technology/cyber depth). Default-if-no-decision: SA-resident-only across all six.

### Q3 — Willingness to consider non-traditional backgrounds

PAX surfaces this on three seats: Human CRO (non-bank-CRO with strong second-line track record), CoSec (rising talent vs seasoned), Compliance Lead (cross-industry MLROs). CEO direction needed: a single answer like "stretch on CRO and CoSec; conventional only for Compliance Lead and NEDs" or "conventional across all six". Default-if-no-decision: conventional across all six (lower regulator-pushback risk; longer search times).

### Q4 — Search-firm exclusivity

Most role-briefs recommend two firms in parallel for the Board seats and one specialist firm for the executive seats (CRO, Compliance Lead, CoSec). CEO direction needed: confirm the two-firms-in-parallel posture for Board seats (some firms decline to compete on the same search) or specify single-firm exclusivity with a longer timeline. Default-if-no-decision: two-firms-parallel as recommended per role-brief.
