# Governance Framework — Board Risk Committee v1

> **Status:** IN FORCE (policy layer). Banks Act Regulation 39 requires a dedicated Board Risk Committee for registered banks. This framework is IN FORCE from adoption; the statutory obligation activates at registration.
>
> **Author:** Owen (Company Secretary, governance); co-owned by Helena (CRO, governance).
>

## 1. Mandate

The BRC assists the Board in overseeing:

1. The bank's risk appetite and risk strategy (RAS approval).
2. The integrity of the bank's risk management framework (RMCP).
3. Capital adequacy (ICAAP/RAS Tier-1 thresholds).
4. Liquidity risk and funding strategy (ILAAP).
5. Model risk governance.
6. Risk culture and incentive compatibility.

The BRC approves the RAS annually and recommends to the Board. Material RAS breaches escalate from the CRO to the BRC within one business day.

---

## 2. Composition

| Role | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Chair | Non-executive Director (independent preferred) |
| Members | Minimum 3 (majority non-executive; CRO attends in advisory capacity) |
| Secretary | Owen (Company Secretary, governance) |

**Build-phase interim:** Interim Risk Forum — chaired by Helena (CRO); attended by relevant engineering and governance leads. Full BRC convened post-NED appointment.

---

## 3. Meeting cadence

Minimum: quarterly. Extraordinary meetings convened on material RAS breach, material credit event, or regulatory request. Quorum: majority of members.

---

## 4. Reporting line

BRC reports to the Board of Directors. CRO presents risk dashboard, RAS utilisation, open findings, and model risk status at each meeting.
