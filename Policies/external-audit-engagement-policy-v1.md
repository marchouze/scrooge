## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for the appointment, independence assessment, scope definition, rotation, and oversight of the bank's external auditor.

---

## 2. Appointment

1. The external auditor is appointed by shareholders at the AGM (Companies Act s.92).
2. The Audit Committee recommends the appointment to shareholders after conducting independence and competency assessment.
3. The auditor must be an IRBA-registered firm with banking-sector audit experience.
4. PA notification: external auditor details filed with the Registrar per Regulation 39(13).

---

## 3. Independence requirements

1. No prohibited relationships: the firm and engagement partner must satisfy IRBA Code Part 4C independence requirements.
2. Non-audit services: any non-audit service by the external auditor requires Audit Committee pre-approval.
3. Rotation: audit firm rotation after 5 years; engagement partner rotation after 3 years.

---

## 4. Engagement scope

The external audit engagement covers, at minimum:
- Annual financial statements under IFRS.
- PA regulatory returns where required by Regulation 39(13).
- Internal control assessment (management letter).

---

## 5. Communication protocol

The external auditor communicates directly with the Audit Committee Chair. The CAE (Thandiwe) facilitates information access. Management responses to audit findings are tabled at the Audit Committee.
