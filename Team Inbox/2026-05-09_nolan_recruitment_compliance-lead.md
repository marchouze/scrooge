# Recruitment scope — Triple-hatted Compliance Lead (MLRO + FIC CO + POPIA IO)

## 1. Why this seat exists

FIC Act s.43A requires designation of an MLRO. FIC Act s.43B requires appointment of a Compliance Officer for FIC obligations. POPIA s.56 requires designation of an Information Officer (POPIA s.56(b) defaults the IO to the CEO if no separate designation; SARB / Information-Regulator precedent expects designation away from the CEO for a regulated financial institution).

Each merger is statutorily contemplated:

- **MLRO + FIC CO:** established FIC practice; reflected in `_obligations-register.md` ORG-FC-11.
- **MLRO/FIC CO + POPIA IO:** established SA practice — the cross-cutting conduct-and-privacy compliance lead.

Mira+Zara's confirmation paper Q1 confirms the triple-hat is "a workload concentration, not a statutory defect". Q3 confirms the CEO-as-MLRO bar is **published-guidance / supervisory precedent**, not statute — but in practice binding (no second-line check on STR escalation). The seat is therefore **mandatory separate from the CEO**.

The triple-hat carries an embedded succession arrangement (Owen+Imani §4.2; Mira+Zara §4.2):

- **Deputy-IO:** the CoSec (priority-2 sister scope).
- **MLRO-alternate:** the AC Chair NED (Independent Chair, priority-2 sister scope) per Mira+Zara §4.2 preferred placement.

## 3. Mandate boundary

**In scope:**

- **MLRO** under FIC s.43A — receives internal STR escalations; files STRs / SARs / CTRs / PARs to the FIC.
- **FIC Compliance Officer** under FIC s.43B — accountable for FIC obligations end-to-end; RMCP custodian.
- **POPIA Information Officer** under POPIA s.56 — designation lodgment with the Information Regulator; manages PAIA requests; breach-notification handling under POPIA s.22; oversees lawful-processing register.
- Sanctions-list operational responsibility at licence-day (inherits from Zara-the-engineering-CCO; Zara remains second-line engineering).
- TCF substrate ownership at commencement-of-trading (per Mira+Zara Q6).
- Day-to-day relationship with FIC, the Information Regulator, and (if FSP-licensed) FSCA conduct supervision.

**Out of scope:**

- Building the compliance / RegTech engineering substrate — Mira owns under Zara (engineering CCO).
- Building the screening engine — Mira's substrate.
- Internal audit — Thandiwe (CAE) owns.

- Day-to-day management.
- FAIS Key Individual seat — separate seat (Saskia steady-state; Marc-interim).

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- 8+ years senior compliance experience at a SA-regulated financial institution.
- Demonstrated MLRO or FIC Compliance Officer experience.
- POPIA Information Officer fluency.
- FIC fit-and-proper credible.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible.
- Substantive independence from the CEO.
- South African resident.

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior MLRO / FIC CO + IO triple-hat experience at a SARB-regulated bank or comparable institution.
- FSCA conduct supervision experience (TCF, FSP licensing).
- Sanctions-screening operational experience.
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- Joint Standard 1 of 2024 fluency.
- Trading-bank exposure (institutional global-markets posture; STR / sanctions risk on counterparties).
- Technology fluency — autonomous-by-default operating model; the seat oversees Mira's screening engine + Iris's POPIA substrate.

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Concurrent compliance-officer seat at a competitor SA bank.
- Prior FIC fit-and-proper findings or supervisory action against the candidate.
- Prior POPIA breach-notification failure under candidate's accountability.
- Cannot demonstrate substantive independence from the CEO.
- Family / close personal relationship with Marc (CEO).
