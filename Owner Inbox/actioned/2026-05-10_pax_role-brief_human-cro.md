## 1. Role purpose + regulatory anchor

The human CRO is the **named accountable person** for the bank's risk-management function under Banks Act 94 of 1990 and the SARB Regulations Relating to Banks, read with Joint Standard 1 of 2024 (PA / FSCA Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience) and the BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015). The seat is the human face of the bank's prudential second-line; Helena (engineering CRO) and Rohan (risk engineer) carry the engineering load — the human CRO governs and signs.

The seat is sequenced **first** of the six thin-human-layer hires because the Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §6–§7 operational-independence read across BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015 §3 makes the interim posture (CEO carrying named CRO accountability) a **finding** at licence-day rather than a precedent challenge that can be answered by a post-licence hiring commitment. Mira (Compliance / RegTech engineer) and Zara (Chief Compliance Officer) read this as a structural defect that must be resolved at lodgment, not after; Owen (Company Secretary) and Imani (Legal-as-code engineer) updated the composition substrate accordingly in the final paper.

| Source | Section / clause | Bearing on the seat |
|---|---|---|
| Banks Act 94 of 1990 | s.60 (Board composition; "sufficient size and composition" to discharge fiduciary duties); s.70(2A)(b) (risk-management process and audit framework) | The Board's risk-oversight discharge and the s.70 risk-management framework both require a named CRO. |
| SARB Regulations Relating to Banks | Reg. 39 (internal-models for capital — IRB / IMM / IMA approvals require organisational independence of the CRO function from model development) | Drives the second-line / first-line architectural separation between this seat and Rohan's model-building. |

## 2. Mandate scope

The seat's mandate is the governance counterpart to Helena's engineering-CRO mandate (`Team/Helena.md` §3). The human CRO holds named regulatory accountability; Helena and Rohan operate the engineering substrate that produces the artefacts the human CRO signs.

### 2.1 Decisions in scope

- Approve and sign **ICAAP / ILAAP** for SARB Prudential Authority engagement (pre-application gate; thereafter on the annual cycle).
- Approve **Risk Appetite Statement & Framework** (CEO-approved today; CRO-approved at licence-day; Helena's engineering output flows in).
- Approve appetite-line operationalisation within the Board-approved framework; dispose of appetite breaches (tolerate / remediate / escalate).
- Approve **model-risk decisions** within the independent-validation envelope (Helena builds; Nadia validates; the human CRO signs the model-risk policy and the in-production-use approvals).
- Chair the **Board Risk Committee** (or its proxy, the Interim Risk Forum, until the Board BRC is constituted).
- Approve **stress-test scenarios** (quarterly tactical + annual strategic).
- Approve **supervisory-letter responses** on prudential matters.
- Veto over second-line risk decisions: RAS limit-breach disposition, production-use of unvalidated models, appetite-line breach dispositions.

### 2.2 Decisions that escalate to CEO / Board

- **Risk Appetite Statement amendment** (framework-level change) — Board approval (interim: CEO with peer-challenge simulation per the dual-hat rule).
- **Tier-1 appetite breach** with prudential implications (e.g. CET1, LCR, NSFR) — Board + PA notification (via Owen and Camille).
- **Supervisory enforcement matter** — CEO + Owen + Imani + (if material) Board.
- **Material model-risk failure** in production producing values used in regulatory submissions — CEO + Camille + Thandiwe (CAE).
- **Stress-test scenario adequacy challenge** — Board + PA dialogue.
- **Climate-risk governance event** outside the Board-approved climate appetite — Board + PA dialogue.
- **Independence-affecting event** — Thandiwe (CAE) pre-decision.

### 2.3 Key outputs

- **Signed ICAAP / ILAAP** to SARB PA — annual cycle.
- **BRC-paper approvals** — quarterly (or per BRC cadence).
- **Supervisory-letter responses** — per PA-stated deadlines (typically 10–20 working days).
- **Risk Appetite Statement & Framework** — at licence-day; thereafter annual review.
- **Model-risk-policy sign-off** — annually, plus event-driven on material model-risk decisions.
- **Standing attendance / contributions** at ALCO, Audit Committee, Board.

### 2.4 Cadence

Hybrid — continuous (event-triggered) on appetite-breach and model-risk events; scheduled for BRC, ICAAP / ILAAP, stress-testing, supervisory engagements. Pre-licence-day: the seat operates on a Scrooge-coordinated tick against this spec while Helena's engineering substrate produces the inputs (per Principle 7 — autonomous-by-default reframe in `Team/Helena.md` §16). Post-licence-day: continuous on the agent-runtime substrate; the human CRO is the named overseer.

### 2.5 Direct reports

The seat oversees **Helena (engineering CRO — dotted line in current substrate; solid line at licence-day)** and **Rohan (risk engineer)** through Helena's reporting chain. **Nadia (independent-validation engineer)** sits in the seat's reporting tree with functional independence from Rohan, per `Team/Helena.md` §3 and the Nolan recruitment scope §5.

## 5. Independence test

The Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §7 operational-independence test is the binding architectural constraint for this seat.

### 5.1 Reporting line

- **Administrative line:** CEO (Marc, today; future human CEO if rotation occurs).
- **Functional line:** Board Risk Committee (interim: Interim Risk Forum until the Board BRC is constituted).
- **Performance assessment:** governable by Board committee, **not** CEO. The engagement letter (Imani-drafted) carves performance-assessment authority out of the CEO line — this is the operational-independence test made operational.
- **Termination:** Board (or BRC, on Board delegation), not CEO — to satisfy the substantive-independence read of BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015 §3 and Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §7.

### 5.2 Committee membership

- **Chair** of Board Risk Committee (or proxy — Interim Risk Forum). Per BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015 §6, the CRO has direct access to the BRC; the bank goes one step further and has the CRO chair, given the small-bank scale.
- **Standing attendee** at Audit Committee — not a member (third-line independence per `Team/Helena.md` §15), but in-room for risk-relevant agenda items.
- **Standing attendee** at ALCO (chaired by the Treasurer governance seat — Eitan's substrate).
- **Co-engages** with Zara (CCO) on Joint-Standard interlocks (cyber-resilience programme oversight under JS 1/2024).

### 5.3 Peer-comparator banks' CRO arrangements

SA Tier-2 / boutique-trading-bank CRO arrangements PAX has surveyed (without naming individual institutions to avoid citation overreach) consistently feature: separate human CRO from CEO at lodgment; performance-assessment carved to BRC chair; termination authority Board-resident; standing BRC attendance plus ALCO and AC standing-attendee status; functional line into BRC, administrative into CEO. The proposed posture matches this pattern.

### 5.4 Substantive-independence safeguards

- **Independent challenge** of Helena's framework outputs from the third line (Vera / Thandiwe) — non-negotiable per `Team/Helena.md` §15.
- **Veto** in §2.1 (RAS breach disposition, unvalidated-model production-use, appetite-line breach disposition) is not subject to CEO override — escalation goes Board, not back to CEO.
- **Direct-access** route to PA Prudential Supervisor — Helena's `SupervisoryLetterReceived` event channel and the human CRO's signing authority are both protected.

## 6. Compensation framing

> Specific numbers deferred to Camille (CFO, governance) per the Nolan recruitment scope §6 hand-off; this section frames the comparator class and package shape so Camille produces the band against a defined reference set.

### 6.1 Comparator class

- **SA Tier-2 / boutique trading-bank CROs.** The bank's R300m-target capital posture and institutional global-markets focus places it in the small-bank / boutique-bank bracket. The comparator class is CRO-seats at SA banks of comparable size (small Tier-2 banks, mutual banks at the upper end, boutique investment-bank subsidiaries). PAX's read is that the band sits **below big-four-SA-bank CRO compensation** but **above generalist-corporate-CRO compensation** — the seat is named-regulatory-accountable, which lifts it above non-bank risk-officer roles.
- **Adjacent comparators (negotiation context, not target):** SA insurance-group CROs of comparable size; building-society CROs; foreign-bank-branch CROs operating in SA. These are useful negotiation context but the band-anchor is bank-CRO comparators.

### 6.2 Package shape

- **Cash component:** base salary at the comparator-class median, variable cash component limited and risk-adjusted per BCBS Principles for Sound Compensation Practices (variable cannot dominate; clawback applicable on subsequent risk-decision quality assessment).
- **Long-term incentive (LTI):** at the bank's small scale and pre-licence stage, LTI is shaped as a multi-year deferred-cash arrangement with risk-adjusted vesting (no equity available pre-licence; equity-equivalent post-licence subject to BRC + Camille design). Vesting is subject to no fit-and-proper findings during the vesting window.
- **Retention:** a sign-on retention (one-third deferred to a one-year anniversary; one-third to two; one-third to three) makes economic sense at this stage given the bank's pre-revenue posture. Camille sets the absolute number.
- **Notice and gardening leave:** 6-month notice on either side, with a gardening-leave clause Imani drafts to protect non-compete on the institutional global-markets vertical.

### 6.3 Risk-adjusted deferral

Per BCBS principles on remuneration (read into `Team/Helena.md` §4 areas of expertise), variable cash and LTI both vest subject to (a) no material risk-event traceable to the CRO's decision-making during the vesting window, (b) no PA fit-and-proper finding against the candidate, (c) no internal-audit (Vera / Thandiwe) finding of governance failure traceable to the seat. Camille sets the deferral percentages.

### 6.4 What the CEO sets

- The absolute compensation band (Camille's input).
- Whether there is geographic flexibility (SA-only vs SA + region — see §9).
- Whether the package can be inflated to reach a candidate not currently sitting (e.g. retired big-four-SA-bank CRO) vs strictly inside-comparator (currently sitting boutique-bank CRO or Deputy-CRO step-up).

## 8. Timeline to licence-application lodgment

The Priority-1 sequencing means this seat lodges **first** of the six thin-human-layer hires authorised under D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM. The downstream gating is concrete:

- **External-counsel engagement gate** (per `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_imani_external-counsel-licence-application.md` §5.4 cross-reference in Nolan recruitment scope §8) — counsel reviews the fit-and-proper file at the licence-application gate; the human CRO must be **named in the licence-application file** by that gate.
- **SARB pre-application engagement** — opens once the bank approaches readiness; the named human CRO is the bank's interlocutor on prudential matters from the moment SARB pre-application opens. Mira + Zara's confirmation paper §3.1 is explicit: the seat must be filled "before lodgment" or a documented Joint-Standard-1-of-2024 derogation must be lodged in pre-application engagement. The CEO chose the former (D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM final disposition).

### 8.1 Concrete implications

- **Sitting CROs run typical 3–6 month notice periods.** This is the binding lead-time. From CEO approval to seat-filled is realistically 4–7 months on a sitting-CRO hire (search + interviews + offer + notice).
- **Step-up Deputy-CROs** can accelerate the timeline to 3–5 months but the fit-and-proper file is more fragile; Mira + Zara's confirmation paper Q5 implies the SARB read on a step-up under JS 1/2024 is more demanding than on a sitting-CRO hire, so step-up candidates need a stronger ICAAP / ILAAP signing track record than the §3 baseline.
- **Ex-PA / ex-SARB seniors** can sometimes be appointed faster (no notice constraint at a regulator) but the PA fit-and-proper read on a recent ex-PA staff member is itself constrained — there is typically a cooling-off expectation (PAX flagged as a `[citation: TBC]` — exact PA cooling-off practice should be counsel-confirmed at fit-and-proper file assembly).

### 8.2 Interim posture

Per the Nolan recruitment scope §8 interim posture: Marc (CEO) retains formal CRO accountability while Helena (engineering CRO) operates the function. This is documented in `CLAUDE.md`'s "Operating model — what is real, deferred, paused" section. The interim ends when this seat is filled. The interim is documented to SARB at pre-application engagement (per Mira + Zara confirmation §3.1 — the alternative to filling the seat early was a documented Joint-Standard-1-of-2024 derogation; Marc chose to fill the seat).
