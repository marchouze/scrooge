## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-330-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`, reusable across all BA returns.
- **BA 330 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba330-contract.json` (1 722 cells), loaded + validated via the return-contract registry (`return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA330")`). Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA330` (provenance — an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements (Phase C batch 4 — MODEST, rate-type-keyed)

IRRBB is per-position-derived (the repricing ladder is built from individual banking-book interest-rate positions), so the product-static set is small and attaches to the **future, unapproved banking-book interest-rate product** (`prd:bank:banking-book:irrbb-position`), never the live FX product:

| Attribute | Required? | Why | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rateType` | **required:true** on the rate-type-defining rows (variable / fixed / benchmark / discretionary) | a banking-book IR position cannot be placed in the right rate-type row without its rate type | Basel IRRBB (SRP 31); SARB reg 30 / D2/2023 |
| `repricingProfile` | required:false | the repricing maturity-band columns slice the rate-type rows; sliced, not populate-or-die | Basel IRRBB (SRP 31); SARB reg 30 / D2/2023 |

The banking-book designation is implied by the **form's scope** (BA 330 reports the banking book only — the trading book is on BA 320), so it is not bulk-attached to every cell. **This is the binding the NPA gate enforces** (`recon:npa-return-data-obligation-integrity`): a future banking-book IR product cannot reach approval unless it captures — or tracks as a deferred gap — its owed `required:true` `rateType` attribute. The live FX product feeds NO `required:true` product-id cell and is therefore **not** blocked.

> **Honest substrate gap (Engineering Charter cmd 3 + 5).** The product-scope declaration schema does not yet model banking-book repricing / rate-type attributes; until extended, a future banking-book product is approvable only by tracking each owed attribute as a `ProductDeferredGap`. Tracked, not hidden.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 1 722 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA330")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the cell's figure from the **`ba330-irrbb-fold`** projection: the IRRBB repricing-gap fold over the banking-book interest-rate-sensitive positions decomposed into repricing maturity bands, plus the EVE / NII shock computation under the prescribed scenarios. Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` / `formula` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate expression (`derivation.expression`) over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication:** every BA 330 cell is **`licence-day-data`** — the banking-book interest-rate positions that fill the repricing ladder require a real banking book the bank-in-formation does not run pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every cell. The fold exists as substrate; the product-attribute `dataRequirements` bind **now** so the future banking-book product is gated — the build-now value.

> **Gate coherence.** A `sourced` cell never carries a `required:true` product-attribute on an unapproved future product (that would fail `recon:ba-return-cell-contract`); the generator forces such a cell to `licence-day-data`.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-330-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 330 instance MUST validate against `BA330.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 330 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; the `Monetary1000` value wrappers; the repricing maturity-band columns; the EVE / NII shock-scenario rows).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA330.zip → BA330.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 330 alongside the other authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-330-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Repricing-gap roll-up integrity** | The net static gap (excluding / including derivatives) equals assets − liabilities by repricing band per `derivation.expression`; the cumulative gap equals the running sum of the per-band net gaps. A subtotal that does not equal its constituents is a control failure. |
| **EVE shock integrity** | The change-in-economic-value-of-equity (ΔEVE) under each prescribed interest-rate shock (parallel up / parallel down / steepener / flattener / short-rate up / short-rate down) is computed over the repricing ladder per the Basel IRRBB / D2/2023 scenarios; the worst-case ΔEVE drives the IRRBB outlier test. A divergence between the reported ΔEVE and the fold's computation is a control failure escalated to the CRO / Treasurer (ALCO). |
| **NII shock integrity** | The change-in-net-interest-income (ΔNII) under the parallel-up / parallel-down shocks (including vs excluding derivatives) ties to the fold's NII projection; a divergence is a control failure. |
| **Derivative-impact reconciliation** | The net impact of derivative instruments held in the banking book (pay-fixed/receive-floating, receive-fixed/pay-floating, basis) reconciles to the difference between the including-derivatives and excluding-derivatives gap rows. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba330-irrbb-fold` source datum; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
