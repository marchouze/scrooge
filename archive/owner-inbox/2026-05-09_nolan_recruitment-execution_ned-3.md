## 2. Engagement summary

**Seat shape.** Third Non-Executive Director and third Audit Committee member, completing the three-NED AC floor required by Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.94(2). The seat is **deliberately non-strict-independent** under the standard SA reading of s.94(4)–(6) that two of three AC members must satisfy the substantive-independence test (the Independent Chair / NED #1 and NED #2 carry that floor); the third member must be non-executive but may fail the strict test [citation: Companies Act 71 of 2008 ss.94(2), 94(4)–(6); PAX role-brief §1.1].

**Defining feature.** Deliberate non-strict-independence is the load-bearing feature of the seat. It widens the candidate pool to practitioner-depth profiles that the strict-independence test (and King IV's three-year look-back) would exclude — specifically SA Reserve Bank and Financial Intelligence Centre alumni inside cooling-off / look-back windows, and recently-retired tier-1 SA bank CRO / CFO / MLRO / Head-of-Compliance / Head-of-Internal-Audit profiles whose recent senior-bank service breaches the King IV three-year look-back.

**Primary axis.** Deep regulatory and financial-crime practitioner depth. The seat strengthens the Audit Committee's substantive challenge capacity on banking-regulatory, AML/CFT, and financial-crime matters — the headline gap that strict-independence forecloses at the three-NED floor and which Owen (Company Secretary, governance) §4.3 of the composition paper calls out as the retained caveat.

**Secondary axis.** Deep CDD / financial-crime practitioner depth — FIC alumni, FATF-typology specialists, retired senior bank Financial Crime Officers, SAMLIT participants — for candidates whose primary credential is AML/CFT rather than prudential supervision.

**Sequencing.** Priority-2 alongside the Independent Chair (NED #1) and NED #2 searches. Appointment is sequenced **behind the Independent Chair**: the Chair's appointed profile shapes the residual-skills allocation across NEDs #2 and #3, and the AC composition (two-of-three independent floor) is best confirmed once the Chair is named. Long-listing for NED #3 begins immediately on PR-merge; shortlist held until Chair shortlist closes; offer issued in parallel with NED #2 so the three-NED AC is named together in the SARB licence-application file.

**Compositional fit.** Per PAX §3.6 skills-matrix table, this seat carries (a) banking-regulatory practitioner depth (required), (b) AML/CFT and financial-crime depth (required, including MLRO-alternate fall-back capability), and (c) the highest-leverage diversity contribution across the three-NED Board floor — explicitly soft at long-list, hard at shortlist (PAX §9 #6 recommendation, accepted).

## 4. Candidate-class definition

### 4.1 Primary candidate-class

**(A) SA Reserve Bank Prudential Authority alumni — senior level.**

- Retired Heads / Deputy Heads of: Bank Supervision Department; Prudential Authority Banks Division; National Payment System Department; Financial Surveillance Department; Financial Stability Department.
- Recently-retired Senior Manager / Divisional Head / Head Analyst level inside cooling-off (subject to §10 verification).
- Public-record observable list: ~10–20 individuals at senior-retired level, of whom 6–10 are inside the cooling-off / look-back window that defines this seat's intended pool.

**(B) Financial Intelligence Centre alumni — senior level.**

- Retired Director / Deputy Director / Senior Manager: FIC's Compliance and Prevention Division; Monitoring and Analysis Division; Legal Division.
- Inside cooling-off and look-back windows: similar pool size to (A).
- Public-record observable list: ~3–8 individuals at senior-retired level.

**(C) Recently-retired tier-1 SA bank CRO / CFO / MLRO / Head-of-Compliance / Head-of-Internal-Audit / Head-of-Internal-Financial-Control.**

- Standard Bank, FirstRand / RMB, Absa, Nedbank, Investec — retired within the last 5 years.
- Public-record observable list: ~20–30 individuals across the five tier-1 banks at the relevant level. Of these, ~12–18 are inside the King IV three-year look-back window (i.e. retired within the last 3 years and therefore not strictly s.94 / King-IV-independent).
- Heaviest weight on retired CROs and retired MLROs; CFO retirees compete with NED #2's designated-financial-expert pool and are weighted lower for this seat.

### 4.2 Secondary candidate-class

**(D) Deep CDD / financial-crime practitioners — 15+ years senior depth.**

- Retired heads of Financial Crime / AML / Sanctions at tier-1 SA banks.
- FATF-typology specialists with documented SAMLIT or FIC engagement.
- ACAMS-SA chapter senior members at Director-equivalent level.
- ICA SA chapter senior practitioners.
- Public-record observable list: ~5–15 individuals at senior-retired level.

### 4.3 Why "non-strict-independent" is intentional

The seat is built around the trade-off: deep regulator-fluency and AML/CFT-practitioner-fluency vs. strict King-IV / s.94 independence. SARB / FIC alumni and recently-retired tier-1 bank executives **typically fail strict independence** because:

- Recent regulator employment within the SARB internal post-employment cooling-off window or the National Treasury / DPSA cooling-off framework breaches both s.94(4)(a) (day-to-day management of related parties — read broadly) and the King IV three-year look-back.
- Recent tier-1 bank senior executive service breaches the King IV three-year look-back (and may breach s.94(4)(b) if the candidate was a "prescribed officer" of any related party).
- Advisory or secondment relationships within the SA banking sector — common for retired senior executives — often breach the de-minimis threshold of s.94(4)(c).

The Audit Committee's overall independence is preserved by the **two-of-three independent floor**: the Independent Chair (#1) and NED #2 satisfy the strict test, and any AC vote requiring independent-majority approval (financial-statement sign-off, external-auditor recommendation) is carried by them. This third seat carries practitioner depth that the AC's independent-majority would otherwise lack at the three-NED floor.

The licence-application narrative explicitly defends this composition under Owen §4.3: SARB will probe banking-regulatory depth at the three-NED floor; non-strict-independence on the third NED is *the* mechanism that allows the Board to bring SARB / FIC / retired-bank-CRO depth into the AC at licence-day rather than waiting for post-licence Board expansion to five NEDs.

### 4.4 Candidate-class priority order

Per PAX §9 #2 recommendation (accepted): **A > B > C > D**, with the head-hunter long-list weighted **3 : 1 : 2 : 1** (SARB-alumni : FIC-alumni : retired-bank-exec : financial-crime-practitioner). The 3:1:2:1 weighting prioritises the SARB-alumni signal that SARB itself most values at licence-application time, and balances the practitioner depth across regulator-side and bank-side experience.

## 6. Screening rubric

### 6.1 Hard fails (auto-disqualify at long-list)

- **Companies Act s.69 disqualification** — insolvency, fraud-related convictions, removal-from-office, court-ordered disqualification.
- **Banks Act s.60 / SARB PA fit-and-proper** prior adverse finding — any prior PA / FSCA / JSE / FAIS fit-and-proper finding against the candidate disqualifies.
- **Currently an executive** of the bank, the bank's group, any related party, or any competitor SA bank.
- **Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank** (executive or non-executive).
- **Cooling-off-period breach** — for SARB / FIC alumni, candidate inside the SARB / FIC internal post-employment cooling-off window OR the National Treasury / DPSA post-employment cooling-off framework cooling-off window. **No SARB-internal exemption pursued** — if the candidate is inside cooling-off, the engagement waits until cooling-off completes (PAX §9 #3 boundary, accepted).
- **Material business relationship with the bank's group above de-minimis threshold AND the relationship is current and unresolvable** (historical relationships are permitted; current unresolvable conflicts disqualify).
- **POPIA / Banks Act unmanageable conflict-of-interest** — where a candidate's recent advisory or fiduciary relationships create a conflict that cannot be cured by recusal.
- **Audit-firm conflict** — current or recent partner at the bank's prospective external-audit firm.
- **Non-resident** of South Africa — at most 1 of 3 NEDs may be non-resident under Companies Act s.69 + Banks Act practice (Marc as resident-executive + 3 NEDs); the Chair brief permits the non-resident slot at NED #1 if SARB-acceptable, and PAX §9 #7 (accepted) reserves the resident requirement for NED #3 (the practitioner-depth pool is overwhelmingly SA-resident anyway).

### 6.2 Scoring matrix (long-list to shortlist)

Each candidate scored 1–5 on each axis; score is **weight × axis**. Shortlist = top 3 by total weighted score, subject to diversity-at-shortlist hard constraint.

| Axis | Weight | 5 = | 1 = |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Banking-regulatory practitioner depth** | 25 | Senior SARB / PA / FIC alumni inside look-back window; documented supervisory / financial-stability role | No regulator-side experience |
| **AML/CFT / financial-crime depth** | 20 | Senior MLRO retiree, ex-FIC, FATF-typology specialist; FIC fit-and-proper-credible | No AML/CFT exposure |
| **Audit-Committee credibility** | 15 | Prior AC membership at regulated entity; financial-reporting fluency at IFRS Director-level | No AC experience; no IFRS fluency |
| **Banks Act / PA fit-and-proper credible** | 15 | Already-cleared for fit-and-proper at another regulated entity; no prior findings | Prior findings (auto-disqualify; this score is binary above 1) |
| **Skills-matrix complementarity** with Chair (#1) and NED #2 | 10 | Fills the highest-residual axis (banking-regulatory or AML/CFT) given the Chair / NED #2 named profiles | Duplicates the Chair / NED #2 profiles |
| **Diversity contribution** (gender, race, age) | 10 | Gender + race + age combined contribution against three-NED Board composition | None |
| **Operational-resilience / op-risk awareness** (Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §6, §8) | 5 | Documented op-risk / op-resilience leadership | None |

**Shortlist threshold:** total weighted score ≥ 60/100, with **at least 4/5 on the banking-regulatory practitioner-depth axis OR the AML/CFT axis** (the seat's primary purpose).

**Diversity floor at shortlist:** shortlist of three must contain at least one female candidate and at least one historically-disadvantaged SA candidate (PAX §9 #6 hard at shortlist).

### 6.3 Soft preferences (weight upward at shortlist, not required at long-list)

- Recent SARB / PA / FIC senior employment within cooling-off window (the candidate-class the strict-independence test most clearly excludes).
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- Cross-Africa or cross-jurisdiction banking experience (Principle 5 — multi-currency, multi-entity, multi-country from day one).
- Prior NED experience at any regulated entity (not required given the seat's practitioner-depth emphasis; soft uplift if present).

### 6.4 Conditional flags (raise but do not disqualify)

- **Prior advisory relationship with the bank's group** above de-minimis but historical (>3 years) — surfaced for Owen + Marc; resolvable via disclosure.
- **Recent secondment from a sponsor / correspondent bank** — surfaced; resolvable via recusal regime.
- **Concurrent NED at another SA listed financial institution** — verified for AC-chairmanship workload conflict (PAX §3.4); concurrent AC chair at another SA-listed FI is heavily down-weighted but not auto-disqualifying.

## 10. Cooling-off and conflict management

### 10.1 SARB / FIC alumni cooling-off framework

For every SARB or FIC alumnus candidate, the following must be cleared **before long-list inclusion**:

| Source | Requirement | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| **SARB internal post-employment policy** | Cooling-off-period for senior officials; varies by role (Bank Supervision Department / Prudential Authority typically 12–24 months; Financial Surveillance and National Payment System Department-specific rules apply) | Spencer Stuart attestation; cross-checked by Mira (Compliance / RegTech) against SARB-published rules; candidate-side written representation of cooling-off-end date |
| **National Treasury / DPSA post-employment cooling-off framework** | Cross-government cooling-off rules under the Public Service Regulations and the Financial Disclosure Framework | Mira cross-checks against published National Treasury / DPSA framework |
| **FIC internal post-employment policy** | Cooling-off applies for senior FIC officials (Compliance and Prevention Division, Monitoring and Analysis Division, Legal Division) | Spencer Stuart attestation; cross-checked by Mira |
| **Prevention and Combating of Corrupt Activities Act 12 of 2004 (PRECCA)** | s.17 (post-employment restriction on accepting employment that creates conflict with prior public-service position) | Mira cross-checks against PRECCA reading; documented in cooling-off representation |
| **Anti-Corruption Task Team (ACTT) sectoral guidance** | Where relevant to SARB / FIC officials post-employment | Mira cross-checks |

**Decision rule.** If the candidate is **inside cooling-off**, the engagement waits until cooling-off completes. **No SARB-internal or FIC-internal exemption is pursued** (PAX §9 #3 boundary, accepted). The seat's value depends on the cooling-off being honoured — pursuing an exemption would corrode the regulator-relationship the seat is built to deliver.

### 10.2 Conflict-of-interest framework

For all candidates (regulator-alumni and bank-retiree), the following are surfaced and managed:

| Conflict type | Disclosure | Management |
|---|---|---|
| **Prior employment within the bank's group** (any tier) | Long-list — Spencer Stuart's conflict declaration | Auto-disqualifier if current; permitted with disclosure if historical (>3 years) and resolvable |
| **Recent secondment from sponsor / correspondent bank** | Long-list | Permitted under non-strict-independence; disclosed in conflicts register; recusal regime triggers on AC matters relating to that counterparty |
| **Advisory or consulting relationship with the bank's group above de-minimis threshold** | Long-list | Permitted if historical; recusal on relevant AC matters |
| **Material shareholder or related-party of the bank's group** | Long-list | Disclosed; recusal on relevant AC matters |
| **Concurrent directorship at SA-regulated entity** (non-competitor) | Long-list | Permitted; cross-conflict screen at shortlist; workload check |
| **Family relationship to bank executives or shareholders** (Companies Act s.94(5)) | Shortlist | Disclosed; recusal regime if material |
| **Prior fee-earning advisory to the bank's group above de-minimis** | Long-list | Permitted if historical; recusal |

**Recusal regime.** Codified in the conflicts-of-interest register (Owen substrate per Imani's legal-as-code update); operationalised at AC-meeting level via the meeting-papers tool's recusal-flagging mechanism (substrate gap — manual at licence-day; see §13).

### 10.3 First-12-to-24-months operating posture

For SARB / FIC alumni candidates specifically, the regulator-relationship freshness is a substantive feature of the seat in the first 12–24 months post-appointment. The operating posture:

- **AC-meeting recusal.** The candidate recuses from any AC matter directly relating to their prior regulator work (e.g., SARB Bank Supervision Department alumnus recuses from any AC review of supervisory correspondence relating to a bank or bank-group they previously supervised).
- **No regulator-back-channel.** Explicitly: the candidate does not act as an informal back-channel to SARB / FIC. All regulator-engagement is through the formal Mira (Compliance / RegTech) and Zara (CCO) channels, with Owen (CoSec) as the governance-side conduit. The candidate's value is fluency-on-the-AC, not informal-influence.
- **Disclosure to SARB.** SARB pre-application engagement explicitly discloses the candidate's regulator-prior-history; SARB's own process ratifies (or, in extreme cases, objects to) the appointment.
- **Annual review.** The conflicts-of-interest register reviews the candidate's recusal log at year-end; if recusal patterns suggest an unmanageable conflict, the seat's future is reconsidered at expansion-time.
