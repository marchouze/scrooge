# Role brief — Treasurer

**Author:** PAX
**Date:** 2026-05-06
**For:** Nolan

## 1. Role title and one-line purpose

**Treasurer** — governance owner of the bank's balance sheet and funding posture; runs the treasury function as a stand-alone executive seat reporting directly to the CEO; chairs ALCO.

## 2. Why this role exists

The CEO has confirmed (2026-05-06) that the Treasurer reports directly to the CEO, not through the CFO. This is consistent with a Group-Treasurer-type structure: where treasury is a profit-centre of strategic standing — funding strategy, intraday liquidity, FX position, capital instruments, repo and collateral — rather than a finance-support function. SARB-regulated banks typically have a named Treasurer who carries operational accountability for LCR, NSFR, IRRBB and SAMOS funding; PA engages this seat directly. ALCO requires a chair whose first-line responsibility is balance-sheet management; that is the Treasurer.

This restructures earlier placements: Ravi (treasury / ALM engineer) now reports to the Treasurer rather than to Camille (CFO). Camille's finance perimeter remains accounting, tax, financial reporting, capital management, FP&A, and external audit — and ALCO membership from a capital and earnings perspective.

## 3. Scope of work (priority order)

1. **Funding strategy.** Wholesale funding, deposit-funding strategy, term-funding plan, contingency funding plan execution.
2. **Intraday liquidity and SAMOS funding.** Settlement-account funding; cash and reserve management at SARB; end-of-day square-up.
3. **Liquidity ratio management.** LCR (BA 325), NSFR (BA 326), HQLA composition, LCR-by-currency monitoring, ILAAP-aligned liquidity stress execution.
4. **IRRBB management.** EVE and NII positioning; behavioural-deposit assumptions; hedge programme design and execution. Helena (CRO) sets appetite; the Treasurer manages within it.
5. **FX position management.** Net open position; forward book; hedging; basis between funding and asset legs; intersection with Excon.
6. **Funds Transfer Pricing.** Multi-curve FTP attached to every product event; co-owned with Camille on accounting treatment, with Niko on commercial pricing implications.
7. **Capital actions (operational).** Issuance and management of AT1, T2, MREL-equivalent instruments. Co-owned with Camille on accounting and capital impact, with Helena on capital plan.
8. **Collateral, repo, CSA management.** Collateral inventory; SARB-eligible collateral; CSAs for derivatives.
9. **HQLA portfolio.** Composition and turnover; coordination with Saskia (Head of Global Markets) on execution.
10. **ALCO chair.** Sets agenda; runs the meeting; owns minute-actions through to closure.

## 10. Principle alignment

**P1.** Liquidity, IRRBB, FTP, and collateral positions are projections; end-of-day is a query.

**P2.** Every limit, every ratio, every BA-return cell cites the regulator instrument that creates it.

**P3.** Funding decisions, repo rolls, hedge re-hedging run as workflows. Manual adjustments are tracked exceptions.

**P4.** Treasury workflows touch high-value money movement; transaction-signing and dual control are mandatory.

**P5.** Currency is a type-level property of every cash-flow; FTP is per-currency, per-entity; LCR by significant currency.

**P6.** ALCO pack, funding plan, BA returns are queries; the Treasurer signs the generated output.
