## Purpose of BA 100

Form BA 100 is the prudential return through which the bank reports its **capital adequacy** to the Prudential Authority — the single most important solvency return. It answers the core prudential question: *does the bank hold enough loss-absorbing capital, of high enough quality, against the risk-weighted value of everything on and off its balance sheet?* It is the **apex of the prudential spine** — every risk return below it (credit BA 200, market BA 325, the operational-risk charge) feeds its denominator; the leverage ratio (BA 400) sits *alongside* it as a non-risk-based backstop (see cross-reference).

The headline capital identity (BCBS RBC20.2; Reg 38; computed by the in-repo `ba-100-capital.ts` generator):

```
netCET1  = grossCET1 − cet1Deductions                                  [corresponding-tier deduction]
netAT1   = grossAT1  − at1Deductions
netT2    = grossT2   − t2Deductions

Tier 1   = netCET1 + netAT1
Total    = Tier 1   + netT2

totalRWA = creditRWA (Reg 23 / BA 200) + marketRWA (Reg 28 / BA 325) + operationalRWA (Reg 33)

CET1 ratio   = netCET1 / totalRWA      ≥ 4.5% + buffers          [RBC20.2]
Tier 1 ratio = Tier 1  / totalRWA      ≥ 6.0% + buffers          [RBC20.2]
Total ratio  = Total   / totalRWA      ≥ 8.0% (Basel) / 9.5% (SA, per Reg 38 summary) + buffers
```

A misstated *numerator* (capital tiering / deductions) or a misstated *denominator* (any of the three RWA components) misstates the entire solvency picture — which is why BA 100 sits downstream of all the risk returns and why the no-silent-zeros posture (below) is load-bearing.

**The bank's actual capital position today.** In the build phase the bank holds **no real capital** (CLAUDE.md "build phase vs licence-day" — the R300m is a *licence-day target*, not a present balance). The capital-adequacy substrate runs against the **ICAAP v1 confirmed build-phase baseline** (`D-MARKETS-CAPITAL-TIME-SHAPE`, CEO-approved 2026-05-12): a R300,000,000 total-capital envelope against a franchise-design-scale total RWA, giving a build-phase CET1 ratio far above the regulatory floor. The live `computeCapitalMetrics()` projection switches from the ICAAP-baseline to a **live event-sourced capital fold** the moment real `CapitalEvent` / `CapitalContributionRecorded` events land (a R300m capital injection was posted in the build phase per `project_continuation_2026_06_01_party_bic_fx_attributes` — folded by the live path); the total-RWA denominator is computed **live from booked positions** (`computeRwaFromPositions`, `D-RWA-LIVE-POSITIONS-PROJECTION-V1`) and falls back to the ICAAP constant only when the store has no trade events.

## The capital numerator — qualifying capital + tiering + deductions

The numerator of every capital ratio is **qualifying regulatory capital**, classified into three tiers of decreasing loss-absorbency (BCBS CAP; Reg 38 summary; `ba-100-capital.ts` `CapitalTier = "cet1" | "at1" | "t2"`):

| Tier | Composition (BCBS CAP) | Loss-absorbency | Min ratio (RBC20.2) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)** | Ordinary shares; share premium; retained earnings; accumulated OCI + disclosed reserves; CET1 of consolidated subsidiaries held by third parties (within limits). | Highest — going-concern, fully loss-absorbing. | **4.5%** of RWA |
| **Additional Tier 1 (AT1)** | Perpetual non-cumulative instruments (e.g. contingent-convertible / write-down AT1 notes) meeting the CAP eligibility criteria; AT1 share premium. | Going-concern (subject to triggers). | Tier 1 (CET1+AT1) **≥ 6.0%** |
| **Tier 2 (T2)** | Subordinated debt with original maturity ≥ 5 years (amortising in the final 5 years); general provisions (SA approach, within limits); T2 instruments of subsidiaries. | Gone-concern. | Total (T1+T2) **≥ 8.0% / 9.5% SA** |

**Regulatory deductions (the corresponding-tier approach).** Each deduction is applied to a specific tier — CET1 deductions first (the binding constraint), then AT1, then T2 (BCBS CAP §66–§90; Reg 38(8); `ba-100-capital.ts` `RegulatoryDeduction`). The deduction categories: goodwill and other intangibles; deferred-tax assets that rely on future profitability; cash-flow-hedge reserve; shortfall of provisions to expected losses (IRB only — `NOT_APPLICABLE` under the bank's SA credit election per `D-CREDIT-RISK-CAPITAL-APPROACH-V1`); gain-on-sale on securitisation; defined-benefit pension assets; investments in own shares; reciprocal cross-holdings; and **threshold deductions** (significant investments in financials, DTAs from temporary differences, and MSRs above the 10%/15% CET1 buckets per Reg 38(8)).

## The capital denominator — total RWA (credit + market + operational)

The denominator is **total risk-weighted exposure (RWA)** — the aggregate-risk-weighted-exposure that Reg 38(2) ties back to s.70(2)/(2A)/(2B) of the Act. Reg 38 aggregates the three Pillar-1 components plus counterparty credit risk (`ba-100-capital.ts` `RwaDecomposition`; `computeRwaFromPositions`):

| Component | Regulation | Return | Approach (this bank) | In-repo source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Credit RWA** | Reg 23 | BA 200 | Standardised (`D-CREDIT-RISK-CAPITAL-APPROACH-V1`; IRB `NOT_APPLICABLE`) | `rwa-engine.ts` `computeRwa()` (CRE20 switch) — see [`ba-200.md`](ba-200.md) |
| **Market RWA** | Reg 28/29 | BA 325 (FRTB) | FRTB Standardised (IMA not elected) | FRTB-SA proxy (`rwa.instrument-weight.*`) — see [`ba-325.md`](ba-325.md) |
| **Operational RWA** | Reg 33 | (in BA 100/700) | Standardised (OPE25) | `BusinessIndicatorInput` (zeroed at v0 — gap below) |
| **Counterparty credit RWA** | Reg 23(15)–(19); D7/2020 SA-CCR | BA 200 / BA 210 | SA-CCR (EAD = 1.4 × (RC + PFE)) | `platform/risk/sa-ccr/` — see [`ba-200.md`](ba-200.md) |

## Minimum ratios and the buffer framework

The bank must hold capital ratios above prescribed **minimums + a stack of buffers** (BCBS RBC20.2 + RBC30.2; Reg 38(2)–(7); `ba-100-capital.ts` `BufferRequirements` + `computeRequiredMinimums()`):

| Layer | CET1 | Tier 1 | Total | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pillar-1 base minimum** | 4.5% | 6.0% | 8.0% (Basel) | RBC20.2 |
| **SA Pillar-1 base (per Reg 38 summary)** | 4.5% | 6.0% | **9.5%** | Reg 38 (SA gold-plate on the Total minimum) |
| **+ Capital conservation buffer (CCB)** | +2.5% | +2.5% | +2.5% | RBC30.2 / Reg 38 (CET1-met; breach → distribution constraints) |
| **+ Countercyclical buffer (CCyB)** | +0–2.5% | +0–2.5% | +0–2.5% | SARB-published rate (0% build-phase) |
| **+ D-SIB / systemic surcharge** | +1–2.5% if D-SIB | … | … | SARB designation (Hoz Bank **not** D-SIB at build-phase) |
| **+ Pillar-2A add-on** | bank-specific | … | … | SARB-set (not yet calibrated → 0%) |

The all-in required CET1 minimum is therefore `4.5% + CCB + CCyB + D-SIB + Pillar-2A`. The `computeRequiredMinimums()` function sums the overlay uniformly across CET1 / Tier 1 / Total. The **CCB conservation-range** subtlety: breaching the 2.5% CCB triggers earnings-distribution restrictions but does **not** make the bank "non-compliant" with the hard floor (4.5% / 6% / 8%) — the generator surfaces both the hard-floor flag and the all-in flag (`ba-100-capital.ts` `computeRequiredMinimums` doc).

## The ICAAP linkage (Pillar 2)

The BA 100 is the **Pillar-1** regulatory-minimum view; the **Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Process (ICAAP)** is the **Pillar-2** internal view — the bank's own assessment of *all* material risks (including those not fully captured by Pillar-1) and the capital it judges necessary to cover them. The two are linked: the ICAAP's **Total Internal Capital Requirement (TICR)** sits *above* the Pillar-1 minimum, and the difference between available capital and TICR is the bank's **capital headroom** (`capital-metrics.ts` `headroomMinor = availableCapital − TICR − accruedCharges`; RAS §B3 appetite bands green/amber/red). The build-phase ICAAP v1 figures (`D-MARKETS-CAPITAL-TIME-SHAPE`) are the substrate baseline: R300m envelope, R36.675m TICR, R263.325m headroom. The bank must **submit an annual ICAAP** to the PA (`ORG-PR-13`), and a **consolidated ICAAP** across the full consolidation perimeter (`ORG-BNK-ICAAP-CONS`, s.70A look-through). The Board approves the ICAAP as a reserved matter (`ORG-GV-17`).

## The leverage ratio (BA 400) — the separate non-risk-based backstop

The **leverage ratio** is the **non-risk-based backstop** to the risk-based capital ratios — `Tier 1 capital ÷ total exposure measure` ≥ 3% (BCBS LEV; Banks Act s.70 + Reg 38; `urn:reg:bcbs:lev` lineage). It is computed by the standalone **`ba-400-leverage-ratio.ts`** generator and reported on the **BA 400** return. It is deliberately **NOT folded into the capital-adequacy ratios** — it uses a different denominator (a non-risk-weighted *exposure measure*: on-balance-sheet + derivative exposure (SA-CCR) + SFT + off-balance-sheet commitments), not RWA, precisely so it catches risk that risk-weighting might understate. The `ba-100-capital.ts` generator *can* ship an optional `leverageRatio` section (composed through the single `generateLeverageRatio` primitive so the BA 100 leverage view and a standalone BA 400 report match byte-for-byte) **using the same net Tier-1 numerator** — but this is a *cross-reference convenience on the face of the return*, not an aggregation into the CAR. The leverage ratio is its own obligation (`ORG-PR-05`), its own return (BA 400), and its own Basel spine ([`Regulations/BCBS/lev-leverage-ratio.md`](../../BCBS/lev-leverage-ratio.md)). The build-phase leverage appetite (RAS §B3) sits above the 3% regulatory floor (green ≥ 4.5%, `leverage-ratio-metrics.ts`). **It is a backstop, not a component of total RWA.**

## Binding obligations on the bank (summary)

1. **Maintain capital adequacy at not less than the regulatory minimum** — CET1 ≥ 4.5%, Tier 1 ≥ 6.0%, Total ≥ 8.0% (Basel) / 9.5% (SA per Reg 38 summary), all measured against total RWA (BCBS RBC20.2; Reg 38; Banks Act s.70; `ORG-PR-01`).
2. **Classify qualifying capital into CET1 / AT1 / T2** per the BCBS CAP eligibility criteria and apply regulatory deductions on the corresponding-tier approach (BCBS CAP §66–§90; Reg 38(8); `ba-100-capital.ts`).
3. **Hold the capital conservation buffer (2.5%, CET1-met) + the countercyclical buffer where required** (BCBS RBC30.2; Reg 38; `ORG-PR-03`); breaching the CCB constrains distributions.
4. **Apply Pillar-2A add-ons as set by the PA** (Banks Act + Basel; `ORG-PR-02`; 0% until SARB-calibrated).
5. **Compute total RWA** as credit (Reg 23 / BA 200) + market (Reg 28 / BA 325) + operational (Reg 33) RWA, integrating counterparty credit risk (SA-CCR), under the bank's elected approaches (Reg 38(2); output floor no-op under SA).
6. **Maintain the CET1 management buffer above all PA minima + Pillar-2A + CCB** — RAS §B2 / §B3, ≥ +1.5pp (internal RAS gold-plate; `ORG-PR-04`; calibration pending).
7. **Maintain the leverage ratio ≥ the regulatory minimum (3%)** — the separate non-risk-based backstop, reported on BA 400, **not folded into the CAR** (BCBS LEV; Reg 38; `ORG-PR-05`).
8. **Run the ICAAP** — assess all material risks against internal capital (TICR), submit an annual ICAAP and a consolidated ICAAP to the PA (Banks Act + PA; `ORG-PR-13`; `ORG-BNK-ICAAP-CONS`, s.70A).
9. **Submit form BA 100** to the PA per Annexures 2A/2B of D5/2025 and the Regulations, on the prescribed (monthly) cadence, accompanied by a certified BA 099; the return content is confidential (D5/2025 §2.1.3; Reg 38(1); `ORG-PR-RETURNS-002`; `ORG-PR-RETURNS-001`; exact cadence + Annexure 2B line schema counsel-gated).

**No-silent-zeros posture (D-TRUSTED-FIGURES-PROGRAM-V1).** The generator rejects negative RWA components and out-of-range buffer ratios (throws), enforces the base-ratio ordering (CET1 ≤ T1 ≤ Total), flags capital-classified accounts with the wrong sign (debit balance → warning note), and renders a **divide-by-zero ratio as `Number.POSITIVE_INFINITY`** (no RWA → no risk to cover → infinite ratio, surfaced loudly, treated as compliant) rather than a silent zero. The `capital-metrics` projection distinguishes a **live event-sourced** capital fold from the **ICAAP v1 build-phase fallback** in its `note`. The generator surfaces explicit `[citation: TBC]` placeholders (RWA fixture-grade; missing subCategory; pending line-numbering) into the output `placeholders` array so the recon pipeline tracks placeholder density. When real capital instruments + real RWA land, the generator computes the live CAR over real events.
