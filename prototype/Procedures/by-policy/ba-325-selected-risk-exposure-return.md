## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-325-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`, reusable across all BA returns.
- **BA 325 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba325-contract.json` (409 cells), loaded + validated via the return-contract registry (`return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA325")`). Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA325` (provenance — an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements (Phase C batch 4 — MODEST, summary return)

BA 325's market-risk-requirement summary line references the BA 320 charge (computed per-trade), so its product-static set is small and attaches to the **future, unapproved trading product** (`prd:bank:trading:market-risk-position`), never the live FX product:

| Attribute | Required? | Why | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `riskClass` | **required:true** on the NON-FX risk-class summary rows (interest-rate, equity, commodity) | the summary row that defines a risk-class dimension cannot be populated without the position's class, and there is no live IR/equity/commodity book pre-licence-day | Basel MAR risk-class taxonomy; SARB reg 28(3) |
| `counterpartyExposureType` | required:false | the counterparty-risk memorandum (OTC / SFT / credit-derivative) slices by exposure type; the aggregate still folds | SARB reg 28 / reg 23(15)–(19) counterparty credit risk; Basel CRE |

**This is the binding the NPA gate enforces** (`recon:npa-return-data-obligation-integrity`): a future trading product cannot reach approval unless it captures — or tracks as a deferred gap — its owed `required:true` `riskClass` attribute. The live FX product (`prd:bank:fx:otc-vanilla`) feeds NO `required:true` product-id cell and is therefore **not** blocked.

> **Honest substrate gap (Engineering Charter cmd 3 + 5).** The product-scope declaration schema does not yet model trading-book risk-class attributes; until extended, a future trading product is approvable only by tracking each owed attribute as a `ProductDeferredGap`. Tracked, not hidden.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 409 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA325")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the cell's figure from the **`ba325-selected-risk-exposure-fold`** projection: the market-risk-requirement summary (drawing the BA 320 charge from `ba-320-market-risk.ts`), the counterparty-risk memorandum (OTC / SFT / credit-derivative exposure), the SARB-repo / liquidity summary, and the VaR / sVaR / IRC measures (`var-engine.ts`). Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` / `formula` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate expression (`derivation.expression`) over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication:** every BA 325 cell is **`licence-day-data`** — the trading & treasury selected-risk positions that fill the cells require a real trading book the bank-in-formation does not run pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every cell. The folds exist as substrate; the product-attribute `dataRequirements` bind **now** so the future trading product is gated — the build-now value.

> **Gate coherence.** A `sourced` cell never carries a `required:true` product-attribute on an unapproved future product (that would fail `recon:ba-return-cell-contract`); the generator forces such a cell to `licence-day-data`.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-325-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 325 instance MUST validate against `BA325.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 325 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; the `Monetary1000` / `Monetary1000NN` value wrappers; the per-currency selected-risk columns; the counterparty-risk and liquidity-summary sections).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA325.zip → BA325.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 325 alongside the other authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-325-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Market-risk-requirement summary ties to BA 320** | The BA 325 market-risk-requirement summary line (minimum Pillar-1 market-risk requirement) equals the BA 320 (Market Risk) reported charge from the same `ba320-market-risk-fold`; a divergence between the BA 325 summary and the BA 320 charge is a control failure (the summary must not restate the charge differently). |
| **Capital surplus/deficit integrity** | `Surplus/(deficit) = allocated capital and reserve funds for market risk − total market-risk requirement (pillar 1 + pillar 2a + pillar 2b)` per `derivation.expression`; a roll-up that does not equal its constituents is a control failure. |
| **Counterparty-risk memorandum roll-up** | The counterparty-risk requirement equals the sum of its OTC / SFT / credit-derivative constituents; sliced by `counterpartyExposureType`. |
| **LCR summary line consistency** | The BA 325 daily LCR summary line (HQLA / net-cash-outflows / LCR) is consistent with the canonical LCR computed on **BA 300** (the LCR return proper); a divergence is a control failure. BA 325 carries a *summary* of the LCR, not the authoritative LCR computation. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba325-selected-risk-exposure-fold` source datum; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
