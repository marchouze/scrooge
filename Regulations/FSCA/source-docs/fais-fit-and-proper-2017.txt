The Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017 (Board Notice 194 of 2017)
replaced Board Notice 106 of 2008. It sets requirements under FAIS Act s.8(1) for:

1. HONESTY AND INTEGRITY — Personal character qualities; criminal record;
   civil judgments; regulatory sanctions; disclosure obligations on change of
   circumstances.

2. COMPETENCE — Qualifications (per product sub-categories in Schedule 1);
   Experience (class of business experience); Regulatory examinations (RE1 for KIs,
   RE5 for representatives); Class of Business examinations.

3. OPERATIONAL ABILITY — Ability to fulfil responsibilities imposed by FAIS Act;
   capacity to oversee representatives; ability to comply with General Code.

4. FINANCIAL SOUNDNESS — Solvency; no unrehabilitated insolvency; no debt
   administration/review; maintenance of continuous financial soundness.

5. CPD (CONTINUING PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT) — Ongoing CPD obligation
   post-qualification; minimum CPD hours per year (typically 12–16 CPD points
   per cycle depending on category).

For Category I and II FSPs:
- Category I: Advice on structured products, shares, bonds, money market, forex,
  derivatives (non-complex). Minimum qualification at NQF4 equivalent.
  Experience: minimum 1 year per sub-category before rendering financial services
  without supervision.
- Category II (Discretionary FSP): Manages investments on discretionary basis.
  Higher qualification and experience requirements. NQF5 equivalent minimum.
  Experience: minimum 3 years.

Key Individual requirements are additive to representative requirements — KIs must
satisfy all five dimensions across the full scope of the FSP licence categories.

Regulatory examinations:
- RE 1 (Key Individuals): FSPs authorised in categories I, II, IIA, III, IV must have
  at least one KI who has passed RE 1. Exemptions available in limited circumstances.
- RE 5 (Representatives): All representatives must pass RE 5 (or have an exemption
  under the transitional provisions). RE 5 tests the Act, Codes, and ethics.

Note: As of 2017, the phased-in implementation of the new Determination allowed
legacy KIs and reps to transition from BN 106 of 2008 requirements.
