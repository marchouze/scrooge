## 1. Why redo, what changed

The inaugural package framed the bank as an institutional global-markets house and let "AI-driven" sit as context. That was a misweighting. The bank's defining characteristic — set explicitly in CLAUDE.md and in memory `project_ai_driven_bank.md` — is that it is **AI-run**: a labour force of ~28+ standing autonomous agents under Principle 7 (autonomous by default; humans oversee the residual), with statutory humans kept to the minimum the law requires. Every other AI-driven bank in the regulated-banking world is "AI-augmented". This one is not. The name should signal that without theatricals.

The redo therefore moves the AI / agentic / intelligence axis from "context" to **anchor**. The other gates from v1 — Banks Act s.22 registrability, CIPC availability, Trade Marks Act 194 of 1993 risk, .za / .com / .ai domain signals, SA 11-language sanity-check, institutional-counterparty fit — remain unchanged. What changes is the gate that "Cadens" failed: **AI-theme alignment**. A name for this bank should carry the AI / agentic / intelligence signal explicitly, or through a strong second reading. "Settling" alone does not.

## 2. Naming axes for an AI-driven bank

Six axes worth exploring. For each: what the axis signals, what to avoid, two or three candidates with one-line rationales.

### 2.1 Cognition / intelligence

**Signals:** the bank thinks. Cognitive vocabulary (mind, lucid, reason, infer, percept) reads as intelligence-first without saying "AI".
**Avoid:** clinical-psychology overlap (Cogni-, Psyche-); over-clever neologisms that read as fintech-startup (Brainly, Mindful).

- **Lucet** — invented (Latin *lucere*, "to shine, to be clear"). Reads as clarity-of-reasoning; two syllables; institutional weight.
- **Noeta** — invented (Greek *noēsis*, "thought, intellect"). Distinctive, ungrabbed, slightly more poetic.
- **Percept** — abstract (perception). Strong second reading as "perceiving the market"; risks reading as ad-tech.

### 2.2 Autonomy / agency

**Signals:** the bank acts on its own counsel. Agentic vocabulary (compass, helm, sentinel, custodian, steward) reads as fiduciary AND autonomous.
**Avoid:** military / surveillance overtones (Sentinel, Vanguard read paramilitary); over-classical (Custodian Bank exists in the US).

- **Helmsman** — abstract (the one who steers). Strong agentic signal; risks reading as too-corporate-folksy.
- **Praxis** — abstract (Greek "action, practice"). Reads as the bank that acts; clean two syllables; some philosophical-textbook overlap.
- **Steward** — abstract. Fiduciary + agentic; very clear; possibly too plain (used by US asset managers).

### 2.3 Computation / substrate

**Signals:** the bank is built from a different substrate than its peers. Compute vocabulary (lattice, mesh, axon, kernel, atrium) gestures at the underlying architecture.
**Avoid:** anything that reads as a tech-startup or a B2B SaaS (Mesh Bank reads SaaS; Kernel reads Linux). Must read as a bank first.

- **Lattice** — abstract (a structured network). Suggests the agent network without saying "AI"; some SaaS-HR overlap (Lattice Inc).
- **Atrium** — abstract (architectural; also Latin "central court"). Reads as the central place where the work happens; very clean; possibly too soft for a bank.
- **Synaps** — invented (clipped *synapse*). Strong AI/neural signal; risks reading as biotech.

### 2.4 Continuous / always-on

**Signals:** the bank does not sleep. The agents run on cadence, not on business hours. Per memory `project_indirect_participant_posture.md`, the bank is institutional-only and uses sponsor banks for SAMOS / CLS — so "always-on" is a real characteristic, not a marketing claim.
**Avoid:** over-cosmic (Aurora, Nimbus read sci-fi); time-boundedness (Dawn, Dusk are temporal not perpetual).

- **Meridian** — abstract (line of longitude; high noon). Reads as positioned, persistent; some overlap (Meridian Bank exists in multiple jurisdictions).
- **Vigil** — abstract (sustained watchfulness). Strong always-on signal; risks reading as funereal in some registers.
- **Continua** — invented (Latin *continuum*). Distinctive; carries the always-on signal cleanly.

### 2.5 Trust / fiduciary + AI

**Signals:** the bank bridges classical-bank trust with AI-agentic precision. Names from the codex / oracle / augur lineage hint at the AI dimension via "consults the data" rather than saying "AI".
**Avoid:** occult / mystical overtones (Oracle is taken anyway; Augur is too divinatory); ad-tech overtones (Codex was OpenAI's product name).

- **Codex** — abstract (a body of laws / a manuscript). Reads as the bank that operates from a coded body of rules; **but** OpenAI's product Codex contaminates the read for the next several years.
- **Axiom** — abstract (a self-evident truth; foundational rule). Reads as first-principles + fiduciary; clean two syllables; some IP overlap (Axiom legal services).
- **Concordia** — abstract (Latin "agreement, harmony"). Reads as agreed-rules, agent-coordination; longer; possibly too soft.

### 2.6 Mathematical / first-principles

**Signals:** the bank reasons from data. Mathematical vocabulary (axiom, theorem, lemma, kernel, vector) reads as rigorous and AI-coherent (the AI substrate IS mathematical).
**Avoid:** reading as a quant fund (Vector, Theorem are taken in that lane); reading as too-academic (Lemma, Postulate).

- **Axiom** — see §2.5; sits across both axes.
- **Kernel** — abstract (mathematical core; OS substrate). Strong AI-substrate signal; risks reading as Linux / B2B-SaaS.
- **Theorem** — abstract (a proven proposition). Distinctive; institutional gravity; some hedge-fund overlap.

### 2.7 Axes I dropped

I considered "Future / next-generation" (Vanta, Nova, Apex) and dropped it — fintech-startup register, fails AI-theme on its own. I considered "Geographic + AI" (Cape, Stellenbosch, Karoo) and dropped it — parochial, contradicts the institutional-international positioning from v1. I considered acronyms (AIB, AIBank, ABank) and dropped them — undignified, IP-thin.

## 4. v2 shortlist (top three)

The three names that survive every gate with the strongest AI-theme signal, ranked.

### 4.1 Lucet

- **Axis:** Cognition / intelligence (§2.1).
- **Rationale (~80 words).** Lucet (Latin *lucere*, "to shine, to be clear") reads as clarity-of-reasoning — exactly the property an AI-run bank should claim. Two clean syllables (LOO-set), distinctive, ungrabbed in the SA financial register. The institutional second reading is "the bank that can be understood" — counterparties, regulators, auditors all care about that. Carries on a phone line. Pairs naturally with the v1 brand-package palette (Slate / Clay / Paper) — "lucet" is itself a colour-of-light reference.
- **Phonetic + visual.** LOO-set, two syllables, stress on the first. In Inter Display 700 it sits cleanly with no awkward letter-pair widths; the `t` terminal gives the wordmark a sharp finish. In IBM Plex Sans 500 (body) it reads precise, not poetic.
- **Logo direction.** Wants a **wordmark + light-mark** treatment — a thin horizontal mark above or beside the wordmark referencing the "shining" / "becoming clear" connotation. Direction A from v1 (the geometric mark with the chord) re-reads as a horizon catching light; the chord becomes a beam. Glyph-only treatment also works: a single thin horizontal at the wordmark's x-height.
- **Domain (best-effort, signals only).** `.com` may be taken by Lucet Health (US healthcare-tech) [citation: TBC]; `.za` likely available [citation: TBC]; `.ai` likely available [citation: TBC]. Imani's pre-clearance is the gate.

### 4.2 Noeta

- **Axis:** Cognition / intelligence (§2.1) — adjacent to autonomy (§2.2).
- **Rationale (~80 words).** Noeta (Greek *noēsis*, "thought, intellect") reads as the bank that thinks. Distinctive, three syllables (no-EH-tah), no major IP collisions I am aware of. The Greek root sits in the same lineage as Lucet's Latin — institutional, ageless, internationally pronounceable. Slightly more poetic / abstract than Lucet, which is either an advantage (more distinctive) or a disadvantage (slightly less self-explanatory). The AI-theme reads explicitly to anyone with a passing classical-education reference and as "interesting Greek-rooted name" to anyone without.
- **Phonetic + visual.** no-EH-tah. In Inter Display 700 the double-letter pairs (oe, ta) sit comfortably at display sizes; some optical-spacing work needed at small sizes for the `oe` pair. In IBM Plex Mono 400 (for typed identifiers, register URNs) it reads cleanly.
- **Logo direction.** Wants a **wordmark-only or wordmark + small monogram** treatment. The "N" lends itself to a serifed monogram (Direction B from v1) more than Lucet does. Glyph-only is harder — there is no obvious abstract symbol for "thought" that does not read clichéd (lightbulbs, brains).
- **Domain.** `.com` likely available or weakly held [citation: TBC]; `.za` likely available [citation: TBC]; `.ai` likely available [citation: TBC]. Strongest domain position of the three.

### 4.3 Synaps

- **Axis:** Computation / substrate (§2.3) — adjacent to cognition (§2.1).
- **Rationale (~80 words).** Synaps (clipped *synapse*) reads as the bank built from a network of connections — exactly the architectural truth of an agent-fleet bank. Two clean syllables (SIN-aps), distinctive, no major financial-services TM I am aware of. The biotech / neuroscience read is the risk: institutional counterparties might pause on first encounter. The reward is the strongest, most explicit AI-theme signal of the three — "synapse" is the most direct neural-network reference that is not literally "Neural" or "AI".
- **Phonetic + visual.** SIN-aps. In Inter Display 700 it reads sharply; the descender-free letterforms make for a tight, contained wordmark. In IBM Plex Mono 400 it reads precise — almost mathematical.
- **Logo direction.** Wants a **mark + wordmark** treatment with a glyph that abstractly references connection — not a literal synapse drawing (clichéd, biotech-coded), but a small geometric abstraction (two nodes with a connecting line; a stylised junction). Direction C from v1 (three parallel lines) re-reads as nodes-and-connections.
- **Domain.** `.com` `synaps.com` likely contested [citation: TBC]; `.ai` likely available [citation: TBC]; `.za` likely available [citation: TBC]. Imani should test the German "Synaps GmbH" register and any biotech-sector TMs.

## 5. Single recommendation

**Lucet.** Recommended over Noeta because it is shorter, more pronounceable across the institutional-international counterparty base, and self-explanatory in a way Noeta's Greek root is not — counterparties who do not catch the Latin still hear "clarity, light", which is the right second reading. Recommended over Synaps because it does not carry the biotech / neuroscience contamination — a name that reads ambiguously as a bank or a biotech firm fails the institutional-counterparty gate, and Lucet does not have that problem. Recommended over Cadens (v1) because Cadens encoded "settling" — true of any bank — whereas Lucet encodes clarity-of-reasoning, which is true of *this* bank specifically. Picking Lucet commits the bank to a posture of legibility: every regulator submission, every counterparty pitch, every customer notice has to be intelligible at first read. That is a constraint I think the brand can carry, and one that is coherent with Principle 6 (presentations derive downward from data; the rendering must be clear).

Full trade name at licence-grant: **Lucet Bank Limited**, with **Lucet** as the standalone build-phase mark. CIPC and TM pre-clearance go to Imani as the next gate.
