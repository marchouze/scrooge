## 6. Conflict-of-interest map

Same structure as Chair §6 / NED #2 §6, with trading-NED-specific additions:

- **Direct trading-desk competitor cross-employment.** The single most material conflict for this seat. Any candidate active at a tier-1 bank's Global Markets desk in a senior role within ≤2 years is materially conflicted on the bank's intended franchise. Restraint horizons typically apply.
- **Active FAIS KI at a competing FSP.** The candidate's KI-status at a major asset manager or broker may overlap with the bank's intended FSP scope (counsel-confirmed); conflicts register and FAIS-KI-allocation review at appointment.
- **Cross-counterparty fiduciary duty.** If the candidate sits on the Board of a likely ISDA / GMRA counterparty, the cross-fiduciary-duty issue is acute on counterparty-credit limit-setting; recusal from related decisions, register entry.
- **Audit-firm overlap** — same as NED #2 §6.
- **MLRO-alternate fit-and-proper** — if §1 MLRO-alternate-fall-back triggers, the candidate must satisfy the FIC fit-and-proper criteria as if principal; verify at offer-stage.
