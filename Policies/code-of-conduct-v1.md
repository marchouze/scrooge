# Code of Conduct v1

> **Status:** IN FORCE. Ethical conduct obligations apply from the bank's inception. This Code applies to all participants in the bank's operations: autonomous AI agents acting on behalf of the bank, and statutory human appointees.

> **Author:** Zara (Chief Compliance Officer, governance); secretariat Owen (Company Secretary, governance).

## 1. Ethical principles

All bank participants are expected to:

1. **Act with integrity** — conduct all activities honestly and transparently.
2. **Avoid conflicts of interest** — disclose any personal, financial, or external interest that could influence bank decisions.
3. **Protect confidential information** — handle customer, counterparty, and proprietary data per the bank's data classification policy and POPIA obligations.
4. **Comply with law** — adhere to all applicable legislation, regulations, and bank policies.
5. **Report concerns** — raise suspected breaches via the whistleblowing channel without fear of retaliation (Protected Disclosures Act 26 of 2000).

---

## 2. Conflicts of interest

- All statutory human appointees must disclose conflicts before taking part in any decision where a personal interest exists.
- AI agents do not have personal interests; however, agent code or prompts that create systematic bias toward a particular outcome must be flagged by the engineering team to the CCO.

---

## 3. Gifts and entertainment (human appointees)

- No gifts exceeding R500 in value may be accepted from counterparties, vendors, or regulators.
- All gifts and entertainment above R200 are recorded in the gifts register (maintained by Zara).
- Hospitality must not create a perception of improper influence.

---

## 4. Market conduct

- No market manipulation, front-running, or misuse of inside information.
- All trading activity is subject to the Trading Mandate and Conduct Risk Policy.
- Suspected market abuse incidents are escalated to Zara immediately.

---

## 5. Breach and reporting

Suspected breaches are reported to Zara (CCO). Anonymous reporting is available via the whistleblowing channel (see `whistleblowing-policy-v1.md`). Substantiated breaches are escalated to the CEO and, where material, to the Board and regulators.
