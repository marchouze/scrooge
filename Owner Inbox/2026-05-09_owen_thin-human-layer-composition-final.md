## 3. Reporting lines and functional independence

The reporting lines preserve second-line independence for the seats where it is regulator-binding:

- **Independent Chair** → chairs the Board; reports through Board fiduciary structure to shareholders.
- **NED #2 and NED #3** → Board members; AC members.
- **Company Secretary** → Board; functional independence from CEO under Companies Act s.86(2)(b).
- **Triple-hatted Compliance Lead** → CEO administratively; **functionally to the Board / AC** for STR-escalation pathways (FIC Act s.43A read with FIC's published RMCP guidance) and POPIA-Regulator pathways (POPIA s.56). The functional line is what FIC's published guidance treats as the second-line check on STR escalations leaving the institution.
- **Human CRO** → CEO administratively; **functionally to the Board / BRC** for risk-appetite, limit-breach, and JS 1 of 2024 §7 operational-independence pathways.

The agent fleet (Helena engineering CRO, Camille engineering CFO, Owen engineering CoSec, Zara engineering CCO + named MLRO of the agent fleet, Iris engineering IO) continues to operate as Principle-7 standing autonomous agents. The licence-day human seats are the regulator-facing designations the agent outputs flow through.

## 4. Concentration-risk caveats (updated)

The original Owen+Imani draft flagged three caveats. With the CRO modification, one is dropped and the others are retained.

### 4.1 ~~CEO + interim-CRO + interim-CFO concentration~~ — **DROPPED**

The interim-CRO-as-CEO concentration is **eliminated** by the modification. Seat #6 is filled before licence-application lodgment, removing the second-line-CRO-on-CEO architectural defect Mira+Zara identified. The CFO half of the original concentration (interim-CFO-on-CEO) is retained and is addressed under §4.4 below, but the CRO half is structurally resolved.

### 4.2 Triple-hatted MLRO + FIC CO + IO — **RETAINED**

The compliance lead carrying MLRO + FIC CO + IO is statutorily compliant (each merger is contemplated) but is a heavy single-person workload. SARB / FIC's expected challenge: *what is the deputy / succession arrangement?* The licence-application narrative defends this on the basis that:

- Zara and Iris are operationally accountable engineering seats running the AML/CFT and POPIA substrates.
- The deputy-IO and MLRO-alternate arrangement is split — see §5.
- The fit-and-proper file declares the triple-hat overlap explicitly and demonstrates capacity.

### 4.3 NED-independence depth — **RETAINED**

Three NEDs is the floor under Companies Act s.94. Two of the three (Chair and NED #2) must be independent in the strict s.94 sense; NED #3 must be non-executive but need not be independent. SARB's expected challenge under King IV and BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015: *is the Board of sufficient depth on banking-regulatory experience, technology / cyber, financial expertise, and transformation / B-BBEE?* The licence-application narrative answers:

- The skills-and-diversity matrix at three NEDs is necessarily concentrated.
- The bank commits to expanding the Board to five NEDs within a defined post-licence window.
- The framework's §1 skills matrix is the document the Board recruits against.

SARB has historically accepted floor compositions for small banks at licence-day on the strength of an explicit expansion commitment.

### 4.4 Interim-CFO-as-CEO — **RETAINED (rephrased from 4.1)**

The CFO half of the original §4.1 concentration stands as a SARB-precedent challenge that the licence-application narrative must defend. The expected challenge: *how does the Board challenge the CEO's finance numbers when the CEO is also the CFO?* The licence-application narrative answers:

- Camille is operationally accountable as the engineering CFO seat.
- The AC and (proxy) BRC chaired by independent NEDs perform challenge.
- A named human CFO is appointed as the **first hire post-licence**, with a defined timeline.
- The external auditor + AC scrutiny substitute for CFO independence at licence-day specifically — this is an applicant's argument to make, not a statutory permission.

This is now the single load-bearing concentration-risk in the licence-application file (vs three in the original draft).

## 5. Alternates split (deputy-IO and MLRO-alternate)

Per Mira+Zara §4.2 and codified in governance framework §3B, the deputy-IO and MLRO-alternate roles are **split across two humans** rather than both falling on the CoSec.

### 5.1 Deputy POPIA Information Officer = Company Secretary (seat #4)

POPIA Regulation 4 contemplates a deputy IO. The CoSec carries this designation alongside the Compliance Lead's primary IO role. Lodgment with the Information Regulator follows the primary IO designation and is itself a CEO act (out-of-system).

**Citation:** POPIA Act 4 of 2013 s.56(c) read with POPIA Regulation 4(b); FIC published guidance on accountable-institution governance (no statutory bar on CoSec-as-deputy-IO; published-guidance recommendation).

### 5.2 MLRO-alternate = AC-Chair NED (seat #1)

FIC published guidance on accountable-institution governance recommends an MLRO-alternate. The AC Chair is designated as the alternate on two grounds:

- AC oversight already touches AML/CFT controls — the AC Chair is closer to the substantive material than any other NED.
- Splitting the alternate load across two humans (CoSec for IO, AC Chair for MLRO) avoids a single-point-of-failure at the CoSec seat.

The fit-and-proper file declares this overlap explicitly per Mira+Zara §4.3 — the AC Chair as both AC Chair and MLRO-alternate is a documented overlap, not a hidden one.

**Citation:** FIC Act ss.43A–43B; FIC published RMCP guidance on MLRO-alternate designation; King IV on AC oversight scope; BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015 §3.

This split supersedes any earlier draft that named the CoSec for both alternates.

## 7. Auditor and key-audit-partner separation

The **external auditor is a firm**, appointed under Banks Act s.61 and Companies Act s.90. The audit firm:

- Is on the bank's regulator-facing register as the named auditor.
- Carries the obligation to nominate a key audit partner who satisfies SARB Prudential Authority fit-and-proper.
- Is approved by the Prudential Authority under Banks Act s.61.

The **key audit partner is an employee of the audit firm**, named in the firm's engagement letter:

- Is **not on the bank's own thin-human-layer roster** — they are the audit firm's named individual, not the bank's.
- Is reviewed by SARB for fit-and-proper because the audit firm carries that obligation, not because the bank does.
- Rotates per the audit firm's professional-standards rotation rules (Auditing Profession Act 26 of 2005).

This separation matters because it determines who is on the bank's regulator-facing fit-and-proper file: the firm, not the partner. The bank's appointment obligation is to the firm; the firm's appointment obligation is to the partner.

**Citation:** Banks Act 94 of 1990 s.61; Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.90; Auditing Profession Act 26 of 2005; SARB Prudential Authority approval under Banks Act s.61.

## 7A. Multi-entity application (added 2026-05-09 per D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0)

> **Decision authority.** D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0 (CEO approve, 2026-05-09; record at `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-legal-entity-tree-v0.md`, PR #82) and D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER (CEO approve, 2026-05-09; record at `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-regulatory-perimeter.md`, PR #85). The 6-human composition specified in §2 was originally bank-centric. Under the three-entity Hoz group structure, the same composition serves as a **shared board** across all three entities.

### 7A.1 Shared-board posture across the three Hoz entities

Per D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0 §4 (CEO confirmed), the same 6 humans + Marc + audit firm in §2 serve as the shared board across:

1. **Hoz Group Limited** — holding company (Companies Act 71 of 2008).
2. **Hoz Bank Limited** — bank entity (SARB Banks Act 94 of 1990 applicant).
3. **Hoz Securities Limited** — securities entity (JSE Equities + Bonds Membership applicant; FAIS FSP scope ratified by counsel at licence-application).

The **composition itself is unchanged** by the multi-entity application — six humans + Marc + audit firm, with the seat allocations exactly as in §2. What changes is the **applicability**: the same humans wear entity-specific hats and clear entity-specific fit-and-proper assessments per the perimeter set out in D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER.

### 7A.2 Entity-specific fit-and-proper triangulation

Every director on the shared board clears three fit-and-proper regimes:

- **Bank fit-and-proper** (Banks Act s.60 + SARB PA expectations + Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §6) — for the Hoz Bank Limited seat.
- **Securities fit-and-proper** (FAIS Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017 + JSE Membership Rules + JSE Listings Requirements) — for the Hoz Securities Limited seat.
- **Director fit-and-proper** (Companies Act ss.69, 71) — for the Hoz Group Limited seat (and base layer applicable to all three).

The fit-and-proper file template owned by Sade (AgentOps engineer) — **PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01** (PR #69, merged) — is the substrate template that extends to multi-entity. Each director's file under PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01 carries entity-specific declarations covering all three regimes; the AgentOps substrate captures the triangulation as a typed structure rather than three separate files. Sade's per-entity extension of PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01 is a **v1 substrate task** surfaced as a follow-on to D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0.

### 7A.3 Per-entity statutory officers

Companies-Act-required officers (CoSec under ss.86–89; Public Officer under Tax Administration Act s.246) operate at each entity, but **may be the same human across all three entities** — there is no statutory bar on a single CoSec or Public Officer serving multiple group entities. Per-entity-regulator officers vary:

- **Hoz Group Limited**: Public Officer + CoSec + External Auditor (all shared with Hoz Bank by default; same humans / firm); no MLRO / FIC CO / FAIS KI (group is not an accountable institution and does not transact in the FAIS sense).
- **Hoz Bank Limited**: Public Officer + CoSec + External Auditor; **MLRO + FIC Compliance Officer** (Seat #5, triple-hatted Compliance Lead under FIC Act ss.43A, 43B); **POPIA IO + Deputy IO** (Seat #5 IO, Seat #4 Deputy IO per §5).
- **Hoz Securities Limited**: Public Officer + CoSec + External Auditor (shared with Hoz Bank); **MLRO + FIC Compliance Officer** — *same human as Hoz Bank* (both bank and securities are accountable institutions under FIC Act Schedule 1 — banks under item 6; persons authorised to deal in securities under item 13); **POPIA IO + Deputy IO** (Iris's per-entity scoping; default same humans as Hoz Bank); **FAIS Key Individual** = Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance) per D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY confirm-A; **JSE Member-firm representatives** (directors of the member firm + Compliance Officer + Settlement Officer designations per JSE Equities & Bonds Membership Rules; counsel ratifies the precise list at licence-application).

The full per-entity table is in the governance framework §3F (`Owner Inbox/2026-05-06_governance-framework.md`, canonical authoring location).

### 7A.4 Regulatory perimeter implications

Per D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER:

- **Hoz Group Limited** is **not separately regulated** (Companies Act + IFRS 10 + IAS 24; PA consolidated-supervision look-through via the bank). The group does not hold a separate prudential / conduct licence.
- **Hoz Bank Limited** is SARB PA-regulated under Banks Act 94 of 1990 + Regulations Relating to Banks + Prudential Standards + Joint Standards. Group-wide consolidated metrics bind on Hoz Bank Limited measured at group level (PA look-through).
- **Hoz Securities Limited** is JSE-regulated (primary) + FSCA-regulated (secondary, FAIS conduct).

This refines the fit-and-proper triangulation in §7A.2: SARB PA fit-and-proper applies primarily to the bank-entity seat, and JSE / FSCA fit-and-proper applies to the securities-entity seat. The group-entity seat carries the Companies Act director duties only — there is no separate prudential / conduct fit-and-proper at the group level.

### 7A.5 Substrate gaps surfaced by multi-entity application

- **Per-entity PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01 extension** — Sade (AgentOps engineer) v1 substrate task. Captures the bank / securities / group fit-and-proper triangulation as a typed structure inside one director-file per human, rather than three files per director.

- **Per-entity POPIA Information Officer designations** — Iris (Information Officer, governance) is producing the substantive scoping in `Owner Inbox/<date>_iris_per-entity-popia-io-designation.md` (concurrent dispatch). This composition paper defers IO substantive analysis to Iris's authoring.
