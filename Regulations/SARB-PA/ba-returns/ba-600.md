## Purpose of BA 600

Form BA 600 is the prudential return through which the bank reports its **statement of financial position** (balance sheet) to the Prudential Authority — the point-in-time snapshot of *what the bank owns* (assets), *what it owes* (liabilities), and *the residual interest of its owners* (equity), as at the reporting date. It is the **financial-position half** of the financial-statement pair; its companion is the **income statement (BA 610)** — the flow statement of how the period's profit-or-loss moved retained earnings, which is itself an equity line on this balance sheet (see [`ba-610.md`](ba-610.md) cross-reference). Together BA 600 + BA 610 are the IFRS-grounded financial-reporting backbone of the BA-return set; the risk-and-capital returns (BA 100, BA 200, BA 325, BA 400, BA 110/120) draw their underlying figures from the same general ledger that BA 600 reconciles to.

The headline balance-sheet identity (IAS 1 §54; Reg 18; computed by the in-repo `ba-600-balance-sheet.ts` generator):

```
assetsTotal       = Σ |amountMinor| over rows classified as `assets/*`
liabilitiesTotal  = Σ |amountMinor| over rows classified as `liabilities/*`
equityTotal       = Σ |amountMinor| over rows classified as `equity/*`

Invariant (the accounting equation):  assetsTotal ≡ liabilitiesTotal + equityTotal   (per reporting currency)
```

The invariant is the **load-bearing control** of the whole return — a balance sheet that does not balance is, by definition, mis-stated. The `ba-600-balance-sheet.ts` generator enforces it with a configurable absolute tolerance (default **0 — strict**) and **throws** on violation (`Ba600GeneratorError`: *"balance-sheet invariant violated — assets ≠ liabilities + equity …"*); a rehearsal soft-fail surfaces the imbalance as a placeholder via `tolerateImbalanceMinor`. This is the no-silent-zeros posture (`D-TRUSTED-FIGURES-PROGRAM-V1`) applied to the most fundamental accounting identity.

**The bank's actual balance sheet today.** In the build phase the bank holds **no real customer assets or liabilities** (CLAUDE.md "build phase vs licence-day"). The balance-sheet substrate runs over the live general ledger, which at build-phase carries the **R300,000,000 capital injection** (posted in the build phase per `project_continuation_2026_06_01_party_bic_fx_attributes`), the FX-desk cash and trading positions (the live sub-ledger), and the inter-bank / nostro balances of the simulator. The generator is a **pure projection over the period-close `TrialBalance`** (Slice 2) — events → projection-runtime → period-close trial balance → IFRS semantic layer → BA 600 — so the figure is always event-sourced (Principle 1), never a stored balance.

## Off-balance-sheet items reported within BA 600

Reg 18's summary expressly lists **"off-balance sheet items"** among the BA 600 content. Off-balance-sheet exposures — loan commitments, financial guarantees, letters of credit, undrawn facilities, and derivative notionals — do **not** sit in the assets/liabilities/equity totals of the statement of financial position (they are *contingent*, not recognised), but the BA 600 return carries them in a **memorandum / off-balance-sheet section** so the PA sees the bank's full contingent exposure alongside its recognised one. There is **no standalone D5/2025 off-balance-sheet return** — off-balance-sheet exposures are reported *within* BA 200 (credit-risk CCF-weighted), BA 210 (counterparty credit risk / SA-CCR), and the BA 600 memorandum section (see the `Regulations/_index.md` off-balance-sheet row: *"no standalone D5/2025 return; reported within BA 200 (credit), BA 210 (CCR) and BA 600 (balance sheet)"*; the companion `off-balance-sheet.md` analysis is STUB pending authoring). The credit-risk CCF treatment of off-balance-sheet exposures (BCBS CRE20 credit-conversion-factor table) is analysed under [`ba-200.md`](ba-200.md); the BA 600 memorandum section re-presents the same contingent notionals on the face of the balance-sheet return.

## GL reconciliation

Reg 18's summary expressly requires **"reconciliation to general ledger"** — the BA 600 totals must tie back, line by line, to the bank's general ledger / chart of accounts. The substrate enforces this structurally: BA 600 is a **pure projection over the period-close trial balance** (`ba-600-balance-sheet.ts` folds `TrialBalanceSnapshotRow`s), and the trial balance is itself a projection over the event log (Principle 1). There is therefore no manual re-keying between the GL and the return — the return *is* a re-grouping of the GL trial balance into the SARB line schedule, and the accounting-equation invariant (`assetsTotal ≡ liabilitiesTotal + equityTotal`, enforced strict by default) is the reconciliation control. The chart of accounts (`prototype/platform/accounting/coa-registry.ts`) classifies each leaf account into an IFRS class; the BA 600 line-classification map (caller-supplied at v0 — the substrate gap) maps each leaf account to a SARB BA 600 line. When the chart-of-accounts schema gains a native `ba600Line` field (the planned gap), the map moves from the call site into the COA and the reconciliation becomes self-describing.

- The **BA 600 return** is described by Reg 18's verbatim title as a **monthly balance sheet** (*"completion of monthly balance sheet"*); the `ORG-PR-RETURNS-020` obligation row records a **monthly** frequency. The return content is **confidential** (Reg 18 summary: *"content is confidential and not available for public inspection"*). Every return set is accompanied by a signed, certified form **BA 099** (`ORG-PR-RETURNS-001`, D5/2025 §2.1.2).

## Material foreign-currency reporting

The balance sheet is multi-currency: foreign-currency monetary assets and liabilities are translated at the closing spot rate (IAS 21 §23); the FX-desk cash positions are `fi:csh:<CCY>:<book>` instruments revalued into ZAR (IAS 21 §28, per `project_continuation_2026_06_03_pnl_cash_valuation`); the FX-translation reserve sits within equity (`equity/*` other reserves). ZAR is the presentation currency; the multi-currency exposure is the data (Principle 5). The v0 `ba-600-balance-sheet.ts` generator surfaces per-currency totals (`Ba600PerCurrencyTotal`) in its output but presents the SARB form in `functionalCurrency` (ZAR); multi-currency face-of-return presentation tracks the IFRS Slice 6 posture.

## Binding obligations on the bank (summary)

1. **Prepare and present a statement of financial position** on the IFRS basis (IAS 1 §54 minimum line-items; order-of-liquidity presentation per IAS 1 §60 for a bank); recognise and measure assets / liabilities / equity per IFRS 9 / IFRS 13 / IAS 32 (`ORG-PR-RETURNS-020`).
2. **Reconcile the BA 600 totals to the general ledger** (Reg 18 summary — *"reconciliation to general ledger"*); the accounting-equation invariant (assets ≡ liabilities + equity) is the reconciliation control.
3. **Report off-balance-sheet items within BA 600** (Reg 18 summary — *"off-balance sheet items"*); contingent exposures carried in the memorandum section, cross-reported in BA 200 / BA 210 (credit / CCR).
4. **Submit form BA 600** to the PA per Annexures 20A/20B of D5/2025 and the Regulations, on the prescribed (monthly) cadence, accompanied by a certified BA 099; the return content is confidential (D5/2025 §2.1.21; Reg 18; `ORG-PR-RETURNS-020`; `ORG-PR-RETURNS-001`; exact cadence + Annexure 20B line schedule counsel-gated).
5. **Maintain accounting records and prepare annual financial statements** per Banks Act s.75 and the Companies Act, of which the BA 600 statement of financial position is the prudential re-presentation.
