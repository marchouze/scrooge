## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-930-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`.
- **BA 930 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba930-contract.json` (795 cells), loaded + validated via `loadReturnContract("BA930")`. Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA930` (an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 The weighted-average rate is an AGGREGATE statistic — ZERO product-attribute requirements

The reported value is a **portfolio-level weighted-average rate** (a balance-weighted aggregate over the whole loan / deposit book), **not** a product-static menu attribute: a single product does not carry "the weighted-average rate" — it is computed across many products' contractual rates and balances at report time. So — like the capital / operational families — BA 930 carries **NO `product-attribute` requirement**. No BA 930 cell gates a product: the NPA gate sees no BA 930 product-attribute edge, so the live FX product and every future product are correctly unaffected. We do **not** manufacture any (Engineering Charter — no fabrication; no bulk-marking).

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 795 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA930")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the figure from the **`ba930-weighted-average-rates-fold`** projection over the loan + deposit book — balance-weighted nominal / effective rates by product category and maturity band. Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate calculation over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication:** **all 795 BA 930 cells are `status: licence-day-data`.** The fold exists as substrate, but every rate / balance cell needs a **real** loan / deposit book with real contractual rates, which the bank-in-formation does not run pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every cell.

> **Currency (P5).** Balances are reported in the functional currency, resolved from reference data, never hard-coded; the weighted-average rates are dimensionless ratios.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-930-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 930 instance MUST validate against `BA930.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 930 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; `Monetary1000` + `Percentage 19,2` value wrappers; the product-category × maturity-band structure).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA930.zip → BA930.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 930 alongside the other 28 authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-930-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Rate = balance-weighted average** | Each reported weighted-average rate must equal the balance-weighted mean of the constituent contractual rates over the matching product / maturity band; an unweighted or mis-weighted rate is a control failure. |
| **Weight consistency** | The balance the rate is weighted by must tie to the corresponding loan / deposit balance the bank reports elsewhere (BA 900 / BA 100 loan + deposit lines) for the same category; a divergence is a control failure. |
| **Rate bounds sanity** | A reported rate outside a plausible bound for its category (e.g. a negative nominal lending rate) is flagged as a control finding for review before submission. |
| **Hashtotal integrity** | The form's control hashtotals must equal the sum over their declared cell range; a mismatch blocks submission. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba930-weighted-average-rates-fold` source data; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
