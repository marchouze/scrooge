## 1. Scope — Companies Act s.11 / s.12 / Reg.8 surface

### 1.1 s.11 — name requirements

Companies Act s.11 sets out the criteria a name must meet to be acceptable for a company. The substantive requirements counsel must confirm "Hoz" + chosen suffix satisfies are (without being exhaustive):

- The name must not be the same as or confusingly similar to the name of another existing company / external company / close corporation / cooperative / business trust [citation: TBC — exact s.11(2) wording to be confirmed by counsel].
- The name must not falsely imply or suggest, or be such as would reasonably mislead a person to believe incorrectly, that the company is part of or associated with any other person or entity [citation: TBC].
- The name must not include any word, expression, or symbol that constitutes propaganda for war, incitement of imminent violence, or advocacy of hatred [citation: TBC]. (Constitutional s.16(2) cross-reference.)
- The name must not be the same as a registered TM / well-known TM under the Trade Marks Act 194 of 1993 (cross-link to Gate 1).

### 1.2 s.12 — reservation procedure

Companies Act s.12 provides for the reservation of a name for a defined period (six months, extendable) [citation: TBC — reservation period]. Counsel + Owen execute the reservation filing using CIPC's online name-reservation service.

### 1.3 Reg.8 — objection regime

Companies Regulations 2011 Reg.8 provides for objections to a registered or reserved name on grounds of similarity, false implication, or non-compliance with s.11. The CIPC may motu proprio raise an objection, or a third party may lodge an objection within the prescribed period.

**Three-letter short-name objection risk.** CIPC has a documented practice of treating very short company names (especially three-letter only) as inviting objection on the basis of insufficient distinctiveness or potential confusion [citation: TBC — confirm by counsel against CIPC practice notes]. Mitigation:

- File with a longer formal-style suffix (see § 2 below).
- Include a written name-justification rider with the reservation explaining the brand strategy, the s.22 posture (cross-link Gate 2), and the cleared-TM posture (cross-link Gate 1).

## 2. Legal-suffix options — `[CEO judgement deferred]`

The Companies Act + Reg.8 + Banks Act § 22 + market-practice combination requires a formal legal suffix on the corporate name. Counsel must file under one of the three options below; Marc must indicate the preference.

### Option A — "Hoz Bank Limited" (DEFAULT RECOMMENDATION)

- The licensed trading entity is registered as `Hoz Bank Limited` — a single public company holding the SARB banking licence.
- Banks Act § 22 alignment is direct (the corporate name says "Bank" so the Banks Act § 22 implication-of-banking-activity test is anchored on the licence).
- Reg.8 short-name objection risk is mitigated by the formal "Bank Limited" suffix; the filing is no longer a three-letter name but a four-word formal name.
- Legal-entity tree under this option remains a single node — `BANK-ZA-001 = Hoz Bank Limited`.

### Option B — "Hoz Limited"

- The licensed trading entity is registered as `Hoz Limited` (Banks Act trading style — i.e. bank-status — apparent through the licence rather than through the corporate name).
- **Not recommended.** Banks Act § 22 generally requires a regulated banking entity to make its bank-status apparent in the corporate name where the entity is a bank; using "Limited" alone risks the s.22 implication test on the *understated* leg (the inverse of the unauthorised-implication leg — that an authorised bank conceals its bank-status from counterparties / the public).
- Counsel must opine; this brief does not pre-empt the opinion.

### Option C — "Hoz Banking Group Limited" (holdco) + "Hoz Bank Limited" (subsidiary)

- A holdco / subsidiary structure: the parent `Hoz Banking Group Limited` holds the shares of the subsidiary `Hoz Bank Limited`, which is the licensed entity.
- Appropriate if the licence-application strategy elects a holdco / subsidiary structure for capital, regulatory, or tax reasons.
- The legal-entity tree under this option grows to two nodes (`HOLDCO-ZA-001 = Hoz Banking Group Limited`; `BANK-ZA-001 = Hoz Bank Limited` as a wholly-owned subsidiary).
- Increases CIPC filing scope (two reservations + two registrations) and Companies Act compliance scope (consolidated MOI considerations, intra-group agreements, etc.).
- Holdco/subsidiary decision is open per the legal-entity-tree note in `Owner Inbox/2026-05-08_imani_legal-readiness.md`.
