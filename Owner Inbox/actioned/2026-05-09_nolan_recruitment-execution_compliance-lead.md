## 1. Engagement summary

### 1.1 Triple-hat scope

The seat carries three statutory designations on a single human:

- **MLRO** under FIC Act 38 of 2001 s.43A — single point of contact for STRs / SARs / CTRs / TPRs to the FIC's gO!AML system; named MLRO judgement on FIC s.29 reportability; tipping-off discipline under FIC s.29(3).
- **FIC Compliance Officer** under FIC Act s.43B — accountable for the bank's RMCP end-to-end (FIC ss.42–42A); RMCP custodian; CDD/EDD discipline (FIC ss.21–28A); sanctions-screening operational accountability; FIC liaison.
- **POPIA Information Officer** under POPIA Act 4 of 2013 s.56 — designation lodgment with the Information Regulator; PAIA requests under PAIA Act 2 of 2000; POPIA s.22 breach notifications; lawful-processing register oversight; cross-border-transfer governance under POPIA s.72.

Multi-entity application: same human carries MLRO + FIC CO for **both** Hoz Bank Limited and Hoz Securities Limited (each is an accountable institution under FIC Schedule 1 — items 6 and 13 respectively). Same human carries IO for both entities (Iris's per-entity scoping). The group entity (Hoz Group Limited) is not an accountable institution.

### 1.2 Workload-concentration verdict (Mira+Zara Q1)

The triple-hat is **workload-concentration**, not statutory-defect. Each statutory merger is contemplated by the underlying regimes; SA Tier-2 / boutique-bank practice routinely co-locates these designations. The licence-application narrative defends the triple-hat on three grounds reproduced from the role-brief §1.2: engineering substrate carries the operational load (Mira + Iris); alternates split across distinct seats (Deputy IO = CoSec; MLRO-alternate = AC-Chair NED); institutional-only counterparty book keeps FIC volume manageable.

### 1.3 Pre-licence fractional ramp to full-time at lodgment

The role-brief §8 cadence allows the seat to onboard **fractional / advisory** during the pre-licence build phase (RMCP authoring, IO designation drafting, sanctions-list-cadence sign-off), ramping to **full-time** at SARB licence-application lodgment. The offer letter codifies the ramp explicitly (§9 below). Compensation §8 is framed against the full-time TGV with a pro-rata pre-lodgment slice.

### 1.4 What this plan delivers

This document is the engagement instrument Nolan uses to:

1. Sign the retained-search mandate with the lead firm (Catalysis) and co-retained firm (Hoppe Carlton).
2. Brief the firms against a single shared candidate-class definition and screening rubric.

## 3. Candidate-class definition

This section operationalises PAX role-brief §3 (required experience) into the screening criteria the search firms use for first-pass.

### 3.1 Hard floor (must-have, no exceptions)

- **8+ years** senior compliance experience at a SA-regulated financial institution.
- **At least 3 years** carrying MLRO or FIC Compliance Officer designation in name (not deputised; not advisory).
- **FIC-accredited MLRO recognition** demonstrable from CISA standing or direct FIC supervisory record.
- **POPIA s.56 IO designation experience** at a SA financial-services institution (lodgment with the Information Regulator on file); PAIA-manual authorship experience.
- **CDD/EDD on institutional / counterparty book** (banks, FIs, asset managers, hedge funds, broker-dealers).
- **South African resident** for at least the prior 36 months; SA citizenship preferred but not required if residency satisfies SARB PA expectation on regulator-accessibility.
- **Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper** clean record (no prior fit-and-proper findings; no prior SARB supervisory action against the candidate).
- **FIC fit-and-proper** clean record (no prior FIC fit-and-proper findings; no prior FIC supervisory action against the candidate).
- **POPIA**: no prior breach-notification failure under candidate's accountability.
- **Substantive independence from the CEO** — no family / close personal / financial relationship with Marc; no concurrent compliance-officer seat at a competitor SA bank or asset manager (CEO open-question §9.4 governs case-by-case treatment).

### 3.2 Strong-fit shape (heavily preferred)

- **CISA professional qualification** — Compliance Practitioner / Compliance Professional / Certified Compliance Specialist; current member in good standing.
- **FAIS RE1** for Key Individuals / Compliance Officers (allows the candidate to also carry the FAIS s.17 Compliance Officer designation at Hoz Securities Limited under the FSP-licence slice).
- **gO!AML submission track record** — has personally submitted STRs / SARs / CTRs / TPRs.
- **Sanctions-screening operational fluency** — UNSC, OFAC, EU, UK HMT, DTI POCDATARA lists; false-positive-reduction discipline; novel-designation interpretation track record.
- **POPIA-breach incident-management** — has managed at least one notifiable breach end-to-end (s.22 notification + data-subject notification + post-incident remediation); demonstrably learned from it (no second notifiable failure).
- **Regulator-engagement track record** — FIC supervisory engagement (RMCP inspection, STR-volume queries, AML thematic reviews); Information Regulator engagement (POPIA assessments, breach-notification follow-up); ideally also FSCA conduct-supervision engagement (TCF, FAIS thematic reviews).
- **Institutional / wholesale-dealer counterparty-book experience** — broker-dealer compliance, asset-management compliance, or institutional-banking compliance; relevance to the Hoz Securities Limited slice.
- **Transaction-monitoring rule design** — engineer-translatable understanding; track record of working alongside in-house engineering on monitoring infrastructure.

### 3.3 Nice-to-have

- **B-BBEE positive contribution** — explicit transformation-discipline lens; not a tick-box; contributes to scoring weight.
- **Joint Standard 1 of 2024 (Cybersecurity & Cyber Resilience) fluency** — useful at the POPIA s.19–22 / Senna-Rashida interface.
- **Trading-bank exposure** — STR / sanctions-risk on counterparty book at an institutional global-markets posture.
- **Technology fluency** — operating model is autonomous-by-default (Principle 7); the seat oversees Mira's screening engine + transaction-monitoring pipeline + Iris's POPIA programme substrate; fluency with engineered-controls is a strong working-style signal.

### 3.4 Hard fails (auto-reject)

PAX role-brief §4.6 + Nolan recruitment scope §7:

- Concurrent compliance-officer seat at a competitor SA bank (subject to CEO open-question §9.4 case-by-case posture).
- Prior FIC fit-and-proper findings or supervisory action against the candidate.
- Prior POPIA breach-notification failure under candidate's accountability without demonstrable post-incident remediation and clean record since.
- Cannot demonstrate substantive independence from the CEO.
- Family / close personal relationship with Marc (CEO).
- Active AML investigation history without resolved, documented closure.
- Active dispute with the FIC, Information Regulator, FSCA, or SARB PA.
- Failure of any reference check at the regulator-facing engagement layer (§7).

## 5. Screening rubric

Nolan and the two firms apply a single shared rubric. The rubric scores §3 against weighted criteria.

### 5.1 Scoring matrix

| Criterion | Weight | Scale 1–5 | Hard fail trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MLRO designation depth** (years carrying MLRO designation; FIC supervisory record; gO!AML track record) | 20% | 1 = no direct MLRO designation; 5 = 5+ years MLRO with strong FIC supervisory record | < 2 (auto-reject) |
| **FIC CO / RMCP authority** (RMCP custodian experience; FIC ss.42–42A fluency; CDD/EDD discipline) | 15% | 1 = no RMCP authority; 5 = RMCP custodian at Tier-2 bank, demonstrable supervisory engagement | < 2 (auto-reject) |
| **POPIA IO depth** (s.56 designation; lodgment with IR; PAIA-manual authorship; s.22 breach-notification track record) | 20% | 1 = no IO experience; 5 = substantive IO at FS institution incl. breach-notification track record | < 2 (auto-reject) |
| **Sanctions screening** (operational fluency on UNSC/OFAC/EU/UK HMT/POCDATARA; false-positive reduction; novel-designation interpretation) | 10% | 1 = no operational sanctions experience; 5 = sanctions-team-lead at SA-regulated FI | < 2 (preference; not auto-reject if §3.2 strong elsewhere) |
| **CDD/EDD on institutional / counterparty book** (institutional clients, FIs, asset managers, broker-dealers) | 10% | 1 = retail-only experience; 5 = wholesale-only expert | < 3 (preference for institutional-only Hoz posture) |
| **Regulator-engagement track record** (FIC + Information Regulator + FSCA) | 10% | 1 = none; 5 = multi-regulator engagement with clean record | (preference, not auto-reject) |
| **Independence + fit-and-proper** (CEO independence; Banks Act / FIC / POPIA fit-and-proper credibility) | 10% | 1 = potential independence concern; 5 = unambiguous independence + clean fit-and-proper across all three regimes | < 4 (auto-reject) |
| **Working style** (engineering-aligned; comfortable with engineered-controls oversight; second-line independence posture) | 5% | 1 = paper-process orientation; 5 = engineer-translatable rule design + technology fluency | (preference) |

### 5.2 Hard fails (any one rejects the candidate)

- Independence-from-CEO concern unresolved at first-screen.
- Active or unresolved AML investigation (regardless of merit).
- Active or unresolved POPIA breach-notification failure under candidate's accountability.
- Active or unresolved fit-and-proper finding under FIC, FAIS, Banks Act, or POPIA regimes.
- Concurrent competitor-bank seat without case-by-case CEO endorsement under PAX role-brief §9.4.
- Family / close personal / financial relationship with Marc (CEO).

### 5.3 Pipeline gates

- **Catalysis / Hoppe Carlton first-cut** — ≥ 60% weighted score on §5.1 to enter Nolan-screen.
- **Nolan screen → panel shortlist** — ≥ 70% weighted score, no hard-fail, plus pipeline-shape constraint (at least 2 triple-hat-experienced candidates per PAX role-brief §7.2).
- **Panel pass → CEO interview** — first-round + second-round weighted average ≥ 75%, no hard-fail surfaced in interview.

### 9.1 POPIA Reg. 4 deputy-IO interlock

POPIA Regulations Relating to the Protection of Personal Information, 2018, Reg. 4 contemplates deputy Information Officers. Per Owen final composition §5 + Mira+Zara §4.2, the **CoSec** (Priority-2 sister hire — separate recruitment-execution scope) is the explicit **deputy IO** under POPIA Reg. 4. | Element | Principal IO (Compliance Lead, this hire) | Deputy IO (CoSec, sister hire) |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory designation | Designated under POPIA s.56 by CEO; lodged with Information Regulator | Designated as Deputy IO under POPIA Reg. 4(1) by CEO; lodged with Information Regulator alongside the principal designation |
| Day-to-day authority | Full POPIA s.56 authority | Stand-in authority for any POPIA s.56 function during principal's absence; permanent authority on PAIA-manual maintenance and PAIA-request administrative handling |

| Reporting line | Reports through Zara (CCO governance) on the governance line; Devon (COO) administrative | CoSec reports to the Board (Chair) functionally, to Marc (CEO) administratively; the deputy-IO function is handled within the CoSec mandate per Owen final composition |
