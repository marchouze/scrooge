## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-340-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`, reusable across all BA returns.
- **BA 340 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba340-contract.json` (183 cells), loaded + validated via the return-contract registry (`return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA340")`). Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA340` (provenance — an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements (Phase C batch 4 — holding-class-keyed)

BA 340 cells genuinely require attributes a **banking-book equity holding** product must carry. These attach to the **future, unapproved banking-book equity product** (`prd:bank:banking-book:equity-holding`), never the live FX product:

| Attribute | Required? | Why | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `equityHoldingClass` | **required:true** on the holding-class-defining rows (listed / unlisted / speculative-unlisted / other equity holdings) | the holding-class row cannot place an equity holding without its class (which selects the applicable simple risk weight) | Basel CRE (banking-book equity); SARB reg 31 |
| `equityRiskWeightMethod` | **required:true** on the method rows (simple-risk-weight method / internal-models approach) | the method row selects the BA 340 method sub-form | Basel CRE; SARB reg 31 |

**This is the binding the NPA gate enforces** (`recon:npa-return-data-obligation-integrity`): a future banking-book equity product cannot reach approval unless it captures — or tracks as a deferred gap — its owed `required:true` `equityHoldingClass` and `equityRiskWeightMethod` attributes. The live FX product feeds NO `required:true` product-id cell and is therefore **not** blocked.

> **Honest substrate gap (Engineering Charter cmd 3 + 5).** The product-scope declaration schema does not yet model banking-book equity-holding attributes; until extended, a future banking-book equity product is approvable only by tracking each owed attribute as a `ProductDeferredGap`. Tracked, not hidden.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 183 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA340")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the cell's figure from the **`ba340-equity-risk-banking-book-fold`** projection: the equity-risk fold over the banking-book equity holdings (listed / unlisted / speculative) plus the simple-risk-weight / IMA capital computation. Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` / `formula` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate expression (`derivation.expression`) over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication:** every BA 340 cell is **`licence-day-data`** — the banking-book equity holdings that fill the cells require real equity investments held in the banking book the bank-in-formation does not hold pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every cell. The fold exists as substrate; the product-attribute `dataRequirements` bind **now** so the future banking-book equity product is gated — the build-now value.

> **Gate coherence.** A `sourced` cell never carries a `required:true` product-attribute on an unapproved future product (that would fail `recon:ba-return-cell-contract`); the generator forces such a cell to `licence-day-data`.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-340-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 340 instance MUST validate against `BA340.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 340 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; the `Monetary1000` value wrappers; the holding-class rows; the simple-risk-weight / IMA method columns; the exposure-value / risk-weighted-exposure / capital-requirement column triple).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA340.zip → BA340.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 340 alongside the other authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-340-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Risk-weighted exposure ties to capital** | `capital requirement = 8% × risk-weighted exposure` per holding-class row (BCBS CRE; Reg 31); the reported capital-requirement column equals `0.08 ×` the risk-weighted-exposure column. A divergence is a control failure escalated to the CRO. |
| **Holding-class roll-up integrity** | The total equity exposure / RWA / capital equals the sum of the listed / unlisted / speculative / other holding-class rows per `derivation.expression`; a subtotal that does not equal its constituents is a control failure. |
| **Simple-risk-weight integrity** | Each holding class's risk-weighted exposure equals `exposure value × the prescribed simple risk weight` for its class (and the 1,5 scaling factor where the "Exposure value — in respect of which the 1,5 scaling factor applies" column is populated). |
| **Capital integration (Reg 38)** | The banking-book equity-risk capital feeds the BA 700 capital-adequacy aggregate via Reg 38; the BA 340 charge that flows into BA 700 equals the BA 340 reported charge. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba340-equity-risk-banking-book-fold` source datum; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
