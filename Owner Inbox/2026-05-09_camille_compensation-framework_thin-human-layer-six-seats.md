> **Authority and scope.** This is the consolidated CFO-framed compensation envelope for the six licence-day human hires authorised under **D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM** (CEO decision record: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-08_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-thin-human-layer-minimum.md`) and codified in **Owen (Company Secretary, governance, agent)**'s final composition paper (`Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_owen_thin-human-layer-composition-final.md`). Each of the six **PAX (Role researcher)** role-briefs (`Owner Inbox/2026-05-10_pax_role-brief_*.md`) explicitly defers compensation specifics to me; this paper is that consolidated framing. The decision Marc (CEO) makes here is approve-as-drafted vs revise vs add-bound — not six separate decisions.

## 1. Compensation philosophy

Three lines, no embellishment:

1. **Institutional, regulated-bank market reference rates.** Bands are anchored to disclosed compensation at SA Tier-2 banks (Sasfin, Bidvest Bank, Capitec at comparable scale, Investec subsidiary structures, African Bank), with secondary cross-reference to SA insurance-group and asset-manager comparators where the SA Tier-2 bank disclosure pool is thin. We do not pay below regulated-bank market — the named-regulatory-accountability of each seat (PA fit-and-proper, FIC s.43A/B designation, POPIA s.56 designation) lifts the floor above generalist corporate equivalents. We do not pay materially above lower-quartile of the comparator class — the bank's R300m capital target and pre-revenue posture do not justify it.
2. **Build-phase fractional-engagement discounts where applicable.** The Company Secretary seat is structurally fractional pre-licence-day (the agenda is thin until Board and committees stand up); the Independent Chair and the two NEDs are inherently fractional roles (NED packages are fixed-fee, not full-time salaries); the human CRO and the triple-hatted Compliance Lead are full-time from day one (PA fit-and-proper continuity expectations bind). Fractional discounts are explicit, not hidden, and the **licence-day uplift trigger** is documented per seat.
3. **Full-time licence-day uplift triggers documented per role.** Where a seat starts fractional and converts to full-time at an event, the trigger event is named (typically: SARB pre-application meeting; licence-application lodgment; commencement-of-trading) and the indicative uplift magnitude is stated. This avoids re-negotiation friction at the inflection point and gives Nolan a concrete offer-letter clause to draft.

> **Independence-preservation overlay (NEDs only).** Per King IV Principle 7 and BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015) Principle 3, NED compensation excludes equity, options, LTI participation, and any variable performance-linked component. NED fees are pure cash retainers + meeting fees + committee fees. This is non-negotiable across NED #1 (Chair), NED #2, and NED #3.

> **Risk-adjusted-deferral overlay (CRO + Compliance Lead).** Per BCBS Principles for Sound Compensation Practices and PA fit-and-proper expectations on second-line officers, the human CRO and the Compliance Lead carry meaningful fixed-component weighting (≥85% of TGV fixed) with the residual variable component subject to multi-year deferral and clawback on subsequent fit-and-proper findings. Independence-preservation overrides revenue-link incentives — variable for these two seats is **not** linked to AML / FIC / POPIA / risk-event throughput.

The 5.8% capital-absorption is **at the lower end** of what a SARB licence-application file could credibly defend — pushing materially below would erode candidate-attraction below the level a credible licence-application Board-composition section requires.
