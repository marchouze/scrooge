## 1. Why this seat exists

Companies Act s.94(2) requires three NEDs on the AC. Two of three (Independent Chair and NED #2) must satisfy the strict s.94 substantive-independence test. The third (this seat) must be non-executive but **not** strictly independent under s.94 — Owen+Imani §3 item 4 specifies "need not be independent in the strict s.94 sense, but must be non-executive". This widens the candidate pool while still satisfying statute.

The seat is the right shape for a NED who brings deep banking-regulatory or technical experience but may not pass the strict-independence test (e.g., prior bank-group secondment within the look-back window; advisory relationship with the bank's group within the de-minimis-exceeded threshold).

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- Non-executive (cannot be a current bank executive or a current executive of any group entity).
- 15+ years senior leadership experience.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible.
- Skills-matrix complementarity with Independent Chair + NED #2.
- South African resident.

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior NED at a SARB-regulated bank.
- Deep banking-regulatory experience (former PA / SARB official within the post-employment cooling-off window; former senior bank executive).
- Technology / cyber depth (Joint Standard 2 of 2024 oversight).
- Treasury / liquidity expertise (the bank's funding posture is institutional wholesale).
- Trading-bank exposure.
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- Transformation expertise (Companies Reg. 43 S&E).

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Currently an executive of the bank or its group.
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings.
- Material business relationship with the bank's group above the de-minimis threshold *and* prospective independence cannot be demonstrated.
