## 1. Statement of the open question

**Does the bank's institutional-only / wholesale posture require an FSP licence under the Financial Advisory and Intermediary Services Act 37 of 2002 (FAIS) at all?**

Two postures are possible. They are not equally evidenced; the conservative posture has been adopted interim. The question is whether the conservative posture is the right *steady-state* posture or whether a documented carve-out is the right answer.

This is genuinely a counsel call. The Mira-Zara conduct-side confirmation paper (Q4) characterised this as "engineer-not-counsel input only" [citation: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_mira-zara_concentration-risk-conduct-confirmation.md` Q4]. PAX's role-research output feeds the counsel engagement at the licence-application gate; it does *not* replace it.

## 2. The two possible postures

### 2.1 Posture A — FSP licence pursued (conservative; current default)

**Premise.** The bank intermediates in financial products as defined in FAIS s.1 even when its counterparties are institutional / professional. Therefore an FSP licence is required, a Key Individual is named (Marc-interim, Saskia-steady-state), and FAIS-record-keeping + General Code of Conduct apply across customer interactions.

**Trigger.** This is the trigger if any of the following holds:
- Counsel reads "advice" or "intermediary service" under FAIS s.1 to capture the bank's institutional-counterparty interactions.
- The FSCA's published interpretive guidance on FAIS s.45 exemptions for institutional / professional counterparties is *narrower* than the bank's planned product set.
- The bank's product set evolves to include any retail-facing product (e.g., professional-counterparty derivatives that get re-purposed for sub-institutional flow).

**Implications.**
- KI seat needed (Marc-interim → Saskia steady-state per `ORG-FAIS-KI` [citation: TBC — Mira's parallel obligations-register update; row currently a gap]).
- FAIS Categories I and II likely sufficient for institutional product set — see Mira's reading in the conduct-side confirmation paper Q4.
- Substrate footprint: FAIS-record-keeping (advice records, suitability assessments, fee disclosures), General Code of Conduct application, complaint-handling under TCF outcomes (see TCF substrate plan v0).
- Procedure footprint: `Procedures/by-policy/fais-advice-record-capture.md` (already in `/Procedures/by-policy/`) is binding.

### 2.2 Posture B — Documented carve-out under FAIS s.45 / Subordinate Legislation

**Premise.** The bank's institutional-only / wholesale posture means its counterparty interactions fall *outside* the definition of "advice" or "intermediary service" under FAIS s.1, or fall within an FSCA-published exemption under s.45 / FAIS Subordinate Legislation for institutional / professional counterparties. Therefore no FSP licence is required; no KI is named; FAIS-record-keeping + General Code of Conduct do *not* apply.

**Trigger.** This is the trigger if all of the following hold:
- Counsel reads the institutional / professional counterparty exemption to cover the bank's planned product set.
- The bank commits to documented eligibility-screening of every counterparty as institutional / professional, with no retail flow.
- A documented FSCA pre-application engagement confirms the carve-out (or at minimum does not contradict it).

**Implications.**
- No KI seat needed; the human-roster math drops by one named accountability (Marc-interim retention is unaffected at licence-day; the question is steady-state).
- Substrate footprint reduced: no FAIS-record-keeping substrate; complaint-handling still applies via TCF residual reach (see TCF substrate plan v0 §3); General Code of Conduct does not apply.
- Procedure footprint: `Procedures/by-policy/fais-advice-record-capture.md` becomes a *conditional* procedure — applicable only if the carve-out narrows or the product set expands.
- Risk: the carve-out is a binding posture the FSCA may revisit. The bank is exposed to a re-licensing event if the FSCA changes its interpretation or if the bank's product set drifts.

### 2.3 Risk of getting the posture wrong

| Direction | If wrong | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Adopted A but B was correct | Carries an unnecessary licence + KI seat + substrate; over-investment, no regulatory exposure. | Cost is over-investment + Marc / Saskia bandwidth on a regime that doesn't apply. |
| Adopted B but A was correct | Operates without a required licence; FAIS s.7 contravention; potentially criminal under FAIS s.36; reputational + regulatory enforcement. | Cost is severe. Asymmetric. |

The asymmetry is why the conservative posture (A) has been adopted interim. The question is whether the asymmetry justifies *steady-state* commitment to A, or whether B is so well-evidenced (under counsel + FSCA pre-application engagement) that A becomes over-investment.

## 3. Recommendation — PAX research dispatch

The decision pack noted `agent:Saskia` would dispatch PAX on this question [citation: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-08_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-thin-human-layer-minimum.md`, follow-on routes]. This paper writes the conduct-side framing PAX must answer; Saskia owns the dispatch mechanic.

**Recommended PAX research dispatch scope (for Saskia to dispatch):**

1. **Statutory scope.** Read FAIS s.1 (definitions of "financial product", "advice", "intermediary service") against the bank's planned institutional-only product set (JSE bonds / equities, OTC IRD, institutional-only). Identify which interactions plausibly fall in or out of FAIS scope.

2. **Exemption scope.** Read FAIS s.45 + FAIS Subordinate Legislation + FSCA Conduct Standards on institutional / professional counterparty exemptions. Identify the exemption tests, their evidentiary requirements (counterparty-categorisation criteria, documentation expectations, ongoing-eligibility monitoring).

3. **FSCA precedent.** Survey published FSCA enforcement actions, Conduct Standards, and Interpretation Letters on FSP-licence-necessity for wholesale / institutional dealers. Identify the FSCA's revealed posture (strict / permissive) on the carve-out.

4. **Comparable institutions.** Identify SA-licensed wholesale dealers / institutional-only banks and their FSP posture (licensed / not licensed; carve-out documented or not). This is a market-intelligence input, not authoritative — but it informs the risk view.

5. **Counsel-engagement framing.** Synthesise the above into a one-page brief that frames the counsel call: what the counsel must answer, what evidence the counsel needs, what carve-out language a counsel sign-off would require. This brief feeds Imani's external-counsel-licence-application engagement [citation: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_imani_external-counsel-licence-application.md`].

6. **Steady-state KI candidate fit.** Confirm Saskia's fit-and-proper readiness for the FAIS Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017 — Categories I and II. (PAX-not-counsel input; Sade's AgentOps fit-and-proper-analogue is engineering-side, separate.)

PAX's research output feeds:
- Counsel engagement at the licence-application gate (Imani routes).
- The licence-application narrative (Owen + Imani own the human-roster section; Mira + Zara own the conduct + AML/CFT section).
- A subsequent CEO-decision pack that resolves D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY.

## 4. Interim posture — unchanged by this paper

**Marc as FAIS KI interim. Saskia steady-state.** [citation: D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM, decision record 2026-05-08; Mira's gap-closure URN `ORG-FAIS-KI`, currently a gap row owned by Mira's parallel obligations-register update.]

The Mira-Zara confirmation paper Q4 characterised CEO=KI as "sustainable through licence-day but **not** sustainable as steady state — the FAIS KI workload at commencement-of-trading exceeds CEO bandwidth" [citation: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_mira-zara_concentration-risk-conduct-confirmation.md` Q4]. This paper does not re-litigate that conclusion.

**Two propositions are simultaneously true:**

1. *Until counsel + PAX research resolve D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY,* the bank operates as if Posture A holds (FSP licence pursued; Marc-interim KI; FAIS-record-keeping substrate planned).
2. *The steady-state posture is open* — it may be Posture A with Saskia as KI, or it may be Posture B with no KI seat. The decision is gated on counsel.

The interim posture is therefore: build the FAIS substrate (record-keeping, advice-records, suitability-checks, complaint-handling) on a Posture-A assumption, while keeping the substrate's binding *commencement-of-trading* not licence-day [citation: `project_rules_bind_at_commencement.md`]. If Posture B is adopted at the resolution of D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY, the substrate is staged but not commenced.
