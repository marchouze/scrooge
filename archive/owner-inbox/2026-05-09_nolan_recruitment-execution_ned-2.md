## 3. Candidate-class definition

The candidate clears three concentric rings of qualification: (a) the King IV substantive-independence baseline (binding gate); (b) AC-member eligibility on the financial-reporting axis (mandatory regardless of skills-axis); (c) the technology / cyber skills-axis depth (working assumption).

### 3.1 Ring 1 — King IV substantive-independence test (BINDING GATE)

Companies Act s.94(4)–(6) read with King IV Principle 7 and BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015) Principle 3. Hard fail on any of the following:

- Material business relationship with Hoz Group Limited, Hoz Bank Limited, or Hoz Securities Limited within the three years immediately preceding appointment.
- Material financial relationship beyond the directorship itself.
- Close family relationship with any executive of the bank, its group, or any material counterparty.
- Prior employment or remuneration by the bank or its group within three years of appointment that would compromise independence judged on a fact-based reading.
- Common directorship at a competitor SA bank or material counterparty.
- Any relationship that compromises substantive independence judged on King IV's fact-based reading (catch-all).

The substantive-independence file is part of the licence-application package; the AC's separate s.94(7) report to shareholders will recur to it annually. This is the load-bearing structural element of how the bank answers SARB Prudential Authority's NED-independence-depth challenge under Owen §4.3.

### 3.2 Ring 2 — AC-member eligibility (mandatory regardless of skills-axis)

The candidate must demonstrate **auditing / financial-reporting fluency to a level satisfying the JSE-listed AC eligibility analogues**, even though the bank is not yet listed. Rationale: the SARB Prudential Authority bias mirrors the JSE-listed AC-member standard; a candidate whose financial-reporting fluency falls below that bar will draw a fit-and-proper challenge from the PA at licence-application.

Concrete tests:

- Demonstrable ability to read and challenge IFRS financial statements at a Tier-2 SA bank's level of complexity (sub-ledger consolidation, ECL, IFRS 9 hedge accounting, IAS 21 multi-currency translation, IFRS 17 if applicable).
- Familiarity with SARB BA-return reporting cadence and content (BA returns are the regulatory equivalent of audited statements; AC oversight of BA-return integrity is a PA expectation).
- Familiarity with audit-firm engagement mechanics — engagement-letter review, materiality discussion, management-letter oversight, year-end auditor-engagement-quality-review challenge.
- Familiarity with internal-audit oversight — annual audit-plan approval, internal-audit-finding tracking, Chief Audit Executive performance review.

**Note on the conditional contingent must-have:** if the Independent Chair search lands on a candidate **without** Companies Act s.94(4)(b) financial-expertise (CA(SA) / former bank-CFO / financial-reporting-fluency-strong), then NED #2 must carry the s.94(4)(b) **financial-expertise designation** itself — and the technology / cyber axis falls to NED #3. The search funnel is built to handle either outcome; the axis-lock is the trigger event.

### 3.3 Ring 3 — Technology / cyber skills-axis depth (working assumption per the Chair shortlist trigger)

Per PAX §3.3 + §7.2 (60% funnel weight). Candidate shape:

- Former CIO / CTO / CISO of a major SA bank (Tier-1 or strong Tier-2) — typical seat: 5–10 years in the C-suite role plus prior senior-IT-leadership progression; **or**
- Former technology-NED or technology-committee-Chair at a JSE-listed financial — Standard Bank, FirstRand, Absa, Nedbank, Investec, Old Mutual, Sanlam, Discovery, Capitec; **or**
- Former senior-partner technology / cyber assurance practice at a Big-Four firm — provided no current audit-firm-engagement conflict with Hoz; **or**
- Former senior official at SARB or FSCA on the technology / cyber-resilience side — subject to the post-employment cooling-off window (typically 12 months).

**Substitutable second-best axes (per PAX §3.4)**, in priority order:

1. **Risk-modelling / model-risk depth.** Closely related to the technology axis (model-risk overlaps with AI-agent-risk discipline). Funnel weight 20%.
2. **Transformation / B-BBEE / S&E expertise.** Companies Reg. 43 S&E-pillar coverage. Funnel weight 15%.
3. **Treasury / liquidity / institutional-wholesale depth.** Usually a NED #3 axis but available as fallback. Funnel weight 5%.

### 3.4 Hard exclusions (auto-disqualifiers, codified for the screening rubric)

- Currently-active audit-firm partner where the firm could be the bank's external auditor.
- Currently-serving executive of any Tier-1 SA bank or material Hoz counterparty.
- Family or close personal relationship with Marc (CEO), with the Compliance Lead (seat #5), with the human Chief Risk Officer (seat #6), or with the Independent Chair (seat #1) once appointed.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings under Banks Act, Companies Act, FAIS, or FIC.
- LinkedIn-only-sourced candidates lacking diligence depth for strict-independence testing (PAX §7.1 channel exclusion).

## 5. Screening rubric

Each candidate surfaced through any channel is scored against a structured matrix before being presented to Marc (CEO). The matrix is structured into three layers (matching the three rings of §3): independence (binding gate); AC-eligibility (mandatory); skills-axis depth (working assumption).

### 5.1 Hard fails (auto-disqualifier — candidate does not progress)

- Independence-test breach on any of the King IV substantive-independence sub-tests in §3.1.
- Banks Act s.60 / Companies Act ss.69, 71 / FAIS / FIC fit-and-proper breach (see §3.4 hard exclusions).
- AC-eligibility gap on the auditing / financial-reporting axis below the JSE-listed AC analogue baseline (§3.2).
- Cannot meet SA-resident-only constraint (cross-cutting Q2 default).
- Cannot commit to the Board + AC + S&E + standing-attendee Risk Committee meeting cadence (estimated 18–25 meeting-days per year).
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank or material Hoz counterparty.

A single hard fail removes the candidate. No exceptions without a fresh CEO decision.

### 5.2 Scoring matrix (used to rank candidates who clear the hard fails)

Each axis scored 0–5; weighted sum produces a composite score. Working assumption is technology / cyber as primary axis; the matrix re-weights once the Chair-shortlist trigger fires (§1.3).

| Axis | Weight (working assumption) | Scoring guidance |
|---|---|---|
| **Substantive independence** (depth and defensibility, beyond pass/fail) | 20% | 5 = no relationships of any kind to Hoz / counterparties / Marc's network; 3 = clean pass with minor cross-Board overlap that does not reach materiality; 1 = clean pass on letter but fact-based reading is closer to the line |
| **AC-member eligibility** (auditing / financial-reporting fluency) | 20% | 5 = CA(SA) + bank-CFO experience or equivalent depth; 3 = senior-finance-leadership exposure to bank-grade IFRS but not CA(SA); 1 = generalist-NED financial-reporting fluency at the JSE-listed-AC-analogue minimum |
| **Technology / cyber skills-axis depth** (primary working-assumption axis) | 30% | 5 = former CIO/CTO/CISO of Tier-1 SA bank with prior NED experience; 4 = former CIO/CTO/CISO of Tier-1 SA bank without NED experience but with credible director-prep (IoDSA DDP or equivalent); 3 = senior technology-committee-NED at JSE-listed financial; 2 = Big-Four technology-assurance partner without current Hoz-conflict; 1 = SARB / FSCA technology-supervisor alumnus past cooling-off |
| **Bank-Board experience or director-prep** | 10% | 5 = 5+ years prior NED at a SARB-regulated bank; 3 = 3+ years NED at a JSE-listed financial; 1 = senior-executive but no NED experience, with IoDSA DDP commitment |
| **Joint Standard 1 of 2024 fluency** (cyber-resilience board oversight) | 10% | 5 = current or recent technology-committee Chair or senior cyber-policy practitioner with JS 1/2024 §6 / §8 reading; 3 = familiar with the Standard's substance through senior-tech role; 1 = generalist technology-NED who would bring up to JS 1/2024 fluency through induction |
| **Skills-matrix complementarity to the Chair** (locked once Chair shortlist narrows) | 10% | 5 = orthogonal to the Chair's profile, fills gaps the Chair does not naturally cover; 3 = complementary but with some overlap; 1 = duplicates the Chair's profile (auto-rebalances the skills-axis once locked) |

A composite score of **≥ 3.5** lifts a candidate to the long-list (5–8 candidates). A composite of **≥ 4.0** plus clean substantive-independence diligence lifts to the shortlist (3–5 candidates).

### 5.3 Diligence sequence per shortlisted candidate

Once a candidate is shortlisted, the diligence package runs concurrently per PAX §7.5:

1. **Substantive-independence diligence** — prior-employment-history review (3 years); related-party-transactions review against the Hoz counterparty list; family-relationships disclosure; concurrent-directorship review; SARB / FSCA / JSE engagement-history review.
2. **Multi-entity fit-and-proper triangulation** — Banks Act s.60 + PA fit-and-proper (Hoz Bank Limited); Companies Act ss.69, 71 (Hoz Group Limited base layer); FAIS Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017 + JSE Membership Rules + JSE Listings Requirements (Hoz Securities Limited). Captured in the Sade-owned PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01 substrate template (PR #69, merged) — extended per Owen §7A.2 and §7A.5 to capture entity-specific declarations covering all three regimes inside one director-file per human.
3. **Reference-check pack** — three professional references; one prior-Board-Chair reference (where applicable); one regulator reference (where applicable, e.g., former SARB/FSCA supervisor of the candidate's prior role).
4. **Technical-fit assessment on the technology / cyber axis** — Rashida (Chief Information Security Officer, governance) leads a structured 60-minute technical interview against a CISO-track / technology-NED competency framework (subset of the Joint Standard 1 of 2024 §6–§7 and BCBS Operational Risk principles).

The diligence package outputs a dossier per shortlisted candidate, lodged with Owen for the Board-Reserved-Matter resolution-substrate.

### 7.2 Director-fee structure (standard SA Tier-2-bank NED with AC-member premium)

Per PAX §6.2, the structure is:

- **Base annual NED fee** — fixed, paid in cash quarterly. No equity (per Companies Act + King IV independence preservation). No variable.

- **Travel and expense reimbursement** — actuals, not allowance, to preserve independence.
- **No Chair premium** — applies only to seat #1.
- **Multi-entity adjustment** — single-roster comp with explicit per-entity allocation in management accounts (Hoz Group / Hoz Bank / Hoz Securities) for arm's-length pricing under IFRS 10 + IAS 24. Camille decides the final treatment per the parent decision record.

### 7.3 Indicative ceiling guidance for Camille's pass

PAX §6.3 recommends the comp clear the SA Tier-2-bank AC-member benchmark plus a small premium (≤15% over benchmark base) to (a) clear the AI-driven-institution candidate-pool risk premium and (b) clear the strict-independence-on-technology-axis candidate-pool narrowness premium. The premium is bounded so the comp does not itself become a substantive-independence concern under King IV.

Camille's framing pass produces the final per-seat package shape (cash + per-meeting fee + AC premium) within the comparator bands and the ≤15% ceiling guidance.
