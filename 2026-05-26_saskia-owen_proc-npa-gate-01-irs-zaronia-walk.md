# PROC-NPA-GATE-01 — FIRST ACTIVATION for vanilla ZAR IRS fixed-vs-ZARONIA (internal pre-licence test)

> **Posture.** First formal PROC-NPA-GATE-01 activation for `prd:bank:ird:vanilla-zar-fix-zaronia`. This is the bank's only derivatives product at M3 phase. The IRS substrate — confirmation generator, MTM engine, coupon-settlement events, and ISDA documentation — landed in PR #820. This is the highest-complexity product in the approved set.
>
> **Product description.** Vanilla interest rate swap: fixed receiver/payer vs ZARONIA-compounded-in-arrears floating leg, ZAR-denominated, bilateral OTC, governed by ISDA 2002 Master Agreement + CSA + ZARONIA protocol adherence. No initial exchange of principal; net cash settlement on each coupon date.
>
> **ZARONIA note.** ZARONIA (South African Overnight Index Average) is the SARB-administered risk-free rate replacing JIBAR. Compounded-in-arrears ZARONIA requires a backward-looking observation period and a 5-business-day lockout; these mechanics are wired in the IRS confirmation generator (PR #820). No JIBAR legacy swaps are in scope for this product; ZARONIA-only.
>
> **Scope.** Internal pre-licence test only — synthetic IRS trades with simulated ZARONIA curve; no real bilateral ISDA counterparty; no real CSA variation-margin call; no real coupon settlement via correspondent. This product requires the most rigorous review of the five walks filed today.
