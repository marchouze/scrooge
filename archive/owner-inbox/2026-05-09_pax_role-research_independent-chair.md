## 1. Mandate boundary

**In scope (governance seat — single human, three hats):**

- **Independent Chair of the Board** — chairs Board meetings; sets the agenda jointly with the CEO and CoSec; signs Board resolutions; carries fiduciary duties under Companies Act s.76 [citation: Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.66; King IV Principle 7 (Chair independence)].
- **Audit Committee Chair** — chairs the AC; signs AC reports to the Board; is the first point of escalation for the external auditor on contentious findings; co-signs the AC's annual statement on financial-reporting integrity [citation: Companies Act s.94; Companies Reg. 43; King IV Principle 8].
- **Social & Ethics Committee independent NED** — sits on the S&E committee under the Companies Act / Companies Reg. 43; in a 3-NED Board the S&E composition is bounded — at least one independent NED is required per Reg. 43 [citation: Companies Act s.72(4); Companies Regulations 2011 Reg. 43].
- **MLRO-alternate** under Mira+Zara §4.2 — designated MLRO-alternate per FIC published guidance on accountable-institution governance [citation: TBC pending Mira register row `ORG-FC-MLRO-ALTERNATE`]. AC oversight already touches AML/CFT controls; the AC Chair is the natural alternate.
- **Banks Act fit-and-proper director** — designated fit-and-proper non-executive director under Banks Act s.60 [citation: Banks Act 94 of 1990 s.60; PA Fit-and-Proper Standards `ORG-HR-11`].

**Out of scope (governance seat does not do):**

- **Executive accountability** — the Chair does not hold executive function; CEO (Marc) holds that. King IV is explicit that combining Chair + CEO is not preferred.
- **Day-to-day risk function** — the human CRO (seat #6) holds.
- **AML/CFT primary accountability** — the Compliance Lead (seat #5) holds; the AC Chair is alternate, not principal.
- **CoSec functions** — the human CoSec (seat #4) holds; the Chair works closely with the CoSec but does not duplicate.

**Strongly preferred:**

- **AI-substrate-aware Board** — willingness to govern an AI-run bank where the executive layer is autonomous agents (Principle 7) is a non-trivial cultural-fit screen. - **SARB-Prudential-Authority engagement track record** — a Chair who has previously engaged the PA on a fit-and-proper or supervisory matter is materially more credible at licence-application gate.
- **S&E mandate emerging-market practice** — Social & Ethics Committee competence in an emerging-market context (B-BBEE, financial inclusion, transformation in financial services) is the SA-specific S&E discipline. King IV S&E Committee guidance.
- **Tier-2 / applicant-bank board experience** — having chaired or sat on a Board through a SARB licence application or material change-in-control filing is a strong signal.

**Disqualifying:**

- Unresolved supervisory finding personally addressed at the candidate.

## 3. Talent-landscape map

The SA NED talent pool, narrowed to bank-credible Chairs, is **structurally thin**. Public-record observable: ~10–12 active SARB-licensed-bank Chairs at any given time, plus comparable size at insurers and at JSE Top-40 financial-services. Rotational lifecycle (King IV recommends 9-year tenure cap) means ~1–2 Chair seats turn over per year SA-wide.

### 3.1 SA primary market — currently-licensed-bank NED Chairs and AC Chairs

|---|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 SA bank Board Chairs (Standard Bank, FirstRand, Absa, Nedbank) | 4 individuals at any time | Highest credibility; PA pre-application engagement reads strongly | Concurrent-Chair-of-licensed-bank disqualifying; rotated Chairs (former Chair, ≥2 years out) are the realistic pool |

### 3.5 Headline talent-pool size

Conservatively, the SA-resident pool of viable Chair candidates (King IV independent; bank-credible; not concurrent-Chair-of-licensed-bank) is **~15–25 individuals**. ## 6. Conflict-of-interest map

**Cross-directorship with any direct competitor.** The bank's institutional-only wholesale dealer franchise positions it against tier-1 SA bank global-markets desks (Standard Bank Global Markets, FirstRand RMB Global Markets, Absa CIB, Nedbank CIB). Any candidate with a current NED seat at a direct competitor is materially conflicted on competitive-strategy matters; King IV practice would expect resignation or recusal.

**Audit-firm overlap.** The Chair is the AC Chair; the AC supervises the external auditor relationship. Any candidate with a recent (≤5 years) employment at the bank's chosen external audit firm is materially conflicted; PA fit-and-proper would not necessarily disqualify, but the Independence Regulator's expectations and the AC's regulator-facing credibility would be compromised.

**MLRO-alternate / AC-Chair conflict.** Owen+Imani §3B + Mira+Zara §4.2 explicitly contemplated this overlap and accepted it on the basis that AC oversight already touches AML/CFT. The conflicts register entry must declare the overlap explicitly per Mira+Zara §4.3. PAX recommendation: at appointment, the engagement letter explicitly addresses the alternate-MLRO duty as part of the AC-Chair scope, with FIC-compliance training as part of induction.

**Major-shareholder representation.** If the bank's eventual shareholder base includes institutional investors with their own NED nominees, the Independent Chair cannot be one of them — King IV independence is binding.

**Prior-employer restraint clauses.** Tier-1 / tier-2 bank Chair contracts may include 12-month restraints. Head-hunter due diligence at long-list stage should map.
