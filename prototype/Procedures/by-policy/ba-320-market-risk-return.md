**Delta on:** [`PROC-FIN-BA-01` — BA Return Generation](ba-return-generation.md). **This procedure does NOT restate** the generic preparer→four-eyes→attest→`BAReturnFiled`→restatement flow; it specifies only what is BA-320-specific. Read PROC-FIN-BA-01 §§3–9 first.

## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-320-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`, reusable across all BA returns.
- **BA 320 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba320-contract.json` (304 cells), loaded + validated via the return-contract registry (`return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA320")`). Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA320` (provenance — an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements (Phase C batch 4 — MODEST, per-trade-derived)

Market risk is largely **per-trade / position-derived** — the SBM sensitivities, the VaR, the position-risk charge are computed from individual trade positions, not from a handful of product-static attributes. So the product-static set BA 320 marks is small, and it attaches to a **future, unapproved trading product** (`prd:bank:trading:market-risk-position`), never the live FX product:

| Attribute | Required? | Why | Clause |
|---|---|---|---|
| `riskClass` | **required:true** on the NON-FX risk-class-defining rows (interest-rate, equity, commodity) | the row that defines a risk-class dimension cannot place an exposure without the position's class, and there is no live IR/equity/commodity book pre-licence-day | Basel MAR risk-class taxonomy; SARB reg 28(3)(a)–(d) |
| `tradingBookDesignation` | required:false | the Trading/Banking column slices the aggregate; the value still folds | Basel MAR trading-book boundary; SARB reg 28(3) |
| `positionSide` | required:false | the Long/Short position columns net within a risk class; sliced, not populate-or-die | Basel MAR; SARB reg 28(3) |

The **FX** risk class is **derived per-trade by the live market-risk fold** (the FX adapter reads real FX positions and classifies them), so the FX-risk-class aggregate cells carry `riskClass` **required:false** and remain `sourced` — they are not forced licence-day. **This is the binding the NPA gate enforces** (`recon:npa-return-data-obligation-integrity`): a future trading product cannot reach approval unless it captures — or tracks as a deferred gap — its owed `required:true` `riskClass` attribute. The live FX product (`prd:bank:fx:otc-vanilla`) feeds NO `required:true` product-id cell and is therefore **not** blocked.

> **Honest substrate gap (Engineering Charter cmd 3 + 5).** The product-scope declaration schema does not yet model trading-book risk-class attributes; until it is extended (a sibling workstream is generalising it), a future trading product is approvable only by tracking each owed attribute as a `ProductDeferredGap`. Tracked, not hidden.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 304 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA320")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the cell's figure from the **`ba320-market-risk-fold`** projection: the **`ba-320-market-risk.ts`** standardised-position-risk engine (IR specific + general, equity, FX incl. gold, commodity per BCBS MAR / Reg 28) over the **FX adapter** (`ba-320-fx-adapter.ts`, reading real FX positions), the IR / bond / equity event adapters, and the **VaR engine** (`platform/market-risk/var-engine.ts`). Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` / `formula` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate expression (`derivation.expression`) over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication (honest per-cell split):**
   - **`sourced`** — the **FX-risk-class aggregate cells** (foreign-exchange-and-gold risk, FX option sub-charges, the FX-and-gold capital-requirement column) have **live substrate** (the FX adapter + the market-risk fold + the VaR engine). Their source genuinely exists; values fold from real FX positions / to an honest 0 (`sourced` = "source exists", not "data present").
   - **`licence-day-data`** — every other BA 320 cell (IR / equity / commodity risk classes, the general-risk and other-desk sub-forms) needs a real trading book at scale the bank-in-formation does not run pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every such cell. The product-attribute `dataRequirements` bind **now** so the future trading product is gated — the build-now value.

> **Gate coherence.** A `sourced` cell never carries a `required:true` product-attribute on an unapproved future product (that would fail `recon:ba-return-cell-contract`); the generator forces such a cell to `licence-day-data`.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-320-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 320 instance MUST validate against `BA320.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 320 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; the `Monetary1000` value wrappers; the GeneralRisk / FXGoldRisk / OtherDesks sub-form structure).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA320.zip → BA320.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 320 alongside the other authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-320-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Market-risk charge ties to RWA** | `market-RWA = 12.5 × market-risk capital` (the reciprocal of the 8% minimum; BCBS MAR `urn:reg:bcbs:mar:20.1`; Reg 28). The reported market-RWA cell equals `12.5 ×` the summed per-risk-class capital charges from the same fold; a divergence is a control failure escalated to the CRO. |
| **Risk-class roll-up integrity** | The total standardised position-risk charge equals the sum of the IR (specific + general), equity, FX-and-gold, and commodity charges per `derivation.expression`; a subtotal that does not equal its constituents is a control failure. |
| **FX NOP charge integrity** | The FX risk charge equals the prescribed net-open-position treatment (e.g. 8% × max(Σlongs, Σshorts) under the simplified-SA shorthand) over the FX adapter's per-currency positions; Reg 28(3)(c) (FX in either book). A divergence between the fold's FX charge and the FX adapter's NOP is a control failure. |
| **Capital integration (Reg 38)** | The market-risk capital charge feeds the BA 700 capital-adequacy aggregate via Reg 38 (integrating Reg 28/29); the BA 320 charge that flows into BA 700 equals the BA 320 reported charge. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba320-market-risk-fold` source datum; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
