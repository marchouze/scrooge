# CEO decision — D-BRAND-DESIGN-HIRE (approved)

**Decision ID:** `D-BRAND-DESIGN-HIRE`
**Author of the proposal:** PAX (role researcher)
**Decided by:** Marc (CEO)
**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Source proposal:** `Owner Inbox/2026-05-07_pax_brand-design-role-brief.md`
**Event:** `CeoDecision` appended to the event store with citations `GOV-FRAMEWORK-CEO-RESERVED` and `COMPANIES-ACT-71-2008` (per `Procedures/by-policy/ceo-decision-review.md`).

## What was decided

**Approved as drafted.** The bank hires a brand & design standing agent. Nolan formalises the persona spec under `Team/_agent-spec-template.md` (canonical agent-spec form per the persona-default-rule set today); the new agent's first run produces the inaugural brand package.

## Persona-name selection

PAX's brief surfaced three candidate names (Linnea, Otto, Yume) in preference order. Persona-name selection from CEO-approved candidates is in Nolan's decision-scope per `Team/Nolan.md`, so Nolan picks from the three. Default to PAX's first recommendation (**Linnea** — Scandinavian-design lineage, modern-professional, pairs with the engineering-led peer set) unless Nolan flags a reason to override.

## Scope locked

- **Reporting line:** under Devon (COO) per the PAX brief recommendation, with hard sign-offs from Zara (CCO) on customer-facing materials, Owen (CoSec) on regulator-facing materials, Imani on naming legal review (Banks Act s.22, Trade Marks Act, CIPC, .za domain), Iris on POPIA s.69 direct-marketing copy where applicable.
- **Scope in:** visual identity (logo, wordmark, palette, typography, iconography, illustration, motion); naming including the bank itself; voice and tone; templates (pitch books, board packs, regulator submissions, customer-facing); brand reviews on Owner-Inbox deliverables.
- **Scope out:** marketing strategy, channel buying, PR/comms, product UX design, substantive content authoring (substance comes from the canonical authoring location per Principle 6).

## Inaugural deliverable

The new agent's first run produces a single brand package in `/Owner Inbox/`:

- **Name longlist for the bank** — 8–12 candidates, each with regulatory analysis (Banks Act s.22 restricted-use check), CIPC + Trade Marks Act pre-clearance signal, .za domain availability signal, linguistic / cross-language check across SA's 11 official languages, market-positioning fit (institutional global-markets register).
- **Three logo concepts** — described in production-grade detail (form, weight, geometric basis, mark vs wordmark relationship); rendered as SVG specs the next pass can produce.
- **Colour palette** — primary, secondary, neutral, with hex codes, contrast-ratio analysis (WCAG 2.2 AA baseline per the PAX brief), and rationale tied to the institutional positioning.
- **Typography system** — open-licensed font selection with type scale, weights, hierarchy.
- **Voice & tone principles** — the bank's external register (distinct from each agent's internal in-voice register).
- **Overall feel** — a one-pager synthesising the above into the bank's design philosophy.

## Sequencing

The hire and the inaugural deliverable produce in this session. The actual choice of bank-name from the longlist becomes a follow-on CEO decision (to be tagged `D-BANK-NAME-SELECTION` when the longlist lands). Logo execution as final SVG and the brand-asset library lift to a separate substrate workstream.
