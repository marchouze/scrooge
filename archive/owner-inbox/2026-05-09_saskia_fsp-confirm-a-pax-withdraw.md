## 5. How this changes the transition cadence

The transition cadence accelerates materially. Under the original two-gate trigger, the seat transfer was held on **the slower of two parallel paths**:

- (a) Counsel's licence-application-gate ratification of FSP-licensing scope — a path Saskia does not control and which sits under Imani (Legal-as-code engineer)'s external-counsel-engagement schedule (Option C: deferred-with-precondition, fired at the SARB pre-application meeting date or licence-application lodgment date, whichever comes first).
- (b) Saskia's own FAIS-KI fit-and-proper file completion — a path Saskia controls (with Owen, Zara, and now Sade's substrate dependencies).

With Gate (a) now closed by CEO decision, the transition gates on Gate (b) alone. **The seat transfer fires when Saskia's fit-and-proper file is complete** — typically at or shortly before commencement-of-trading, but no longer held on counsel's parallel timeline. Counsel's licence-application-gate ratification of Posture-A scope still runs on Imani's Option C cadence but is no longer the binding handover trigger.

In practice this means:

1. **Human-Saskia recruitment** (Nolan's scope) can now drive the transition timing directly. Nolan's recruitment cadence is unchanged (second-wave hire alongside human-Niko at commencement-of-trading per the thin-human-layer composition), but the recruitment is now on the critical path for the seat transfer rather than running in parallel with a counsel question.
2. **The fit-and-proper file template substrate** (Sade's AgentOps follow-on per the D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY confirm-A decision record — `Procedures/by-policy/fais-ki-fit-and-proper.md` or similar, capturing the five Determination-of-Fit-and-Proper-Requirements-2017 dimensions: honesty / integrity, competence, operational ability, financial soundness, oversight) is now load-bearing for the transition. Sade's v0 substrate-stub becomes a planned dependency in Gate (b).
3. **Counsel's parallel ratification** (Imani's narrowed scope per `claude/zara-imani-fsp-confirm-a-scope-updates`) runs on its own clock — Posture-A application-bundle ratification at the licence-application gate, plus the institutional-only counterparty-eligibility evidence requirements scoping. Counsel does not evaluate Posture B and does not re-litigate the binary A-vs-B question.
