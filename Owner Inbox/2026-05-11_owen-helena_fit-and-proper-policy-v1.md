## 1. Policy statement

Hoz Bank (the "Bank") requires that every person who holds a designated seat — whether as a director, executive officer, key individual, or named regulatory officer — meets fit-and-proper (F&P) standards at the time of appointment and **continuously** thereafter. F&P is not a one-off gate; it is a standing condition of holding the seat.

The Bank's fit-and-proper framework rests on three pillars:

1. **Competence** — the person possesses the qualifications, experience, and continuing professional development (CPD) necessary to discharge the seat's responsibilities effectively.
2. **Integrity** — the person has a demonstrated record of honesty, probity, and sound judgment; no disqualifying criminal, civil, or regulatory history.
3. **Solvency** — the person is personally financially sound; no insolvency event, court-ordered debt arrangement, or material conflict arising from personal financial distress.

Every designated seat has a written F&P file maintained in the Records Management Substrate. Every appointment is preceded by a documented F&P assessment; every year of tenure is attested by an annual declaration; every material change is notified within five business days.

Failure of any pillar — at appointment, on continuous assessment, or on re-assessment — is a removal event. Concealment of a disqualifying fact is itself disqualifying and may constitute a criminal offence.

## 3. Fit-and-proper criteria — three pillars

### 3.1 Competence

Competence must be demonstrably matched to the seat. A person is competent for the seat if they can show:

**3.1.1 Qualifications**

- Academic qualifications at a level commensurate with the seat's regulatory and technical complexity.
- Professional qualifications and memberships where the seat's obligations require them (e.g., CA(SA) for CFO; Chartered Risk professional designation or equivalent for CRO; FAIS RE1 + RE5 for FAIS Key Individual).
- Qualifications verified against an independent source (university registry; professional body register) before appointment.

**3.1.2 Experience**

- A sufficient track record of progressively responsible roles in the relevant domain.
- At a minimum: experience in a regulated financial-institution environment of equivalent size and complexity.
- Seat-specific experience minimums are set out in the Per-Seat F&P Matrix in §4 below.
- Experience gaps that would impair the person's ability to discharge the seat's statutory obligations disqualify the candidate; a "compensating measure" (e.g., a more experienced deputy) does not cure a core-qualification gap.

**3.1.3 Continuing professional development (CPD)**

- Each designated person maintains a CPD log.
- Minimum CPD obligations are as per the person's professional body or, where no professional body sets a minimum, as per the Bank's per-seat minimum in §4.
- CPD is attested annually in the annual declaration (see §7).
- Failure to maintain CPD triggers a re-assessment under §7.3.

### 3.2 Integrity

Integrity is assessed holistically; the absence of a disqualifying event is necessary but not sufficient. The Bank assesses:

**3.2.1 Probity and honesty**

- No conviction for fraud, theft, dishonesty, corruption, market manipulation, insider trading, or financial crime in any jurisdiction.
- No civil judgment arising from dishonesty, breach of fiduciary duty, or market-abuse conduct.
- No finding of fact in any regulatory proceeding (PA, FSCA, FIC, foreign equivalent) that the person acted dishonestly.
- No conviction under PRECCA or the FIC Act for a relevant offence.

**3.2.2 Regulatory history**

- No current or historic suspension, prohibition, or restriction issued by the PA, FSCA, FIC, JSE, or any equivalent foreign regulator, unless fully spent and declared.
- Any regulatory finding — even an informal supervisory concern that did not proceed to enforcement — must be declared; the Bank assesses materiality and decides.

**3.2.3 Business conduct**

- No prior role as director or officer of an entity that was placed into curatorship, judicial management, or liquidation in circumstances where the person's conduct contributed to the institution's failure.
- No prior role as director or officer censured under the Companies Act (ss.162–163) or comparable foreign law.
- No outstanding judgment debts arising from commercial disputes that indicate a pattern of dishonesty rather than isolated business failure.

**3.2.4 Personal character**

- Referees' assessments must support appointment; material adverse references are escalated to the NomCo before appointment.
- The Bank reserves the right to request a personal interview with the NomCo or Board for any Group A seat.

### 3.3 Solvency

Financial soundness protects the institution against the risk that personal financial distress creates susceptibility to fraud, corruption, or regulatory evasion.

**3.3.1 Insolvency**

- No sequestration or personal insolvency (voluntary or compulsory) in the past 10 years, unless rehabilitated and the rehabilitation disclosed.
- Confirmed via court-records search (CIPC; High Court registries) at appointment.

**3.3.2 Debt distress**

- No current debt-review order or administration order under the NCA.
- No court-ordered debt arrangement (sequestration, administration, debt review) active at appointment.

**3.3.3 Personal financial exposure**

- No personal exposure to a counterparty, loan, or financial instrument that could create an impermissible conflict with the Bank's institutional interests.
- Disclosed in the annual declaration; material exposures assessed by Owen (governance) against the Conflicts of Interest Policy.

**3.3.4 MRT additional solvency check**

- For MRT-designated persons, a current personal statement of assets and liabilities is filed with the annual declaration and held in the F&P file.

## 5. FAIS Key Individuals — supplementary provisions

### 5.1 KI designation under FAIS

Where the Bank holds or applies for an FSP licence under the FAIS Act, it must designate one or more Key Individuals (KIs). The KI is the person accountable to the FSCA for the FSP's compliance with the Act and the General Code of Conduct for Authorised FSPs.

At licence-day, Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance) is the FAIS-KI-elect under CEO decision `D-FSP-LICENCE-NECESSITY` (`Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-fsp-licence-necessity-confirm-a.md`). If the Bank designates additional KIs (e.g., for additional FSP-licence categories), each additional KI is subject to the full five-dimension framework below.

### 5.2 Five-dimension F&P framework

Per the FSCA Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017, every KI must hold an approved F&P file across five dimensions. The procedure for assembling, verifying, and maintaining the file is `PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01`. Each dimension produces a typed evidence event and the composite approval event (`FaisKiFitAndProperFileApproved`) is the canonical substrate record.

| Dimension | Content | Typed event (planned — Atlas v1 substrate) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Honesty + integrity | Criminal-record certificate; civil-judgments declaration; regulator-prior-action search; insolvency search | `BackgroundCheckCompleted` |
| 2 — Competence | Qualifications (verified); experience record; FAIS RE1 + RE5 certificates; CPD log | `CompetenceAttestationFiled` |
| 3 — Operational ability | Seat-specific responsibility map; capacity assessment; operational-independence confirmation | `OperationalAbilityAssessed` |
| 4 — Financial soundness | Personal solvency declaration; insolvency confirmation (10-year lookback); debt-arrangement confirmation | `FinancialSoundnessAttested` |
| 5 — Oversight | Reporting-line confirmation; conflict-of-interest disclosure; ongoing-monitoring covenant | `OversightStructureRecorded` |
| **Composite** | All five dimensions present, none retracted | `FaisKiFitAndProperFileApproved` |

### 5.3 RE examinations

The Regulatory Examinations are a statutory requirement under the FSCA Determination:

- **RE5** — required for all Representatives and for KIs who are also Representatives in Category I FSPs.
- **RE1** — required for Key Individuals in Category I and Category II FSPs.

The RE results must be verified directly against the FSCA's examination registry before the file is approved. No RE substitution is permitted. Where an FSP-licence category requires a specific product-specific examination (e.g., Derivatives; Bonds), the applicable examination must also be confirmed.

### 5.4 CPD obligations for KIs

KIs must maintain ongoing CPD per the FSCA Determination's requirements for the relevant licence categories. The Bank's CPD tracking operates via the Records Management Substrate; CPD-point feeds trigger the ongoing-monitoring cycle in PROC-FAIS-KI-FAP-01 §4. Lapse in CPD invalidates the `CompetenceAttestationFiled` event and triggers a re-assessment.

## 7. Ongoing obligations

### 7.1 Annual attestation

Every designated person completes an **Annual F&P Attestation** within 30 days of each anniversary of their appointment (or at the Bank's financial-year-end, whichever the Board determines). The attestation covers:

1. Confirmation that the person continues to meet all three pillars (competence, integrity, solvency) as at the attestation date.
2. Updated Declaration of Interests (full refresh, not delta-only).
3. CPD log for the preceding 12 months.
4. Declaration of any proceedings, charges, investigations, or regulatory actions commenced or resolved since the prior attestation.
5. Declaration of any personal insolvency, debt-review, or court-order since the prior attestation.

Owen (Company Secretary, governance) collects attestations; failures or concerns are escalated to the NomCo.

### 7.2 Material-change reporting

A designated person must notify Owen (Company Secretary, governance) within **five business days** of:

- Any criminal charge, investigation, or conviction.
- Any civil judgment or regulatory finding against the person.
- Any insolvency event, sequestration, debt-review application, or court order.
- Any regulatory action (suspension, prohibition, restriction, censure) in any jurisdiction.
- Any change in the person's outside directorships, advisory roles, or material shareholdings that could give rise to a conflict.
- Any change in the person's professional qualifications (revocation, suspension, lapse).
- Any material change to the factual basis of the pre-appointment declarations.

For FAIS KI: the notification window to the FSCA is as per the Determination [citation: TBC — exact interval; Imani (Legal-as-code engineer) + external counsel to confirm at licence-application gate].

For the MLRO/FIC Compliance Officer: changes in the designated-officer detail must be notified to the FIC within 30 days (FIC Act s.43(3)).

### 7.3 Re-assessment triggers

A formal re-assessment of a person's F&P status (outside the annual cycle) is triggered by:

- A material-change notification under §7.2.
- A finding by Vera (Internal-audit / continuous-assurance engineer) or any third party that a disqualifying fact may not have been disclosed.
- Any external development (e.g., a court judgment; regulatory enforcement action) that comes to Owen's attention.
- Any PA, FSCA, or FIC inquiry directed at the person.

The re-assessment follows the same three-pillar framework as the pre-appointment assessment. The NomCo is informed and convenes if the re-assessment result is adverse or inconclusive.

## 8. Breach — disqualification, PA notification, acting provisions

### 8.1 Disqualification events

A person is disqualified from holding a designated seat upon:

1. **Conviction** for fraud, theft, dishonesty, corruption, market manipulation, insider trading, or any offence involving dishonesty, in any jurisdiction.
2. **Sequestration, insolvency, or court-ordered debt arrangement** that is active and undisclosed at appointment, or that arises during tenure without immediate notification.
3. **Regulatory suspension, prohibition, or restriction** issued by the PA, FSCA, FIC, JSE, or equivalent foreign regulator.
4. **Finding of fact** in any regulatory or court proceeding that the person acted dishonestly, breached fiduciary duties, or engaged in market abuse.
5. **Concealment** of any disqualifying fact at the pre-appointment stage or in any subsequent annual attestation.
6. **Material misrepresentation** in any document submitted to the PA, FSCA, FIC, or the Bank in connection with the F&P process.
7. **Disqualification under the Companies Act ss.162–163** (delinquency or probationary director).
8. **Failure of the competence pillar** where the deficiency is material and cannot be remediated within a reasonable period (defined by the NomCo on a case-by-case basis; generally not exceeding 6 months for a remediable CPD gap; immediately for a fundamental-qualification gap).

### 8.2 PA notification requirements

Where a disqualification event occurs for a Group A designated person or a Board director, Owen (Company Secretary, governance) notifies the PA:

- **Immediate notification (within 1 business day):** criminal arrest or charge; regulatory suspension or prohibition; insolvency event.
- **5-business-day notification:** all other disqualification events under §8.1.
- **Notification form:** per PA's prescribed form or, in the absence of a prescribed form, a written notification from the CEO setting out the person's name, role, the nature of the event, and the date Owen became aware.

The PA notification is a **separate** obligation from any internal escalation. Failure to notify the PA is itself a regulatory breach.

For FAIS KI: FSCA notification within the applicable window under the Determination (Imani + counsel to confirm exact period). FSCA may revoke FSP-licence categories or the licence itself if a KI ceases to be fit-and-proper and no replacement is made within the FSCA's prescribed remediation period.

For MLRO / FIC Compliance Officer: FIC notification within 30 days of the disqualification event (FIC Act s.43(3)).

### 8.3 Acting-appointment provisions

When a Group A seat or FAIS KI falls vacant (by resignation, disqualification, incapacity, or death), the following acting provisions apply:

**CEO:** Marc-as-CEO is the founding and current CEO. If Marc is unable to act, the Board appoints an acting CEO from within the eligible directors; the PA is notified. Marc's absence in the build phase is managed through the documented escalation protocol in the Governance Framework.

**CFO / CRO:** The Board appoints an acting CFO or CRO from among existing executive team members who meet the minimum competence requirements for the seat. An acting appointment may not exceed 6 months without PA engagement regarding the substantive appointment process. The PA is notified within 5 business days of the vacancy.

**MLRO / FIC Compliance Officer:** The FIC Act permits the accountable institution to appoint an acting MLRO on the terms and timing the FIC prescribes. Owen notifies the FIC of the acting appointment and initiation of substantive appointment process within 30 days.

**Information Officer:** A temporary IO may be designated under POPIA. The acting person must be registered with the Information Regulator within the applicable window.

**FAIS KI:** The FAIS Act requires that an FSP has a fit-and-proper KI at all times. Where a KI vacancy arises, the FSP must notify the FSCA and commence the appointment of a replacement KI within the FSCA's prescribed remediation period. Conduct of FSP-regulated activities during a KI vacancy must be assessed carefully by Zara (Chief Compliance Officer, governance) against the risk of unauthorised-regulated-activity liability under FAIS s.8.

### 8.4 Removal process

Where a disqualification event arises for an incumbent, the removal process follows this sequence:

```
Step 1 — Event identified (Owen, or Vera via recon harness, or external notification)
  ↓
Step 2 — Owen notifies CEO + NomCo within 24 hours; regulatory-authority notification (§8.2)
  ↓
Step 3 — NomCo convenes within 3 business days; reviews the facts; determines acting-appointment status
  ↓
Step 4 — Person notified of disqualification event and given opportunity to respond (except where
          immediate suspension is required by the disqualification event's nature)
  ↓
Step 5 — NomCo makes recommendation to the Board; Board resolves on removal
  ↓
Step 6 — Removal effected in compliance with employment law (Imani, Legal-as-code engineer, and
          external employment counsel where required); CoSec manages the CIPC / PA / FSCA / FIC filings
  ↓
Step 7 — Acting-appointment activated (§8.3); substantive recruitment commenced (Nolan, Recruiter)
```

Imani (Legal-as-code engineer) and external employment counsel manage the employment-law dimension; removal on F&P grounds is distinct from disciplinary dismissal but they may overlap. The employment-law slice activates at licence-day per the operating model.
