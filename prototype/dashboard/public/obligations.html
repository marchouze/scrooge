Obligations register

Mira's curated graph of statutory and policy obligations the bank discharges. Every row maps an external requirement (regulator, standard, internal RAS) to the policy that fulfils it — the operational expression of CLAUDE.md Principle 2 (every action traces to a source) and Principle 6 (the upward chain Regulation OR Bank Objective → Policy → Procedure → System Capability). Source: Regulations/_obligations-register.md, parsed live by prototype/dashboard/obligations-view.ts.

Principle 6 — "no orphans" rule

Obligations whose Reg → Policy → Procedure → System-Capability chain is incomplete at the register layer. Resolving these is part of Mira's regulatory-change-management cadence and Vera's continuous-controls assurance programme. Substrate gap surfaced separately: the obligations register currently does not author the procedure and system-capability columns directly — they are inferred from the linked-policy column. v1 of this page should ingest Procedures/_index.md and the runtime handler-metadata to close that loop.
