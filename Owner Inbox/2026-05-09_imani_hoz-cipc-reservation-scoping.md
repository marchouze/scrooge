## 1. Scope — Companies Act s.11 / s.12 / Reg.8 surface

### 1.1 s.11 — name requirements

Companies Act s.11 sets out the criteria a name must meet to be acceptable for a company. The substantive requirements counsel must confirm "Hoz" + chosen suffix satisfies are (without being exhaustive):

- The name must not be the same as or confusingly similar to the name of another existing company / external company / close corporation / cooperative / business trust [citation: TBC — exact s.11(2) wording to be confirmed by counsel].
- The name must not falsely imply or suggest, or be such as would reasonably mislead a person to believe incorrectly, that the company is part of or associated with any other person or entity [citation: TBC].
- The name must not include any word, expression, or symbol that constitutes propaganda for war, incitement of imminent violence, or advocacy of hatred [citation: TBC]. (Constitutional s.16(2) cross-reference.)
- The name must not be the same as a registered TM / well-known TM under the Trade Marks Act 194 of 1993 (cross-link to Gate 1).

### 1.2 s.12 — reservation procedure

Companies Act s.12 provides for the reservation of a name for a defined period (six months, extendable) [citation: TBC — reservation period]. Counsel + Owen execute the reservation filing using CIPC's online name-reservation service.

### 1.3 Reg.8 — objection regime

Companies Regulations 2011 Reg.8 provides for objections to a registered or reserved name on grounds of similarity, false implication, or non-compliance with s.11. The CIPC may motu proprio raise an objection, or a third party may lodge an objection within the prescribed period.

**Three-letter short-name objection risk.** CIPC has a documented practice of treating very short company names (especially three-letter only) as inviting objection on the basis of insufficient distinctiveness or potential confusion [citation: TBC — confirm by counsel against CIPC practice notes]. Mitigation:

- File with a longer formal-style suffix (see § 2 below).
- Include a written name-justification rider with the reservation explaining the brand strategy, the s.22 posture (cross-link Gate 2), and the cleared-TM posture (cross-link Gate 1).

## 6. Filing cadence

Counsel files all three CIPC reservations **in a single batch** at the SARB / FSCA pre-application engagement window. Rationale:

- **Single coherent group story.** The three reservations describe one corporate group (parent + bank + securities subsidiary). Filing them concurrently presents CIPC + the regulators with the full v0 group structure rather than a sequence that could read as a late-stage restructuring.
- **Reduced Reg.8 short-name objection surface.** A coordinated filing with consistent brand-justification riders (Linnea PR #72 v3 supplement) addresses the three-letter `Hoz` distinctiveness question once across all three names rather than three separate times.
- **Aligns with regulator-engagement timing.** SARB Prudential Authority pre-application engagement is the moment the bank's name choice is first notified to the regulator (Gate 2 of the master scoping). Filing the three reservations before that meeting (so the certificates are in counsel's hand) presents the regulator with a settled corporate identity rather than a name-in-flight.

**Cross-reference — SARB pre-application engagement timing.** The exact SARB / FSCA pre-application engagement window is an open question on the CRO-seat-criteria gate (the appointed CRO is the engagement counterparty per `Owner Inbox/2026-05-08_helena_cro-seat-criteria.md`). Counsel-engagement is **deferred** pending that gate per the operating memory `feedback_decisions_workflow.md` decisions-workflow note and per `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_imani_external-counsel-licence-application.md` Option C (defer-with-precondition). This brief specifies the cadence; the wall-clock date attaches when the CRO seat is filled and SARB engagement is scheduled.

**Reservation period — operational note.** Companies Act s.12 reservations run for a fixed period (six months, extendable) [citation: TBC — confirm reservation period + extension procedure]. The single-batch filing means the three reservations expire on the same date; counsel's reservation-extension plan (deliverable #5 in § 4 above) covers all three.
