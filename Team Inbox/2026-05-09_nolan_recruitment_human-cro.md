**From:** Nolan (recruiter) · reports to Devon (COO).

## 1. Why this seat exists

The Joint Standard 2 of 2024 (PA / FSCA Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience) read with BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks (2015) treats CRO operational independence from the CEO as a **structural** expectation at licence-day, not a SARB-precedent challenge that can be answered by a post-licence hiring commitment. Mira+Zara's confirmation paper (`Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_mira-zara_concentration-risk-conduct-confirmation.md` §3.1) reads interim-CRO-as-CEO as a JS-2-2024 finding rather than a flagged risk — the CEO's adoption of that read is the substantive change in D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM relative to Owen+Imani's original five-human draft.

The seat is the **human face** of the bank's prudential second-line. Helena (engineering CRO) and Rohan (risk engineer) carry the engineering load; the human CRO's outputs flow through Helena's substrate. The human seat is what SARB sees.

## 3. Mandate boundary

**In scope:**

- Named accountable person for the bank's risk-management function under Joint Standard 2 of 2024 §6 + Banks Act s.70.
- Chair of the Board Risk Committee (or its proxy — Interim Risk Forum until Board AC and BRC are constituted).
- ICAAP / ILAAP signing authority for SARB Prudential Authority engagement (pre-application gate).
- Risk Appetite Statement & Framework approval (CEO-approved today; CRO-approved at licence-day; Helena's engineering output flows into).
- Model-risk governance final accountability (Helena builds; Nadia validates; the human CRO signs the model-risk policy).
- SARB Prudential Authority engagement on risk matters (capital, liquidity, large exposures, market risk, IRRBB, operational risk, model risk).
- Veto over risk-decisions reserved for second-line (RAS limit breach disposition; production-use of unvalidated models; appetite-line breach dispositions).

**Out of scope:**

- First-line risk-taking — the CEO and the Head of Global Markets (Saskia / future human Markets seat) own first-line risk-taking.
- Compliance / AML-CFT — Zara (CCO) owns. The CCO is a separate seat.
- Internal audit — Thandiwe (CAE) owns; third-line independence is non-negotiable.
- Day-to-day risk engineering — Helena (engineering CRO) and Rohan (risk engineer) own, reporting through this seat.
- Model building — Rohan owns.
- Independent model validation — Nadia owns; reports to this seat with functional independence from Rohan.

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- 10+ years senior risk-management experience in a SARB-regulated bank or comparable-regime regulated bank.
- Demonstrated CRO or Deputy-CRO seat in a regulated bank.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible (no prior fit-and-proper findings; clean SARB engagement history).
- Demonstrated independence from the CEO — performance-assessment structure must be governable by Board committee, not CEO.
- IFRS 9 ECL governance experience (the bank's first capital-model is the credit-risk ECL).
- Joint Standard 2 of 2024 fluent — operational independence, responsible-person designation.
- BCBS Corporate Governance Principles 2015 fluent.
- ICAAP / ILAAP signing experience.
- South African resident (or willing-to-relocate before licence-application lodgment).

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior experience at a small-bank or institution-in-formation (the bank's R300m-target capital posture is small-bank shape).
- Trading-bank experience (institutional global-markets posture per `project_strategic_foundation`).
- AI / agent-substrate fluency — the bank's CRO oversees an engineering CRO (Helena-the-agent) and a model-validation agent (Nadia-the-agent); cultural fit with autonomous-by-default operating model is a strong-fit signal.
- B-BBEE positive contribution (under Companies Reg. 43 S&E read).

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Prior fit-and-proper findings against the candidate at any SA or comparable-regime regulator.
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank or material counterparty.
- Failed reference check on integrity / honesty.
- Cannot satisfy Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper documentation (criminal record; insolvency; tax-compliance).
