> **Purpose.** A consolidated register of every obligation the bank carries under applicable regulation, mapped to where it is fulfilled in the bank's policy stack. The operational expression of Principle 2 (every action traces to a source). Entries are projections over the underlying instrument analyses in this library; reproducible at any as-of date.

## How to read this register

- **ID** — a stable register identifier `ORG-<domain>-<n>` for cross-referencing.
- **Citation** — instrument and section / clause.
- **Requirement** — plain-English statement of what the regulator requires.
- **Fulfilment policy** — where in the bank's policy library this obligation is met (cross-reference to `Owner Inbox/2026-05-06_policy-register.md` and the bundle files).
- **Owner** — accountable seat (typically a governance seat, with the engineering seat in parentheses).
- **Status** — `IN FORCE` (policy approved & live) · `DRAFTING` · `PLANNED` · `PARTIAL` · `N/A-yet` (e.g., FAIS-conditional when no FSP licence held) · `corporate-bind` / `licence-bind` / `commencement-bind` / `conditional-bind` (per `project_rules_bind_at_commencement.md` — when an obligation is registered against a future binding-trigger rather than already in force).

Where multiple regulators converge on the same obligation, it is listed once with all relevant citations.

## Entity scope

> *Added 2026-05-09 (v1.8) under CEO decision `D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0` (approved 2026-05-09; record `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-legal-entity-tree-v0.md` / PR #82). Source: `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_imani-owen_legal-entity-tree-v0.md` (PR #80; the entity-tree v0).*

The bank is structured as a three-entity group:

- **`Hoz Group Limited`** — non-operating parent / holding company. Subject to consolidated supervision under **Banks Act 94 of 1990 § 60+** (parent-of-bank designation, controlling-company / holding-company supervision, group capital adequacy) `[citation: TBC — exact § 60-series paragraph index]`, **Joint Standard 1 of 2024** (group-level cyber-resilience programme) `[citation: TBC]`, **BCBS Corporate Governance Principles for Banks** (group governance) `[citation: TBC — Principle numbers 1–13]`, **IFRS 10 / IFRS 12** (consolidated financial statements + disclosure of interests in other entities), **IAS 24** (related-party disclosures at group level), **Companies Act 71 of 2008** (group-related-party regime).
- **`Hoz Bank Limited`** — regulated banking entity. Subject to the Banks Act, Regulations Relating to Banks, FIC Act 38 of 2001 (as Section 43A accountable institution), POPIA, FSR Act, BCBS Basel III/IV directly.
- **`Hoz Securities Limited`** — FSP / OTC Derivative Provider entity. Subject to FAIS Act 37 of 2002 (FSP licence + Key Individual under Determination of Fit and Proper Requirements 2017), FMA 19 of 2012 + FSCA Conduct Standards 1–3 of 2018 + Joint Standard 2 of 2020 (ODP regime), and to FIC Act as a separately-licensed Section 43A accountable institution.

Every obligation row carries an `entity-scope` field with one of these values:

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `group` | Binds **only** at the group / `Hoz Group Limited` level (e.g. consolidated capital adequacy under Banks Act § 60+; group recovery plan; consolidated financial statements under IFRS 10). |
| `bank` | Binds **only** at `Hoz Bank Limited` (e.g. SARB FinSurv reporting under the Currency and Exchanges Manual; Authorised Dealer obligations; BA returns to the Prudential Authority). |
| `securities` | Binds **only** at `Hoz Securities Limited` (e.g. FAIS Key Individual; FSP advice records / suitability assessments; FSCA conduct-of-business; ODP trade-reporting and margin under JS 2 of 2020). |
| `multi-entity` | Binds at **two or more** entities — the row enumerates which entities and how the obligation manifests at each (e.g. FIC Act Section 43A AML/CFT obligations apply to **both** `Hoz Bank Limited` and `Hoz Securities Limited` as separately-licensed accountable institutions; POPIA Information Officer designation under ss.55–56 owed **per-entity**; Companies Act 71 of 2008 director-duties bind every group entity). |
| `consolidated-supervision` | Binds at the **group level under Banks Act § 60+ AND simultaneously at each entity** — i.e. there is a group-level discharge that aggregates / consolidates the per-entity discharges, plus distinct entity-level discharges (e.g. cyber-resilience programme under Joint Standard 1 of 2024 — group programme + per-entity controls; consolidated ICAAP / ILAAP — group-level capital / liquidity adequacy + bank-entity ICAAP / ILAAP under the bank's standalone obligations). |

**Default for un-classified rows.** Existing rows authored before v1.8 default to `entity-scope: bank` (the bank-entity has historically been the register's implicit subject). The default is **provisional**: full classification of the remaining ~190 rows is the v1 substrate task surfaced in `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_mira_per-entity-register-scoping-v0.md`. The 9 sample rows in v1.8 carry an explicit `entity-scope` field; the rest remain implicit-`bank` until classified.

- **Banks Act 94 of 1990 § 60+** (controlling-company / parent-of-bank consolidated-supervision regime) `[citation: TBC — exact § 60-series sub-section index for parent-of-bank designation, group capital, group large-exposures]`.
