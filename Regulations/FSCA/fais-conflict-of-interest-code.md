## Enabling context — GCC s.3(1)(b) (retrieved)

The General Code already establishes the baseline conflict-of-interest disclosure obligation. Verbatim from GCC s.3(1)(b) (source-docs/fais-general-code-of-conduct.txt):

> the provider must disclose to the client the existence of any personal interest in the relevant service, or of any circumstance which gives rise to an actual or potential conflict of interest in relation to such service, and take all reasonable steps to ensure fair treatment of the client: non-cash incentives offered and/or other indirect consideration payable by another provider, a product supplier or any other person to the provider could be viewed as a potential conflict of interest.

The CoI Code (BN 58 of 2010) provides the detailed implementation framework for this baseline obligation.

## Known structure (pending retrieval)

Based on secondary references and knowledge of the instrument:

1. **Conflict of interest management policy** — every FSP must have a written policy;
2. **Register of financial interests** — FSPs and representatives must maintain a register of financial interests received from or given to third parties;
3. **Disclosure to clients** — actual or potential conflicts must be disclosed before rendering the financial service;
4. **Prohibition on certain arrangements** — certain inducements, incentive schemes, or financial interests that create unmanageable conflicts are prohibited;
5. **Record-keeping** — records of conflicts identified, disclosures made, and management measures taken.

## Application to Hoz Securities Limited

- The institutional OTC trading model creates inherent conflicts (principal vs agent; proprietary desk vs client-flow desk). The CoI Code requires these to be systematically identified, managed, and disclosed.
- The bank's Conflicts of Interest Policy (`ORG-WB-04`) is the fulfilment policy for the CoI Code obligations.
- Information barriers / Chinese walls between the proprietary trading desk and client-advisory functions are the primary structural mitigation.
- Cross-binds to GCC s.3(1)(f) (front-running prohibition) and the trading conflict-management controls in the Trading Policy.
