# Role brief — Head of Global Markets

**Author:** PAX
**Date:** 2026-05-06
**For:** Nolan

## 1. Role title and one-line purpose

**Head of Global Markets** — governance owner of the bank's sales-and-trading franchise; runs market-making, risk-taking, and institutional sales as a stand-alone executive seat reporting directly to the CEO.

## 2. Why this role exists

The CEO has confirmed (2026-05-06) that the Head of Global Markets reports directly to the CEO and is responsible for **sales and trading**. This is the standard front-office structure: institutional sales (counterparty-facing distribution of FX, rates, credit, and other markets products) and trading (market-making, risk warehousing, and franchise hedging) under a single seat. Saskia is a profit-centre executive of equivalent stature to the COO and CFO.

This restructures earlier placements: Kai (trading systems engineer) now reports to Saskia rather than to Devon (COO). Niko (sales / CRM engineer) **stays with Devon** — Niko's mandate is retail / commercial customer CRM, not institutional markets sales; the two are different functions. The institutional-markets-sales engineering counterpart is currently **a gap**, flagged below.

## 3. Scope of work (priority order)

1. **Market-making and risk-taking.** Set the trading mandate; manage the market-risk warehouse within Helena's appetite; coverage across asset classes (initial focus: FX, rates, money markets; expansion as the franchise grows).
2. **Institutional sales.** Counterparty coverage; client onboarding for institutional segments (in coordination with Mira/Zara on KYC and Imani on master agreements); pricing in coordination with the Treasurer on funding cost.
3. **Execution for internal clients.** Treasury (Eitan) and any future asset-management arm execute through Markets where applicable.
4. **Market-abuse and conduct posture.** First-line conduct ownership for the trading floor; voice-recording, e-comms surveillance feeds delivered to Mira / Zara as the second-line surveillance pipeline.
5. **Booking model and STP.** Co-owns with Kai the trading-systems architecture — OMS / EMS, FIX connectivity, ISO 20022 confirms, multi-asset booking, exchange and ECN connectivity.
6. **Risk limits.** Operates within market-risk limits set by Helena; daily VaR / sensitivity / concentration discipline. Limit breaches are events under P1.
7. **Counterparty credit.** Coordinates with Helena on counterparty-credit appetite; CSAs and netting agreements via Imani.
8. **Sales-trader mandate management.** Best-execution discipline; client-suitability under FAIS and conduct standards.
9. **Research / market-colour function** as the franchise grows — separate, with Chinese-wall discipline if research becomes investment-recommendation in scope.

## 5. Required expertise

- Senior front-office leadership at a SA bank or large institutional dealer.
- Multi-asset trading — FX, rates, money markets at minimum; credit and equities desirable.
- Institutional sales coverage; counterparty relationships across SA and selected offshore.
- Market-risk discipline at executive level — VaR, sensitivities, behavioural assumptions.
- Conduct and market-abuse regimes — Financial Markets Act, JSE rules where applicable, FCA / IIROC analogues.
- Trading-systems architecture fluency — OMS / EMS, FIX, ISO 20022 confirms, exchange connectivity.
- ZARONIA transition front-office implications.

## 6. Desirable expertise

- Prior Head of Markets, Head of Trading, or Head of FICC at a SA bank.
- Track record of building a markets franchise from a small base.
- ACI Dealing Certificate / Diploma; CFA preferred.
- Experience standing up market-abuse surveillance integrated with second-line tooling.

## 7. Regulatory / certification requirements

- **Fit and proper** under PA / FSCA standards.
- Financial Markets Act 19 of 2012 — to the extent the bank transacts in regulated instruments.
- FAIS Act 37 of 2002 — Key Individual designation likely required where advice is given.
- Market-abuse provisions (FMA Chapter X) — first-line accountability.
- JSE / Strate / TradElect rules where market access is direct.
- ACI Dealing Certificate or Diploma — strongly preferred.
- Working knowledge of ISDA and GMRA documentation as it interfaces with Imani.

## 10. Principle alignment

**P1.** Trades, risk events, limit breaches, P&L are events. Trading book at any past as-of date is reproducible.

**P2.** Every limit, every product approval, every market-abuse rule cites its authority. Mandate paper is register-linked.

**P3.** Booking, confirmation, settlement, surveillance are coded; voice-trading is surveilled, not exempted.

**P4.** Trader workstation auth is strong; trading actions are signed events; insider-risk controls apply to the desk.

**P5.** Multi-currency, multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction by construction — every trade carries entity, currency, jurisdiction.

**P6.** Trading P&L, mandate compliance, and ALCO/BRC packs are queries; Saskia signs the generated output.
