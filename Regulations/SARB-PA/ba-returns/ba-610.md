## Purpose of BA 610

Form BA 610 is the prudential return through which the bank reports its **statement of profit or loss** (income statement) to the Prudential Authority — the *flow* statement of how much profit (or loss) the bank earned over the reporting period, decomposed by income and expense category. It is the **performance half** of the financial-statement pair; its companion is the **balance sheet (BA 600)** — the point-in-time *stock* statement. The two are linked through equity: the BA 610 period **profit-or-loss flows into the retained-earnings line of the BA 600 statement of financial position** (and through the IFRS statement of changes in equity, `ifrs-changes-in-equity.ts` — *"movement in retained earnings = period P&L"*). BA 610 also feeds the BA 100 capital-adequacy return indirectly — retained earnings are a CET1 component — and the operational-risk Business-Indicator (the Reg 33 / OPE25 BI is derived from income-statement lines, which is why the operational-RWA feed on [`ba-100.md`](ba-100.md) is gated on the income-statement / trial-balance projection landing).

The headline income-statement computation (IAS 1 §82; computed by the in-repo `ba-610-income-statement.ts` generator):

```
interestIncome      = Σ |amount| over lines classified `interest-income`
interestExpense     = Σ |amount| over lines classified `interest-expense`
netInterestIncome   = interestIncome − interestExpense
feeIncome           = Σ |amount| over lines classified `fee-income`
tradingProfitLoss   = Σ signed-amount over lines classified `trading-pnl`   (sign-preserving — trading P&L can be a loss)
operatingExpenses   = Σ |amount| over lines classified `operating-expense`
profitBeforeTax     = netInterestIncome + feeIncome + tradingProfitLoss − operatingExpenses
```

The **sign-preserving treatment of trading P&L** is load-bearing: net interest income and fee income are summed on `|amount|` (always positive contributions), but trading P&L is summed on the *signed* amount because a trading loss must reduce the bottom line, not inflate it (`ba-610-income-statement.ts`: *"sign-preserving: trading P&L can be a loss"*). FX P&L flows through the trial balance via the `FxPositionRevalued` posting rule and is classified into `trading-pnl` (or a dedicated `fx-pnl` sub-category) at the call site — no special handling in the generator.

**The bank's actual income statement today.** In the build phase the bank holds **no real loan book or customer revenue** (CLAUDE.md "build phase vs licence-day"). The income-statement substrate runs over the **period-delta** general ledger (the P&L accounts only — income + expense classes), which at build-phase carries the **FX-desk trading P&L** (the live `FxPositionRevalued` / `RealisedPnlRecognised` stream — see `project_continuation_2026_06_07_fx_subledger_and_ras_b2` and the daily-P&L work) and the simulator's interbank interest flows. The generator is a **pure projection over the period-delta trial balance filtered to P&L accounts** (Slice 2) — events → projection-runtime → period-delta trial balance → IFRS semantic layer → BA 610 — so the figure is always event-sourced (Principle 1).

## The BA 610 selected-detail breakdown (BA 610 detail)

D5/2025 carries a **selected income-statement detail** alongside the headline income statement — the `ba-610-income-detail.ts` generator (*"BA 610 detail — Selected Income-Statement Information / Profitability Detail"*). It takes the `Ba610IncomeStatement` output as its primary typed input (it does **not** re-fold the trial balance independently — the BA 610 output is the canonical entry point) and decomposes NII + non-interest income by:

- **(a) Instrument class** — interbank / corporate / sovereign / retail-placeholder, per the caller-supplied `Ba610DetailBandingMap`.
- **(b) ALM maturity band** — overnight / 1-7d / 8-30d / 1-3m / 3-6m / 6-12m / >1y, per the banding map.
- **(c) Net-interest margin (NIM)** — NII ÷ average earning assets; client NIM vs structural NIM split deferred pending Ravi's (Treasury & ALM engineer) FTP engine.
- **(d) Non-interest income** — `feeIncome` + `tradingProfitLoss` lifted straight from the BA 610 output.
- **(e) Operating efficiency** — the cost-to-income ratio (`operatingExpenses ÷ (NII + feeIncome + tradingProfitLoss + otherIncome)`).

The FTP rates (`Ba610DetailFtpRates`) and ALM banding are caller-supplied fixtures at v0; when Ravi's FTP engine + ALM cashflow engine land, the typed outputs replace the fixtures with no generator API change.

## GL reconciliation

The BA 610 totals tie back to the general ledger's **P&L accounts** (income + expense classes). The substrate enforces this structurally: BA 610 is a **pure projection over the period-delta trial balance filtered to P&L accounts** (`ba-610-income-statement.ts` over `TrialBalanceSnapshotRow`), and the trial balance is itself a projection over the event log (Principle 1). The **period-delta** scope is the distinction from BA 600: the balance sheet reads the *cumulative* trial balance (stock); the income statement reads the *period-delta* trial balance (flow). The chart of accounts (`prototype/platform/accounting/coa-registry.ts`) classifies each leaf account into an IFRS income/expense class; the BA 610 line-classification map (caller-supplied at v0 — the substrate gap) maps each P&L leaf account to a SARB BA 610 line. When the COA schema gains a native `ba610Line` field (the planned gap), the map moves into the COA. The BA 610 → BA 600 link (period P&L → retained earnings) is itself a reconciliation control: the income-statement bottom line must equal the period movement in the balance-sheet retained-earnings equity line.

## Material foreign-currency reporting

The income statement is multi-currency: foreign-currency income and expense are translated at the rate at the date of the transaction (IAS 21 §21) or an average rate (IAS 21 §22); FX trading P&L (realised + unrealised, the `FxPositionRevalued` / `RealisedPnlRecognised` stream) is the largest build-phase income line and is reported in ZAR. ZAR is the presentation currency; the multi-currency exposure is the data (Principle 5). The v0 `ba-610-income-statement.ts` generator presents the SARB form in `functionalCurrency` (ZAR).
