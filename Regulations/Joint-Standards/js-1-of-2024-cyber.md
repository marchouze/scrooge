# Joint Standard 1 of 2024 — Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience

**Curator:** Mira (with Senna) · **Status:** POPULATED · **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06

## Citation

- **Title:** Joint Standard 1 of 2024 on Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience.
- **Issuing authorities:** South African Reserve Bank Prudential Authority (PA) and Financial Sector Conduct Authority (FSCA), jointly under the FSR Act 9 of 2017.
- **Effective:** 2024 (with implementation grace periods for in-scope financial institutions).
- **Source:** [resbank.co.za](https://www.resbank.co.za) and [fsca.co.za](https://www.fsca.co.za) — Joint Standards.

## Scope and applicability

The Joint Standard applies to financial institutions designated under the FSR Act, including banks. The bank is in scope on licensing.

## Key obligations

### Governance and accountability

- **Named accountable executive ("responsible person") for cyber risk and resilience.** Direct access to the Board (or BRC).
- **Board-approved cyber strategy** integrated with the bank's overall risk management framework.
- **Adequate resources** allocated to cyber capability — people, technology, budget.

### Cyber risk management framework

- Documented framework covering identification, protection, detection, response, recovery (NIST CSF-aligned).
- Risk assessment of cyber risk integrated into the bank's enterprise risk management.
- Threat modelling on new systems, products, integrations.
- Vulnerability management programme with documented cadence.

### Controls

- Identity and access management (least privilege, MFA, just-in-time access).
- Cryptographic controls (encryption in transit and at rest; key management).
- Secure software development lifecycle (SAST / DAST / supply-chain integrity).
- Network and endpoint protection.
- Logging, monitoring, anomaly detection.
- Backup and recovery for critical systems and data.

### Incident response

- Documented incident response plan covering detection, classification, containment, eradication, recovery, post-incident review.
- Severity tiering aligned with regulator-notification thresholds.
- Rehearsed runbooks; periodic exercises.
- **Incident reporting to PA / FSCA** within stipulated timelines per severity.

### Third-party and outsourcing risk

- Third-party cyber risk assessed pre-engagement and continuously.
- Material outsourcing aligned with SARB PA Directive 3 of 2018 — see complementary policy.

### Operational resilience integration

- Cyber resilience integrated with operational resilience: cyber-induced disruption tested as a severe-but-plausible scenario per Important Business Service.

### Reporting and assurance

- Periodic reporting to the BRC on cyber posture, KPIs, incidents.
- Independent assurance over the cyber programme — by Internal Audit, supplemented by external assessments where appropriate.

### Industry threat-intelligence sharing

- Participate in sector-relevant threat-intelligence sharing arrangements.

### Recovery and resumption

- Recovery objectives (RTO / RPO) defined for critical systems and tested.
