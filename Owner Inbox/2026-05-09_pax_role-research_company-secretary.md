## 1. Mandate boundary

**In scope (governance seat — single hat plus deputy-IO):**

- **Company Secretary under Companies Act ss.86–89** — guides directors on duties; provides the certificate under s.88(2)(e) (annual statement of compliance); takes minutes; ensures Board / committee meetings are properly constituted; maintains the bank's statutory registers; functionally independent of the CEO under s.86(2)(b) [citation: Companies Act 71 of 2008 ss.86–89].
- **Board secretariat** — with the Independent Chair (seat #1), sets agenda, drafts resolutions, manages board-pack workflow [citation: King IV Principle 6 (Board's role); Companies Act s.86].
- **Deputy POPIA Information Officer** — designated deputy IO under POPIA Regulation 4; lodgment with the Information Regulator parallel to the primary IO designation [citation: POPIA Act 4 of 2013 s.56; POPIA Regulations Reg. 4; `ORG-PR(IV)-13` (deputy-IO sub-gloss pending — Mira)].
- **Conflicts register custodian** — King IV practice; first-pass conflicts checks at NED appointment, executive appointment, and annual refresh [citation: King IV Principle 7; `Procedures/by-policy/conflicts-declaration.md`].
- **Whistleblowing channel custodian** — King IV practice; CoSec is the typical first point of contact for whistleblowers in SA-listed-co practice [citation: King IV Principle 4; Protected Disclosures Act 26 of 2000].
- **Statutory register maintenance** — securities register (Companies Act s.50), directors register (s.71), Public Officer notification (Companies Act / SARS Tax Administration Act).
- **Banks Act fit-and-proper executive officer** — CoSec is a designated fit-and-proper officer [citation: Banks Act s.60; PA Fit-and-Proper Standards `ORG-HR-11`].

**Out of scope:**

- **General Counsel role** — Imani (legal-as-code engineer) builds the legal-as-code substrate; the post-licence GC hire (Owen framework §13 hiring sequence) carries the legal-counsel function. The CoSec is governance-secretariat, not counsel.
- **Primary POPIA Information Officer** — held by the Compliance Lead (seat #5). CoSec is deputy.
- **Audit-Committee accountability** — AC Chair (seat #1) holds.
- **Risk-function accountability** — human CRO (seat #6) holds.

## 6. Conflict-of-interest map

- **Concurrent-CoSec-at-licensed-bank disqualifying.** A CoSec serves a single accountable institution at a time as a practical matter; concurrent dual-CoSec is unworkable.
- **Family / domestic relationship with CEO.** Companies Act s.86(2)(b) functional independence is absolute; spouse / parent / child / sibling of Marc is disqualified.
- **Major-shareholder-aligned candidates.** If the candidate has been employed by a likely future shareholder, the CoSec independence question arises; conflicts register at appointment.
- **Cross-directorship.** Less acute than for NED seats — CoSecs typically hold one CoSec role at a time; if the candidate has any other CoSec role or significant NED role, declare and assess.
- **Audit-firm overlap.** If the candidate's recent (≤5 years) employer is a Big-4 audit firm, the firm cannot be the bank's external auditor under independence rules — same constraint structure as NED #2 §6. Mitigation: audit-firm choice avoids the candidate's prior firm.
