# Governance Framework — Social and Ethics Committee v1

> **Status:** IN FORCE (policy layer). The obligation to constitute a Social and Ethics Committee arises under Companies Regulations 43 where the company's public-interest score exceeds 500. Until the bank's public-interest score is confirmed, the S&E function is carried out by the Board as a whole; a dedicated S&E Committee is constituted when the statutory trigger is met.
>
> **Author:** Owen (Company Secretary, governance).
>
> **Build-phase note:** The bank is in its build phase (pre-licence-day). No external customers, employees, or revenue streams exist yet; the statutory S&E trigger (public-interest score > 500) is not expected to fire until post-launch. This framework governs the interim arrangements and the full S&E mandate that activates on trigger.

## 1. Mandate

The S&E Committee is a statutory committee of the Board established under Companies Regulations 43. Its mandate covers:

1. Social and economic development — B-BBEE compliance, Skills Development Act obligations, UNGC Ten Principles.
2. Good corporate citizenship — environmental impact, public health/safety, consumer relationships.
3. Labour and employment — Employment Equity Act compliance, working conditions, remuneration.
4. Ethics management — code of ethics, anti-corruption, ethics culture.

The Committee reports to shareholders at the Annual General Meeting.

## 2. Composition

| Role | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Chair | Director (preferably non-executive) |
| Members | Minimum 3 (at least one non-executive) |
| Secretary | Owen (Company Secretary, governance) |

**Build-phase interim:** Until the full Board is constituted, the S&E mandate is held by the CEO with Owen as secretary.

---

## 3. Meeting cadence

Minimum: twice per financial year, or as required to discharge statutory obligations. Quorum: majority of members.

---

## 4. Reporting

The S&E Committee tables a report to the Board annually and to shareholders at the AGM covering progress against each statutory oversight area.
