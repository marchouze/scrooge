Hedge-accounting election

Camille / Eitan
F1● CEO
Source: Hedge Accounting Policy (Finance bundle §7) ↗

Hedge accounting framework election: IFRS 9 vs IAS 39 carryover

IFRS 9 hedge accounting (Camille's recommendation) — modern; closer alignment between risk management and accounting; simpler effectiveness regime; broader range of eligible hedged items. Or IAS 39 carryover (Eitan's flag) — legacy continuity for institutions migrating from existing infrastructure; permitted election under IFRS 9. The election is one-way and applies bank-wide.

Recommendation: IFRS 9. The bank is a build-not-buy de novo bank with no IAS 39 legacy to preserve; modern alignment is the simpler, regulator-preferred path.
