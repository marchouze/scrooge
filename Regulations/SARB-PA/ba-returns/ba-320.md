## Purpose of BA 320

Form BA 320 is the prudential return for the **alternative (simplified) market-risk capital treatment**. Market risk is a **Pillar-1** risk; BA 320 carries the *reduced* / *alternative* route to the standardised position-risk charge, distinct from the full standardised method on BA 310 and the FRTB recalibration on BA 325. The economic perimeter is the same four risk classes (interest-rate, equity, FX, commodity — Reg 28(3)); the *alternative* form differs in the **calculation granularity / calibration** it prescribes (a simplified building-block treatment rather than the full standardised or FRTB sensitivities method).

The intent of an **alternative / simplified** market-risk return is well-established in the Basel architecture: banks with **small or non-complex trading books** are permitted a **reduced-sensitivity / simplified standardised approach** so that the capital methodology is proportionate to the size and complexity of the market-risk exposure — avoiding the full FRTB-SA computational burden for institutions whose market risk is immaterial. The **precise eligibility thresholds and the substantive calculation rules** that BA 320 prescribes (the in-corpus §2.1.13 routes them to *"the relevant requirements specified in the Regulations"*) are counsel-gated (see Open items).

## Binding obligations on the bank (summary)

1. **Measure trading-book (and in-scope banking-book FX/commodity) market risk** and hold capital per Reg 28 — where the BA 320 alternative form is elected / directed, on the simplified / alternative basis (`ORG-PR-RETURNS-012`; Reg 28; BCBS MAR 20.1).
2. **Determine eligibility for the alternative treatment** — the small / non-complex trading-book proportionality test (counsel-gated thresholds).
3. **Capitalise FX positions in either book** — the FX NOP attracts market-risk capital whether held in the banking book or the trading book (Reg 28(3)(c)).
4. **Integrate the market-risk charge into the capital-adequacy aggregate** — feed the capital (× 12.5 → market RWA) into BA 700 / BA 100 (Reg 38, integrating Reg 28/29).
5. **Submit form BA 320** to the PA per Annexure 12A of D5/2025 and the Regulations, on the prescribed (monthly) cadence — *if* the alternative form binds (D5/2025 §2.1.13; eligibility + methodology + cadence counsel-gated).
6. **Operate the market-risk appetite limits above the regulatory floor** — MR-1-FX (1-day 99% VaR) and the per-currency NOP sub-limits (internal RAS; the appetite layer, not this catalogue).
