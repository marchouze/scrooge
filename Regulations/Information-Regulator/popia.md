# POPIA — Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013

**Curator:** Mira (with Iris) · **Status:** POPULATED · **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06

## Citation

- **Title:** Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (Act No. 4 of 2013).
- **Promulgated:** 2013; provisions came into effect 1 July 2020 (one-year grace ended 30 June 2021).
- **Authority:** Information Regulator (IR).
- **Companion:** POPIA Regulations, 2018.
- **Source:** [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za) — Acts, Regulations, Guidance Notes, Codes of Conduct.

## Scope and applicability

The bank is a "responsible party" under POPIA. All eight conditions for lawful processing apply across every processing activity touching personal information of customers, employees, counterparties, and third parties.

## Key obligations

### Lawful processing — eight conditions (Ch. 3)

1. **Accountability** — s.8 — the bank ensures POPIA conditions are complied with.
2. **Processing limitation** — ss.9–12 — lawful, minimal, with consent or other lawful ground.
3. **Purpose specification** — ss.13–14 — specific, explicit, lawfully defined purpose; retention only as necessary.
4. **Further processing limitation** — s.15 — compatible with the purpose for which collected.
5. **Information quality** — s.16 — accurate, complete, not misleading, updated.
6. **Openness** — ss.17–18 — documentation; notice to data subjects.
7. **Security safeguards** — ss.19–22 — appropriate technical and organisational measures.
8. **Data subject participation** — ss.23–25 — access, correction, deletion.

### Lawful processing grounds — s.11

Personal information may only be processed if at least one of:
- Consent.
- Necessary for contract performance.
- Compliance with a legal obligation.
- Protection of a legitimate interest.
- Public-interest task.
- Pursuit of a legitimate interest, balanced against the data subject's rights.

### Special personal information — s.34

Religious / philosophical beliefs, race / ethnic origin, trade union membership, political persuasion, health / sex life, biometric, criminal behaviour. Processing prohibited unless an exception applies (s.27).

### Children's personal information — s.35

Processing of children's information prohibited unless an exception applies (s.35(1)).

### Security safeguards — ss.19–22

- **s.19** — Identify reasonably foreseeable risks; establish and maintain safeguards; verify implementation; update.
- **s.20** — Operator (processor) safeguards: written contract; security obligations.
- **s.21** — Notification of security compromises by operator to responsible party.
- **s.22** — Notification of security compromises by responsible party to the Information Regulator and affected data subjects, **as soon as reasonably possible** after discovery.

### Data subject rights — ss.23–25

- **s.23** — Access: confirmation of holding + record of personal information.
- **s.24** — Correction: rectify or delete.
- **s.71** — Automated decision-making: limitations and right to challenge.
- **s.11(3)** — Right to object to processing.

### Information Officer — ss.55–58 + Reg. 4

- **s.55** — Duties of the Head of the Body (default Information Officer), unless otherwise designated.
- **s.56** — Designate the Information Officer; lodge with the Information Regulator under POPIA Reg. 4(1)(a).
- **s.57** — Prior authorisation for certain processing types.
- **s.58** — Notification of personal-information processing where required.
- **POPIA Reg. 4** — Information Officer responsibilities: ensure POPIA compliance; PAIA Manual; deal with the Regulator; promote awareness; address requests.

### Cross-border transfer — s.72

Transfer of personal information outside SA only if:
- Comparable law in the destination, OR
- Binding-corporate-rules / model clauses, OR
- Data-subject consent, OR
- Strict necessity for contract or interest of the data subject, OR
- Other lawful condition.

### Codes of Conduct (sector-specific)

The Information Regulator may approve sector-specific Codes of Conduct (e.g., for banking / financial services). Track issuance and adoption.

### PAIA — Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000

- **s.51** — Maintain and lodge a PAIA Manual.
- Respond to PAIA requests within statutory timeframes.

### Penalties

- **s.107** — Administrative fines up to **R 10 million** per offence.
- Criminal liability for certain offences (obstruction, hindering, breach of confidentiality).
- Civil liability of the responsible party for damage caused.
