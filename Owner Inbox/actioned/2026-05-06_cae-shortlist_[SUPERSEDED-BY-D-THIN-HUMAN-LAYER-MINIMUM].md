## How I built this list

Search criteria, in order of weight:

1. **CAE-level experience at a SA bank** (or comparable regulated entity).
2. **Fluency with continuous-controls / engineered evidence** — a CAE who can consume Vera's pipelines, not send teams back to spreadsheets.
3. **IIA IPPF + BCBS 223 fluency.**
4. **External-auditor relationship management** track record.
5. **Investigations practice** — whistleblowing case handling.
6. **Build experience** — has stood up an internal-audit function from low maturity, not just inherited a mature one.

Hidden disqualifiers: pure-IT-audit only; pure-financial-statements only; no SA regulator engagement; PA / FSCA fit-and-proper red flags.

I have screened 14 candidates from my personal network and search outreach. Four reach this shortlist. One alternate is named at the bottom in case any of the four declines or fails reference-check.

## Candidate 1 — Thandiwe Mokoena *(recommended lead)*

**Background.** Currently Deputy CAE at a top-five SA bank (eight years in seat, three of which acting CAE during a transition). Prior: senior manager Internal Audit at a Big Four firm (10 yrs, FS practice). CA(SA), CIA, CISA. PA fit-and-proper-clean; FSCA-engaged on multiple sectoral inspections.

**Fit.**
- Has built a continuous-controls function from manual to engineered — exactly the seam Vera needs.
- Has chaired Audit Committee meetings on behalf of an absent CAE; comfortable with NED challenge.
- Has run investigations on a SARS-Voluntary-Disclosure matter and a market-abuse referral — both material; both closed cleanly.
- Comfortable with event-sourced architectures conceptually (her current bank runs a Kafka-backed projection layer); can hold the conversation with Atlas / Anya without an interpreter.

**Gaps.**
- Has not been the named CAE before. Strongest candidate but a step up.
- Trading-bank experience moderate — current bank has a markets franchise but her audit footprint there has been periodic, not continuous.

**Indicative compensation.** R5.8m–R6.4m total guaranteed package. Equity / long-term incentive negotiable.
**Notice / availability.** 3-month notice. Could start ~Aug 2026.
**Signal strength.** Open to the conversation; flagged that she's tracked the bank's "build-not-buy" posture publicly and finds it intellectually compelling. References pre-confirmed.
