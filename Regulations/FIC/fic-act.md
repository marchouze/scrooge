# FIC Act 38 of 2001 (as amended)

**Curator:** Mira · **Status:** POPULATED · **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-06

## Citation

- **Title:** Financial Intelligence Centre Act, 2001 (Act No. 38 of 2001).
- **Promulgated:** 2001; substantially amended by FIC Amendment Act 1 of 2017 and subsequent legislation; further amendments to address SA's grey-listing remediation.
- **Authority:** Financial Intelligence Centre (FIC), with PA / FSCA cooperation under the FSR Act.
- **Source:** [fic.gov.za](https://www.fic.gov.za) — Acts and regulations.

## Scope and applicability

The bank is an "accountable institution" under FIC Act Schedule 1 (item 6 — banks). All FIC obligations apply.

## Key obligations

### Risk Management and Compliance Programme (RMCP) — s.42

- **s.42(1)** — Develop, document, maintain and implement a Risk Management and Compliance Programme.
- **s.42(2)** — RMCP addresses customer due diligence, monitoring, reporting, training, record-keeping.
- **s.42A** — Designate a senior person responsible for compliance with the RMCP. *(MLRO and FIC Compliance Officer designations land here.)*

### Customer Due Diligence — ss.21–21H

- **s.21** — Establish and verify the identity of every prospective client before establishing a business relationship or concluding a single transaction.
- **s.21A** — Apply enhanced due diligence (EDD) to high-risk customers.
- **s.21B** — Verify beneficial ownership; recursive identification of natural persons exercising effective control.
- **s.21C** — Ongoing due diligence: monitor business relationships and transactions for consistency with the bank's knowledge of the client.
- **s.21D** — Doubt about identity must be re-verified.
- **s.21E** — Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs): EDD; senior-management approval required.
- **s.21F** — Foreign correspondent banking: EDD on respondent institutions.
- **s.21G** — Reliance on third parties (e.g., other accountable institutions) is permitted on conditions.
- **s.21H** — Enhanced ongoing due diligence for higher-risk relationships.

### Record-keeping — s.22

- **s.22** — Maintain CDD records and transaction records for at least **5 years** after the end of the relationship or the date of the transaction.

### Reporting

- **s.28** — Cash Threshold Reports (CTRs) for cash transactions at or above the prescribed threshold (currently R 24,999.99).
- **s.28A** — Property Associated with Terrorist and Related Activities Reports (TPRs).
- **s.29** — Suspicious Transaction / Activity Reports (STRs / SARs) — file as soon as practicable, but in any event no later than 15 days after suspicion is raised.
- **s.29(3)** — **Tipping-off prohibition.** Disclosing the existence of an STR (or that an STR is contemplated) to the subject is a criminal offence.

### Training and awareness — s.43

- **s.43** — Provide training to staff on AML / CFT obligations and the bank's RMCP.

### Sanctions — s.26B (incorporating POCDATARA)

- **s.26B** — Targeted Financial Sanctions: freeze property of designated persons / entities; report to FIC.

### FIC Guidance Notes (binding interpretive)

- **GN 7** — Risk-Based Approach (RBA): customer typologies, periodicity for ongoing CDD (high → annual; medium → 24m; low → 36m).
- **GN 5** — Beneficial ownership.
- **Public Compliance Communications (PCCs)** — issued from time to time; binding on accountable institutions.

### Penalties

- Administrative penalties up to **R 10 million** for non-compliance with RMCP / CDD / reporting obligations.
- Criminal liability for tipping-off, knowing facilitation of money laundering, or failure to report STRs.

### Grey-listing remediation context

- South Africa was grey-listed by FATF in February 2023; remediation is ongoing. The bank's RMCP must reflect the remediation pillars: beneficial-ownership transparency, sanctions, STR quality, supervision.
