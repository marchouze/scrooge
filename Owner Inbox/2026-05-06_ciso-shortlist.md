## How I built this list

Search criteria, in order of weight:

1. **CISO or deputy-CISO seat at a SA bank or comparable regulated FS entity** (named-accountability shape).
2. **Joint Standard 1 of 2024 programme implementation** — has authored or led one, not just complied with one.
3. **Cloud-native security at executive depth** — Azure preferred (M8 target); AWS / GCP cross-credible.
4. **Trading-floor / market-infrastructure security awareness** — surveillance-pipeline integrity, dealer-mandate authorisation, exchange / SAMOS / SWIFT connectivity.
5. **Cryptographic-key governance** — FIPS 140-2/3 Level 3 boundary design and HSM operations.
6. **Incident command** — has commanded a regulator-reportable incident and survived the regulator response.
7. **Secure SDLC + supply-chain** — SLSA, sigstore, SBOMs; comfortable engaging Senna's seam directly.
8. **Build experience** — has stood up a security function from low maturity, not just inherited a mature one.

Hidden disqualifiers: pure-IT-ops-security only; pure-GRC / paper CISO; no SA regulator engagement; PA / FSCA fit-and-proper red flags; reflexively risk-averse posture incompatible with a build-only bank.

I have screened 17 candidates from my personal network and search outreach. Four reach this shortlist. One alternate is named at the bottom in case any of the four declines or fails reference-check.
