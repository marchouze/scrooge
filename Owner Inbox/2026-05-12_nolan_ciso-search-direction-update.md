## 2. Double-hat analysis — which seat should carry CISO?

The six seats established under [citation: D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM] are assessed below as candidates for the CISO double-hat. The test is: does the seat holder's existing accountability portfolio make the CISO function a natural extension, and would the PA / FSCA recognise the pairing?

| Seat | Already carries | CISO double-hat fit | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent Chair | Board governance; AC Chair; S&E NED duties | No — independence conflict; the Chair must oversee management, not sit inside it | Reject |
| Human CRO | Risk oversight; cyber risk ownership in the RAS; regulatory interface on JS 1 of 2024 | High — CRO/CISO is an established pattern at smaller SA banks; both are risk/control seats with complementary mandates | **Recommend** |
| Compliance Lead | MLRO + FIC Compliance Officer + POPIA Information Officer (already triple-hatted) | Low — adding a fourth regulatory hat (CISO under PA/FSCA Joint Standard 2 of 2024) creates unacceptable concentration and regulatory-conflict risk; the PA specifically reviews capacity and conflict in fit-and-proper assessments | Reject |
| Company Secretary | Corporate governance; AC secretariat; statutory filings | Low — no information-security expertise expected; CISO accountability would be cosmetic and unenforceable | Reject |
| NED #2 | AC member | Low — NEDs carry oversight responsibility, not executive accountability; a CISO who cannot direct operational response is structurally defective | Reject |
| NED #3 | AC member | Low — same as NED #2 | Reject |

### Recommendation: Human CRO double-hatted as CRO + CISO

The Human CRO seat is the sole credible host for the CISO double-hat. The case is as follows:

- **Regulatory coherence.** Both the CRO and CISO functions sit inside the risk-and-control pillar. The PA / FSCA will recognise the pairing; PA/FSCA Joint Standard 2 of 2024 places information-security governance squarely in the risk-management framework and treats the CRO as a natural accountability anchor at proportionate-scale institutions.
- **Mandate alignment.** The CRO already owns cyber risk in the Risk Appetite Statement (RAS). Designating the same officer as CISO closes the gap between risk ownership (CRO) and security operational authority (CISO) — no separate command chain needed.
- **Headcount discipline.** Headcount stays at six. The CRO/CISO double-hat requires no Board approval beyond the original D-THIN-HUMAN-LAYER-MINIMUM authorisation.
- **Scalability path.** Once trading volume warrants a standalone security function (typically post-licence at meaningful AUM / transaction throughput), the CISO hat is stripped from the CRO and assigned to a dedicated hire under a future Board decision. The double-hat is explicitly transitional — it is not a permanent structural choice.

**Consequence for the CRO candidate spec:** the Human CRO profile must now include CISO-compatible competencies. This is a follow-on action for Nolan (see §4 below).
