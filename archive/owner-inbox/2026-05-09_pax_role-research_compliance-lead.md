## 1. Mandate boundary

**In scope (governance seat — single human, three hats):**

- **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)** — designated under FIC Act for STR escalation; single point of contact with the FIC for suspicious-transaction-report submissions and FIC supervisory engagement [citation: FIC Act 38 of 2001 s.43A; FIC published guidance — `ORG-FC-11` register row exists, gloss-hardening pending Mira].
- **FIC Compliance Officer** — designated under FIC Act ss.43A–43B for accountable-institution compliance with FIC Act; signs the RMCP (Risk Management and Compliance Programme); runs the bank's CDD / EDD / sanctions-screening / record-keeping discipline [citation: FIC Act ss.43A–43B; `ORG-FC-11`].
- **POPIA Information Officer** — designated under POPIA s.56; lodgment with the Information Regulator; primary contact for data-subject-rights cases, breach notifications, and PAIA requests [citation: POPIA Act 4 of 2013 s.56; `ORG-PR(IV)-13`].
- **Conduct + TCF programme accountability** — FAIS conduct standards, TCF principles operationalised through the bank's institutional-only wholesale dealer franchise [citation: FAIS Act 37 of 2002; FSCA TCF Outcomes; `ORG-CD-01`]. TCF activates at commencement-of-trading per `project_rules_bind_at_commencement.md`.
- **Sanctions-screening operational accountability** — FIC ss.21–28A CDD/EDD; UNSC sanctions regime; FIC PCC on sanctions screening [citation: TBC pending Mira `ORG-FC-SANCTIONS-SCREENING` register row].
- **Banks Act fit-and-proper executive officer** [citation: Banks Act s.60; PA Fit-and-Proper Standards `ORG-HR-11`].
- **Functional reporting line to Board / AC** for STR-escalation and POPIA-Regulator pathways — preserves second-line independence FIC's published RMCP guidance requires [citation: FIC published RMCP guidance; `ORG-FC-11` gloss].

**Out of scope:**

- **MLRO-alternate designation** — held by AC Chair (seat #1) per Mira+Zara §4.2; this seat is principal, not alternate.
- **Deputy-IO designation** — held by CoSec (seat #4) per Mira+Zara §4.2; this seat is principal IO, not deputy.
- **FAIS Key Individual designation** — Saskia (Head of Global Markets, governance — agent) is the steady-state KI per Owen §3C; Marc retains FAIS KI interim until counsel confirms FSP scope per `2026-05-09_imani_external-counsel-licence-application.md` §2.4. The Compliance Lead is **not** the FAIS KI by default; counsel may assign at the licence-application gate. See §8 #1.
- **CRO accountability** — human CRO (seat #6).
- **External-audit relationship** — Camille / Marc.

**Strongly preferred:**

- **Triple-hat-experience precedent** — has previously held two of the three hats simultaneously (e.g. MLRO + FIC CO at a tier-2 bank). The full triple-hat (MLRO + FIC CO + POPIA IO) is uncommon in SA; Mira+Zara expect workload concentration, not impossibility.
- **AI-substrate-aware** — willingness to govern Mira / Zara / Iris agent-substrate outputs (sanctions-screening engine, RMCP cycle, IO cycle).
- **FIC Public Compliance Communication (PCC) cycle awareness** — FIC engagement around PCCs and supervisory-letter cycles.
- **Cross-jurisdictional sanctions experience** — bank's institutional posture means non-SA-counterparty sanctions exposure (UNSC, OFAC, EU) is real.
- **FAIS Subordinate Legislation s.45 institutional-counterparty exemption fluency** — counsel-confirmed at gate but the candidate should already have a view on whether the bank's posture excludes FAIS scope or requires an FSP licence.
- **Whistleblowing-channel-handling experience** — POPIA + Protected Disclosures Act intersection; CoSec (seat #4) is first-contact, but Compliance Lead handles AML-related disclosures.

**Disqualifying:**

- Failure of FIC fit-and-proper at any prior accountable institution.
- Cooling-off live for ex-FIC / ex-PA / ex-FSCA candidate.
- Any unresolved STR / RMCP-related supervisory finding personally addressed.
- Concurrent MLRO at any other SARB-licensed bank.
- POPIA breach personally attributed (Information Regulator finding).

## 3. Talent-landscape map

The SA MLRO + FIC CO talent pool is more developed than the IO pool — FIC has built the accountable-institution compliance ladder over 20+ years. Public-record observable: ~50–80 active SARB-licensed-bank-and-major-FSP MLROs / FIC COs at any time. The IO pool is smaller — POPIA s.56 has been operative since July 2021; the named-IO pool is ~30–50 individuals at large institutions.

The triple-hat pool (carrying all three hats simultaneously) is **very thin** — order-of-magnitude **5–15 individuals nationally**.

### 3.1 SA primary market — currently-licensed-bank MLRO / FIC CO

## 6. Conflict-of-interest map

- **Concurrent-MLRO disqualifying.** Same logic as CoSec — accountable-institution MLRO is a single-institution role.
- **Prior-employer restraint clauses.** Tier-1 SA bank Compliance contracts typically include restraints; head-hunter due diligence at long-list stage.
- **Audit-firm overlap.** Same constraint structure as NED #2 §6. If candidate's recent (≤5 years) employer is a Big-4 audit firm, that firm is disqualified as the bank's external auditor.
- **STR-confidentiality conflicts.** A candidate who has personally signed STRs that touch the bank's intended counterparty universe should declare and consider whether the candidate's prior-institution STR confidentiality binds in the new seat. FIC engagement at long-list stage may be necessary.
- **FAIS KI overlap.** If candidate is currently a FAIS KI at a competing FSP, that KI status must be transferred or relinquished before appointment. Verify at offer-stage.
- **POPIA personal-data conflicts.** Candidate as IO at prior institution may have handled data-subjects who are now bank counterparties; declare in the appointment form.
