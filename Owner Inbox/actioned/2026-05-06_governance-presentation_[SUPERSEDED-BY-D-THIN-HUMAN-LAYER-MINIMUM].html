Engineering vs governance — a structural commitment

Engineers build coded controls, projections, and platform components. Governance seats hold named regulatory accountability and oversee the engineers' outputs. The two are distinct seats; conflating them collapses the lines of defence and weakens regulator-facing accountability. The CRO is the first explicit case (CEO directive, 2026-05-06).

Interim governance — pending board formation

The bank does not yet have a Board, sub-committees, or external NEDs. Helena has been asked to design defensible interim governance until the board is constituted — the CEO cannot mark his own homework. This is one of three deferred questions held over for her framework draft.

The six architectural principles

Source: CLAUDE.md · Architectural principles. These bind every team member and every deliverable.

  1. Events are the only source of truth. Balances, capital, liquidity ratios, regulatory cells — all queries over the event log. As-of replay is first-class.
  2. Every action traces to a source. A versioned obligations register holds typed citations to regulator instruments and internal policy. No control without citation.
  3. Cloud-native; nothing manual or physical except where essential. Infrastructure, workflows, customer interaction, and documents are coded by default.
  4. Security designed in from the start. Threat modelling per design, zero trust, HSM-backed keys, secure SDLC, immutable audit, rehearsed incident response.
  5. Multi-currency, multi-entity, multi-country from day one. Adding the second of any of these is a configuration change, not a project.
  6. Single source of truth; presentations derive from data. Data → Process → Standard → Policy → Presentation. External artefacts are summarised derivations — never independent authorship.
