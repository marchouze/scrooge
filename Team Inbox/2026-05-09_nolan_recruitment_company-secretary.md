## 1. Why this seat exists

Companies Act ss.86–89 require a public company to maintain a CoSec. Companies Act s.86(2)(b) read with SA practice precludes the CEO from holding the CoSec function. SARB Prudential Authority precedent reads CoSec independence from the CEO as substantive (Owen+Imani §2.8). The CoSec is one of two clear "no merger with CEO" categories under SA company law.

The bank already names Owen as the engineering CoSec (an *agent*); the licence-day human CoSec is the seat the human is appointed to, with Owen-the-agent's outputs flowing through.

Mira+Zara's confirmation paper §4.2 places the **deputy-IO designation under POPIA Reg. 4** on the CoSec — this seat carries the deputy-IO role. The MLRO-alternate role is split off to the AC Chair NED (preferred placement).

## 3. Mandate boundary

**In scope:**

- Companies Act ss.86–89 CoSec function — Board secretariat, statutory registers, MOI compliance, AGM administration.
- Director-induction and ongoing director-development administration.
- Conflicts and related-party register custodianship.
- Whistleblowing-channel administration (Companies Act s.159).
- Board / committee meeting administration — papers, minutes, action-tracking.
- **Deputy-IO under POPIA Reg. 4** (split from the triple-hatted compliance lead per Mira+Zara §4.2) — carries the IO function during the IO's absence; lodgment with the Information Regulator under Reg. 4 is itself a CEO act.
- PAIA Manual co-ownership with the IO (already named in `_obligations-register.md` ORG-PR(IV)-16).

**Out of scope:**

- Day-to-day management.
- AML / CFT — the human compliance lead (priority 5) owns; the CoSec does not double as MLRO or FIC CO.
- MLRO-alternate role — placed on the AC Chair NED per Mira+Zara §4.2.
- Internal audit — Thandiwe (CAE) owns.

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- Companies Act s.86(2)(a) competence demonstrated — qualified CoSec via CGISA/ICSA or equivalent, or by experience.
- 8+ years CoSec experience including at least 3 years at a regulated financial institution or listed entity.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible.
- POPIA / PAIA fluency — deputy-IO role requires it.
- King IV fluency.
- Substantive independence from the CEO.
- South African resident.

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior CoSec at a SARB-regulated bank.
- Joint Standard 2 of 2024 fluency (Board-papers cybersecurity coverage).
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- Trading-bank exposure.
- Technology fluency — autonomous-by-default operating model means CoSec interfaces with engineering Owen-the-agent.

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Concurrent CoSec at a competitor SA bank.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings.
- Cannot demonstrate Companies Act s.86(2)(a) competence.
- Cannot be substantively independent of the CEO.
