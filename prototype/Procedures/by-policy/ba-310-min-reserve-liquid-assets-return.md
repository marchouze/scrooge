# Procedure — BA 310 (Minimum Liquid Reserve Balance and Liquid Assets — HQLA) return

**Procedure ID:** PROC-FIN-BA-310
**Delta on:** [`PROC-FIN-BA-01` — BA Return Generation](ba-return-generation.md). **This procedure does NOT restate** the generic preparer→four-eyes→attest→`BAReturnFiled`→restatement flow; it specifies only what is BA-310-specific. Read PROC-FIN-BA-01 §§3–9 first.
**Owner:** Camille (Chief Financial Officer) · Bea (Accounting & financial reporting engineer, engineering — reports to Camille). Liquidity / minimum-reserve **methodology** (the reserve-balance computation off the liabilities base, level-1 liquid-asset eligibility) is the standing domain of Eitan (Treasurer, governance); Bea authors the **return cell contract**, not the reserve methodology.
**Cadence:** Monthly (per PROC-FIN-BA-01 §4; triggered by `MonthEndCloseCompleted { period }`). The minimum-reserve balance is computed on a daily-average basis over the maintenance period; the BA 310 *return* is the monthly filing of those averages.

## 2. The cell-data-requirement contract (the BA-310-specific input)

Cell population is **driven by the typed contract**, not hand-coded line logic:

- **Schema (the framework):** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/cell-contract.ts` — the Zod `ReturnCellContractSchema`, reusable across all BA returns.
- **BA 310 instance:** `prototype/v2-core/regulatory-returns/ba310-contract.json` (63 cells), loaded + validated via the return-contract registry (`return-contracts.ts` → `loadReturnContract("BA310")`). Machine-generated by `gen-return-contract.py BA310` (provenance — an entry for EVERY XSD leaf cell, no hand-omission).

### 2.1 Product-attribute requirements — the NEW binding (Phase C batch 3)

BA 310 reports the bank's level-1 HQLA stock (gold, treasury bills, central-bank reserves, eligible securities) and its minimum-reserve balance. The HQLA / liquid-asset rows key off the **HQLA-eligible-asset** product axis — `prd:bank:treasury:hqla-eligible-asset`:

| Attribute | Why required | Clause |
|---|---|---|
| `hqlaLevel` | a "level one high-quality liquid assets" row cannot place an asset without the asset's level (BA 310 is a level-1 reserve/liquid-asset return) | SARB reg 26 / reg 27; Basel LCR BCBS D295 §24–§54 |

Sliced / driver attributes (`hqlaEligibility`, `hqlaHaircut`, `repoEligibility` for the resale/repo-agreement rows) are attached `required:false` — the aggregate still folds. The deposit-funding attributes also appear `required:false` where a row mentions funding (the head-office / inter-branch funding adjustments), since they slice but do not populate-or-die. **The NPA gate binds** the future HQLA-eligible-asset product on its owed `required:true` attribute. The live FX product feeds NO BA 310 product-attribute cell and is therefore **not** blocked.

> **Honest substrate gap (Engineering Charter cmd 3 + 5).** As BA 300: the product-scope declaration schema is FX-shaped; an HQLA-asset product is approvable only by tracking each owed attribute as a `ProductDeferredGap` until the schema is extended. Tracked, not hidden.

## 3. Cell-population steps (delta on PROC-FIN-BA-01 §6 "data extraction")

For each of the 63 cells, the generator:

1. **Reads the contract entry** via `loadReturnContract("BA310")`.
2. **Resolves the value by `derivation.kind`:**
   - `direct` → fold the cell's figure from the **`ba310-min-reserve-liquid-assets-fold`** projection: the **HQLA stock fold** (`platform/reporting/hqla-stock.ts`) over the **HQLA classifier** (`platform/collateral/hqla-classifier.ts`) and **SecurityMaster** (`platform/projections/markets/security-master.ts`) for the level-1 liquid-asset stock (gold, treasury bills, central-bank reserves, securities); the reserve-balance computation off the liabilities base (which ties to BA 100 line R0790). Cell values are **projections, never stored** (P1).
   - `sum` / `formula` → evaluate the SARB cell-coordinate expression (`derivation.expression`) over the already-resolved constituent cells.
3. **Honours status — no silent zeros, no fabrication (honest per-cell split):**
   - **`sourced`** — the level-1 liquid-asset stock cells (Reserve Bank notes and coin, gold coin and bullion, central-bank reserves, treasury bills, resale-agreement level-1 HQLA) have **live substrate** (HQLA classifier + SecurityMaster). Their source genuinely exists; values fold to an honest 0 until positions land.
   - **`licence-day-data`** — the minimum-reserve-balance and liabilities-base cells need the real liabilities base and held liquid-asset positions the bank-in-formation does not book pre-licence-day. The `statusReason` states this on every such cell.

## 4. XSD validation before submission (BA-310-specific hard gate)

**Before** the four-eyes review (PROC-FIN-BA-01 §7), the generated BA 310 instance MUST validate against `BA310.xsd`:

1. Serialise the populated cells into the BA 310 upload XML (the `BAxxxxxxxx` element envelope; the `Monetary1000` value wrappers; the `BA310_HQLAOther` sub-form structure).
2. Validate against `Regulations/SARB-PA/ba-returns/schemas/BA310.zip → BA310.xsd`.
3. **Fail-closed (Engineering Charter cmd 2):** an XSD-invalid instance is NOT eligible for review or submission; the failure is a blocking finding, never suppressed.

This is in addition to the build-time completeness guarantee enforced by `recon:ba-return-cell-contract` (every XSD cell has a contract entry — asserted for BA 310 alongside the other authored returns).

## 5. Reconciliation invariants (BA-310-specific)

| Invariant | Assertion |
|---|---|
| **Minimum-reserve tie** | The average daily minimum reserve balance to be held with the Reserve Bank equals the required-reserve percentage (SARB Act 90 of 1989; reg 27) applied to the **liabilities base, as adjusted** (the "Liabilities, as adjusted" / "as reduced" lines, which tie to **BA 100 line R0790, C0030**). A reserve-balance cell not equal to `required% × adjusted-liabilities-base` is a control failure. |
| **Liabilities-base tie to BA 100** | The "Liabilities as per Line no. R0790, C0030 of form BA 100" cell equals the corresponding BA 100 balance-sheet cell for the same period; a cross-return divergence is a control failure (the same liabilities figure must reconcile across BA 100 and BA 310). |
| **Level-1 liquid-asset stock integrity** | The total level-1 liquid-asset holdings equal the sum of the constituent HQLA-classifier-sourced rows (Reserve Bank notes/coin, gold, central-bank reserves, treasury bills, securities, less the specified set-offs and adjustments) per `derivation.expression`. |
| **Set-off / adjustment correctness** | The regulation-13 set-off, regulation-16 cash-management adjustment, and the head-office / inter-branch funding adjustments are applied per the SARB-prescribed signs (Add: / Less:) before the reserve requirement is computed. |
| **Trace-to-source** | Every **populated** cell traces to its `ba310-min-reserve-liquid-assets-fold` source datum; a populated cell with no resolved source is a control failure (no value without a source — P1/P2). |
