## 1. Why this seat exists

Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.94(2) requires a public company's Audit Committee to have at least three non-executive directors with at least one carrying appropriate financial / accounting expertise. Companies Regulation 43 layers a Social & Ethics Committee with at least one independent NED. SARB Prudential Authority + King IV expect the Board Chair to be independent.

The "minimum possible" composition collapses three bindings — Independent Chair, AC Chair, S&E independent NED — into **one person** (per Owen+Imani §3, item 2). This is the load-bearing seat in the three-NED floor.

## 3. Mandate boundary

**In scope:**

- Chair of the Board — sets agenda, presides over meetings, manages CEO relationship.
- Chair of the Audit Committee — under Companies Act s.94(7), AC reports separately to shareholders.
- Independent NED member of the Social & Ethics Committee (the same individual; the S&E and AC may share members per Owen+Imani §2.2 caveat).
- AC oversight of external auditor (firm) appointment, independence, fees.
- Standing escalation point for whistleblowing under Companies Act s.159.
- Co-sign of the licence-application file as the Board Chair.

**Out of scope:**

- Day-to-day management — the CEO (Marc) owns first-line management.
- Risk Committee chair — held by the human CRO (separate seat, priority 1).
- Compliance / AML-CFT — the CCO (Zara, governance line; future human compliance lead, priority 5) owns.
- Internal audit — Thandiwe (CAE) owns; AC oversees but does not run.

- **Reports to:** shareholders (the Board reports to shareholders; the Chair is the named lead).
- **Committee membership:** Chair of Board; Chair of Audit Committee; member of Social & Ethics Committee (independent-NED slot); standing attendee at Risk Committee (chaired by the human CRO).
- **Engagement letter terms:** King IV NED appointment shape — letter of appointment, term of office (typically 3 years renewable once at small-bank shape), independence undertaking, fit-and-proper attestation.

## 7. Search criteria

**Must-have:**

- Substantive independence under King IV + Companies Act s.94 — no material business / financial / family relationship with the bank or its group.
- 15+ years senior leadership experience including at least 5 years in board / NED capacity.
- Appropriate financial / accounting expertise — qualified accountant (CA(SA) / equivalent), former CFO, or equivalent demonstrable financial-reporting fluency.
- Banks Act + PA fit-and-proper credible.
- Demonstrated prior AC chair experience or AC member experience at a regulated financial institution.
- South African resident.
- Available to commit to Board + AC + S&E meeting cadence (estimated 18–25 meeting-days per year).

**Nice-to-have:**

- Prior NED at a SARB-regulated bank.
- Banking-regulatory experience (prior in-house regulatory engagement; senior secondment or equivalent).
- Trading-bank exposure (institutional global-markets posture).
- B-BBEE positive contribution.
- Joint Standard 2 of 2024 fluency for AC oversight of cybersecurity.

**Automatic disqualifiers:**

- Material business relationship with the bank or its group (lender, supplier, customer above de minimis threshold).
- Family or close personal relationship with Marc (CEO).
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank or material counterparty.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings.
- Cannot meet substantive-independence test under King IV + Companies Act s.94.
