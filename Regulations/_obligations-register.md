## How to read this register

Every obligations table row carries the same nine columns (per v1.13 schema unification):

- **ID** — a stable register identifier `ORG-<prefix>-<n>` for cross-referencing. The 23 prefixes seen in IDs are: `AC, BNK, CD, CS1, CS2, CS3, CY, EL, EXCON, FAIS, FC, FMA, FX, GRP, GV, HR, JN2, JS2, MK, PR, RM, TX, WB`. Some prefixes carry sub-domain meaning (`BNK` and `GRP` distinguish bank-entity-with-consolidated-measurement vs group-direct rows under D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER; `CS1` / `CS2` / `CS3` track FSCA Conduct Standards 1, 2, 3 of 2018 as separate instruments; `JS2` and `JN2` track Joint Standard 2 of 2020 and Joint Notice 2 of 2024; `EXCON` carries the Excon-ODP non-resident derivative reporting row; `FAIS` and `RM` map to Domain P and Domain RM respectively).
- **URN** — machine-readable URN slug (`urn:obligation:bank:*:v1`). For rows that do not yet carry an explicit URN, this reads `[TBD]` — populating URNs across the register is a follow-on workstream `WS-URN-COVERAGE` per `Owner Inbox/2026-05-10_mira_obligations-register-v1.13-reconciliation.md`.
- **Citation** — instrument and section / clause.
- **Requirement** — plain-English statement of what the regulator requires.
- **Fulfilment policy** — where in the bank's policy library this obligation is met (cross-reference to `Owner Inbox/2026-05-06_policy-register.md` and the bundle files).
- **Owner** — accountable seat (typically a governance seat, with the engineering seat in parentheses).
- **Status** — bare lifecycle status: `IN FORCE` (policy approved & live) · `DRAFTING` · `PLANNED` · `PARTIAL` · `N/A-yet` (e.g., FAIS-conditional when no FSP licence held) · `corporate-bind` / `licence-bind` / `commencement-bind` / `conditional-bind` (per `project_rules_bind_at_commencement.md` — when an obligation is registered against a future binding-trigger rather than already in force) · `wave-2-deferred` (Domain FX long-tail) · `PRE-LICENCE` · `IN FLIGHT` · `PHASED`. Per v1.13 the Status cell no longer carries `entity-scope:` / `applies-at:` annotations — those values are now in their dedicated columns.
- **Entity scope** — one of `group`, `bank`, `securities`, `multi-entity`, `consolidated-supervision`, or `[TBD]` (per the v1.8 vocabulary in the "Entity scope" section below). Rows authored before v1.8 inherited an implicit-`bank` default; v1.13 makes the unclassified state explicit by reading `[TBD]` rather than baking the implicit default into the column. Resolution sprint is workstream `WS-ENTITY-SCOPE-CLASSIFICATION`.
- **Applies-at** — one of `entity-only`, `consolidated`, `look-through`, or `[TBD]` (per the v1.9 vocabulary in the "`applies-at`" section below). Resolution sprint is workstream `WS-APPLIES-AT-CLASSIFICATION`.

**Exempt from the 9-column schema:** Domain N (the M1 markets-foundation citation-URN inventory — `Symbol | URN slug | Tranche | Instrument | Section | First consumed at | Confidence | Cross-references`) is structurally distinct from the obligations rows; it provides machine-readable handles that obligations rows cite. The Status-summary, Entity-scope-vocabulary, and Applies-at-vocabulary tables are also exempt — they are explanatory tables, not obligations rows.

Where multiple regulators converge on the same obligation, it is listed once with all relevant citations.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `group` | Binds **only** at the group / `Hoz Group Limited` level (e.g. consolidated capital adequacy under Banks Act § 60+; group recovery plan; consolidated financial statements under IFRS 10). |
| `bank` | Binds **only** at `Hoz Bank Limited` (e.g. SARB FinSurv reporting under the Currency and Exchanges Manual; Authorised Dealer obligations; BA returns to the Prudential Authority). |
| `securities` | Binds **only** at `Hoz Securities Limited` (e.g. FAIS Key Individual; FSP advice records / suitability assessments; FSCA conduct-of-business; ODP trade-reporting and margin under JS 2 of 2020). |
| `multi-entity` | Binds at **two or more** entities — the row enumerates which entities and how the obligation manifests at each (e.g. FIC Act Section 43A AML/CFT obligations apply to **both** `Hoz Bank Limited` and `Hoz Securities Limited` as separately-licensed accountable institutions; POPIA Information Officer designation under ss.55–56 owed **per-entity**; Companies Act 71 of 2008 director-duties bind every group entity). |
| `consolidated-supervision` | Binds at the **group level under Banks Act § 60+ AND simultaneously at each entity** — i.e. there is a group-level discharge that aggregates / consolidates the per-entity discharges, plus distinct entity-level discharges (e.g. cyber-resilience programme under Joint Standard 2 of 2024 — group programme + per-entity controls; consolidated ICAAP / ILAAP — group-level capital / liquidity adequacy + bank-entity ICAAP / ILAAP under the bank's standalone obligations). **Note (v1.9):** under D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER (PR #85, 2026-05-09), most rows previously framed as binding "at the group level under § 60+" are more precisely framed as binding on `Hoz Bank Limited` and *measured* at consolidated level via PA look-through — these rows now use `entity-scope: bank` with `applies-at: consolidated` (see below). The `consolidated-supervision` value is retained for rows where the group-level discharge is itself a separate obligation distinct from the per-entity discharge (e.g. `ORG-CY-02` per-entity vs the group cyber-resilience programme). |

### `applies-at` — where the obligation is measured / reported

> *Added 2026-05-09 (v1.9) under CEO decision `D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER` (approved 2026-05-09; record `Owner Inbox/2026-05-09_scrooge_ceo-decision-record_d-regulatory-perimeter.md` / PR #85). Source: §"What this clarifies" item 2 + §"Follow-on routes recorded" Mira route.*

The `entity-scope` field answers **on which entity the obligation binds**. The `applies-at` field answers **at what aggregation level the obligation is measured / reported / supervised**. The two are distinct because — under the regulatory-perimeter clarification — `Hoz Group Limited` is not separately regulated by the SARB PA in its own right; the PA exercises **look-through** from `Hoz Bank Limited` to assess group-wide risk. Consolidated-CGPs / ICAAP / ILAAP / recovery / cyber-resilience are obligations that **bind on the bank entity** but are **measured on a consolidated basis** including subsidiaries within scope of consolidation.

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `entity-only` | **Default.** The obligation is measured at the entity stated in `entity-scope`. No consolidation, no look-through. (e.g. SARB FinSurv reporting under the Currency and Exchanges Manual binds on `Hoz Bank Limited` and is measured at the bank entity — not consolidated.) |
| `consolidated` | The obligation binds at the entity stated in `entity-scope`, but is **measured / reported on a consolidated basis** via PA look-through. The aggregation perimeter is the group of entities within consolidation scope under Banks Act § 60+ + IFRS 10. (e.g. consolidated ICAAP binds on `Hoz Bank Limited` under PA prudential supervision but the capital adequacy ratio is computed including `Hoz Securities Limited` and any future group entity within scope.) |
| `look-through` | The SARB PA (or other regulator) reads through the entity to assess group-wide risk. The **obligation may bind on the parent (`Hoz Group Limited`) or on the entity** depending on the regulatory anchor — the citation is decisive. (e.g. parent-of-bank PA notification binds on the group; PA look-through prudential ratios bind on the bank with consolidated measurement.) |

**Default for un-classified rows.** Existing rows authored before v1.9 default to `applies-at: entity-only` (the field is not set explicitly on rows where `entity-scope: bank` and consolidation does not apply). The default is **provisional** for rows where the obligation may carry a consolidated reading; full classification of consolidated-vs-entity-only is part of the v1 substrate task already named under v1.8.

**Authority chain.** D-REGULATORY-PERIMETER refines D-LEGAL-ENTITY-TREE-V0 (PR #82) at the regulatory-perimeter layer; the `applies-at` vocabulary is the operational expression of "the bank is subject to PA prudential regulation; the group is not separately regulated; PA look-through assesses group-wide risk via the bank entity" (CEO posture statement, 2026-05-09).
