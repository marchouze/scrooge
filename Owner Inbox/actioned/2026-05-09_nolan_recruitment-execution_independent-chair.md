## 2. Search-firm engagement

Per role-brief §7.1, two SA Board-search executive-search firms are engaged in parallel. The IODSA channel runs alongside as a diligence cross-check (see §5).

### 2.1 Firm selection rationale

PAX role-brief §7.1 names the typical SA Board-search universe: Spencer Stuart SA, Heidrick & Struggles SA, Egon Zehnder SA, Korn Ferry SA, Russell Reynolds SA, plus SA-only firms (Jack Hammer, GR8 Leaders, Search Partners International, Boyden SA, Amrop SA). From this universe, Nolan (Recruiter) selects **two** firms in parallel; the criteria are: (i) substantive SA-bank Board-search practice (not generic exec search); (ii) demonstrated AC-chair-tenure deliveries on SA-listed banks in the past five years; (iii) substantive-independence diligence as part of the standard product (not bolt-on); (iv) firm independence from the bank's group and from each other (no shared engagement-history with the bank's near-counterparties).

**Selected firms (in priority order):**

#### Firm 1 — Spencer Stuart South Africa

**Why selected.** Spencer Stuart's SA practice has the densest Board-Chair-search bench in the SA-listed-bank cluster over the past decade — substantive engagements at multiple SA-Tier-2 / mutual / co-operative-bank Chair searches, the Investec subsidiary-Board universe, and the SA-insurer Board cluster that PAX names as the comparator class (role-brief §6.1). Spencer Stuart's "Director and Board Services" practice runs substantive-independence diligence as part of the standard product, including the King IV substantive-read (not just the Companies Act s.94 formal-read), which the role-brief §5 names as the binding gate. Spencer Stuart's IODSA-network reach is high, including CD(SA) and Fellow rosters that the role-brief §3 names as nice-to-have credentials. Cultural fit for an institutional global-markets bank with R300m capital is credible — Spencer Stuart has historically advised on smaller-balance-sheet financial-institution Boards, not only the large four.

**Engagement-letter scope.** Retained search; exclusive on this seat for the engagement period. Scope items: longlist construction (15–25 candidates per role-brief §7.2); initial-diligence shortlist (8–12); short-list (3–5); reference temperatures; substantive-independence pre-screen (formal s.94 + King IV substantive read); CEO + Owen briefing pack on each shortlist candidate; offer-stage support including substantive-independence attestation discussion. Off-limits clauses: the bank's named near-counterparties (correspondent-bank candidates, SAMOS-sponsor candidates per `project_indirect_participant_posture.md`) excluded by name; the firm's own client-roster off-limits standard applies to the bank's eventual external counsel and external auditor.

**Fee structure framing.** Retained search; convention in SA Board-search is **30–35% of first-year director fees**, paid by the bank in three instalments — engagement, shortlist, completion. The Chair seat's fees-base for the percentage calculation is the **aggregate** of the Board Chair retainer + AC Chair fee + S&E member fee (role-brief §6.2). Indicative search-firm fee envelope: **0.35 × (Chair retainer + AC fee + S&E fee)**, with the absolute number deferred to Camille's licence-day human-cost budget pending the comparator-class numbers (role-brief §6.5).

**Reporting cadence.** Weekly written update from Spencer Stuart's lead search consultant to Nolan (Recruiter); fortnightly call (45 min) with Nolan and Owen on the engineering-substrate side; monthly presentation to Marc (CEO) on longlist-to-shortlist progress.

#### Firm 2 — Egon Zehnder South Africa

**Why selected.** Egon Zehnder's SA practice carries a complementary profile to Spencer Stuart — stronger weight on the Board-effectiveness diagnostic alongside the search itself, which is useful given the role-brief §7.3 skills-matrix complementarity argument. Egon Zehnder's "Banking and Financial Services" Board practice has substantive AC-chair-search history at SA insurers and fund-administrators, complementing Spencer Stuart's bank-only weight; for a Chair seat that must clear AC-chair-tenure as a must-have, parallel engagement of two firms with different but adjacent rolodexes maximises longlist depth without duplicating coverage. Egon Zehnder also runs a structured substantive-independence diligence product. Cultural fit: Egon Zehnder has advised SA-based banks-in-formation in the past; the licence-application-gate posture is familiar to the firm.

**Engagement-letter scope.** Retained search; non-exclusive (parallel with Spencer Stuart) on this seat. Same scope-items as Firm 1 (longlist; initial-diligence shortlist; short-list; references; substantive-independence; offer-stage). Off-limits clauses: same list as Firm 1, plus mutual exclusivity with Spencer Stuart on candidates already surfaced (the firm that surfaces a candidate first carries the candidate through to shortlist; cross-presented candidates are flagged at the longlist stage so the second firm's longlist is reweighted).

**Fee structure framing.** Retained search; same convention as Firm 1 (30–35% of first-year director fees). Indicative fee envelope identical. Aggregate two-firm spend therefore lands in the **0.50–0.70 × first-year director-fee aggregate** envelope across the two firms — convention in SA parallel-firm Board-search engagements is for the firm that closes the seat to be paid the full fee, with a partial fee to the firm that did not close (typically 30–50% of the closing-firm's fee, recognising substantive longlist work). Final fee structure deferred to Camille (Chief Financial Officer, governance) and the Remuneration Committee.

**Reporting cadence.** Same as Firm 1 — weekly written update; fortnightly call with Nolan and Owen; monthly to Marc.

## 3. Candidate-class definition

The longlist screen distils the role-brief §3 must-haves into a structured candidate-class profile. Spencer Stuart and Egon Zehnder receive this profile as the Section A of the engagement-letter brief.

### 3.1 Hard-floor profile (must-have, binding gate)

A candidate enters the longlist only if **all** of the following hold:

1. **King IV substantive-independence test passable** — at the relationship structure, not just the formal-employment relationship. Per role-brief §5.5, the candidate must clear all ten worked-example disqualifiers (no recent executive role; no current/recent audit-firm partnership; no current/recent regular-advisor relationship; no counterparty-Board overlap; no family relationship with any licence-day human seat; no controlled-entity material-business relationship; no income concentration > 25%; no recent share-incentive scheme participation; no tenure > 9 years on a related Board; no concurrent NED at competitor SA bank or material counterparty).
2. **Companies Act s.94 formal substantive-independence test passable** — the floor (role-brief §5.1).
3. **AC chairmanship track record** — minimum 2 financial years as AC Chair at a JSE-listed entity OR AC Chair at a regulated financial institution (role-brief §3 must-have on AC chairmanship). AC member experience without chair tenure does not pass.
4. **Companies Act s.94(4)(b) financial-expertise designation candidate** — qualified accountant (CA(SA), ACCA, CIMA, or equivalent) OR demonstrable BA-return-level financial-reporting fluency. The Chair seat carries the s.94(4)(b) designation unless NED #2 or #3 is structurally certain to (role-brief §3 must-have on financial-statements literacy).
5. **SA banking-regulatory fluency** — substantive familiarity with SARB PA / FSCA / FIC supervisory practice. Need not be former regulator; must be able to engage SARB on Board-composition and fit-and-proper at the licence-application gate (role-brief §3 must-have on banking-regulatory fluency).
6. **Companies Act s.69 disqualification screen passable** — clean (no un-rehabilitated insolvency; no court order of mental incapacity; no FAIS de-barment; no prior removal for breach of duty; no fraud/forgery/perjury/theft/breach-of-trust conviction under SA financial-services legislation or comparable foreign regime).
7. **PA fit-and-proper screen passable** — probity, competence, financial soundness; no FSCA or PA enforcement history without satisfactory disposition (role-brief §4).
8. **SA residence** — per role-brief §9.1 PAX recommendation accepted by the CEO (international Chairs ruled out at licence-day).
9. **Capacity** — able to commit to ≈ 18–25 meeting-days per year; not concurrently a Chair at another listed-entity (concurrent NED roles capped at three per role-brief §3 strong-fit).

### 3.2 Strong-fit profile (preferred shape)

Above the hard-floor, candidates are scored upward on:

- Prior NED tenure at a SARB-licensed Tier-2 / mutual / co-operative bank with substantive engagement on PA fit-and-proper, ICAAP, ILAAP, BA-return architecture, IFRS 9 ECL adoption.
- AC chair through an external-auditor change or through a SARB supervisory engagement.
- S&E Committee Chair tenure; engagement with B-BBEE Codes 2013/2019 verification.
- Joint Standard 1 of 2024 fluency for the AC's cybersecurity-oversight agenda item.
- IODSA Chartered Director (CD(SA)) credential.

### 3.3 Differentiating profile (nice-to-have)

- Prior Chair role at a SA-licensed bank or insurer.
- Prior IODSA Director-Development or King-IV-trainer credentialing.
- Material advancement of the bank's B-BBEE Codes contribution (gender, race, disability) — provided substantive-independence and AC-chairmanship-tenure hard-floor are not relaxed (role-brief §7.4).
- Markets / trading-bank exposure (the bank's institutional global-markets posture — `project_strategic_foundation.md`).

### 3.4 Hard fails (automatic disqualifiers)

Distinguished from "did not score highly" — these eliminate at longlist:

- Failure of any Companies Act s.69 disqualifier.
- Material business / financial / family relationship with the bank's group beyond the directorship itself.
- Concurrent directorship at a competitor SA bank or material counterparty.
- Prior fit-and-proper findings.
- Failure of King IV substantive-independence on any of the ten role-brief §5.5 worked-example dimensions.
- Two or more concurrent SA-listed-Bank Chair roles (capacity-test fail).

## 4. Sourcing channels beyond search firms

The two-firm parallel engagement is the primary channel; below are the diligence-and-cross-check channels Nolan (Recruiter) runs alongside, in line with role-brief §7.1.

### 4.1 IODSA director-database

The Institute of Directors South Africa (IODSA) maintains the SA director registry. The CD(SA) and Fellow rosters are searchable. Nolan (Recruiter) requests a directed search at engagement-T+15 (post engagement-letter signing) on:
- CA(SA)-credentialed CD(SA) Fellows with prior AC-chairmanship at JSE-listed financial-services entities.
- Substantive-independence-clean candidates with prior NED tenure at SA-listed banks (filtering against the off-limits list in §2.3).
- Candidates with prior AC-chairmanship through an external-auditor change at a regulated entity (a structural marker of the kind of substantive-engagement experience the role-brief §3 strong-fit names).

The IODSA channel produces volume **lower** than the search firms' internal databases but is the strongest source of King IV substantive-read diligence — the IODSA director registry includes substantive-independence attestations on Fellows, which Nolan cross-references against Spencer Stuart's and Egon Zehnder's longlists.

### 4.2 JSE-listed-bank Chair alumni mapping

The set of individuals who have served as Chair (or AC Chair) at a JSE-listed bank or major SA insurer in the past 10 years is finite — Nolan (Recruiter) compiles the list at engagement-T+10 from JSE Listings disclosures, BASA committee histories, and IODSA Fellow rosters. Each individual is screened against the off-limits list (§2.3) and against role-brief §5.5 worked-example #9 (King IV nine-year tenure clock) and §9.6 (cross-Board career-tenure aging-out). Survivors are cross-referenced into the search firms' longlists.

### 4.3 B-BBEE-compliant candidate pool considerations

Per role-brief §7.4, the search targets a Chair whose appointment materially advances the bank's B-BBEE Codes contribution **provided** substantive-independence and AC-chairmanship-tenure hard-floor are not relaxed. The B-BBEE-positive candidate pool intersects the JSE-listed-bank Chair alumni list (§4.2) and the IODSA Fellow roster (§4.1) substantively but not exclusively. Nolan (Recruiter) directs both Spencer Stuart and Egon Zehnder to run a parallel B-BBEE-positive pass on the longlist construction — explicitly: female candidates, black African / coloured / Indian candidates, candidates with disability — and to surface the candidates' demographic profile at longlist for Marc (CEO)'s and Owen (Company Secretary, governance)'s skills-matrix-mix review.

### 4.4 CGISA Chair-fellow network

The Chartered Governance Institute of Southern Africa (CGISA, formerly Chartered Secretaries Southern Africa) maintains a Fellow roster of senior governance professionals. The Chair-fellow subset overlaps with the IODSA Fellow set but adds candidates whose Chair-readiness is demonstrated through long Company-Secretary careers transitioning into NED roles. This is a niche but high-quality channel for the AC Chair financial-expertise dimension when paired with a CA(SA) credential. CGISA reach is requested at engagement-T+30.

### 4.5 Marc (CEO)'s personal / professional network — last resort, with enhanced diligence

Per role-brief §7.1, the CEO-network channel is **last resort** because of the substantive-independence-test exposure. Nolan (Recruiter) opens this channel only if the search-firm and IODSA channels have not produced a viable shortlist by T+90; any candidate surfaced through Marc's network is subject to **enhanced** substantive-independence diligence (Spencer Stuart's product applied as a separate engagement; documented for the Sade fit-and-proper file). Any prior commercial relationship between Marc and the candidate is an automatic disqualifier (role-brief §5.5 worked-example #5 + #6).

## 5. Screening rubric

The rubric below is the longlist-to-shortlist scoring tool. Each candidate is scored on each dimension; the binding gate is the hard-floor (§3.1) — failure on any one is elimination, not low score. Scoring is on a 1–5 scale; the shortlist composition target is candidates with **average ≥ 4.0 on strong-fit** AND **all hard-floor items passed**.

### 5.1 Hard-floor pass/fail (binding gate)

| # | Dimension | Test | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| HF-1 | King IV substantive-independence | All ten role-brief §5.5 worked-example dimensions clear. Investigated at shortlist depth, not at longlist. | King IV Principle 7. |
| HF-2 | Companies Act s.94 substantive-independence | s.94(4)–(6) formal test clear; s.94(5) regular-advisor exclusion clear. | Companies Act 71 of 2008 s.94. |
| HF-3 | AC chairmanship track record | ≥ 2 financial years AC Chair at JSE-listed or regulated-FSI entity. | Role-brief §3. |
| HF-4 | Financial-expertise designation eligibility | CA(SA) / ACCA / CIMA / equivalent OR demonstrable BA-return-level fluency. | Companies Act s.94(4)(b). |
| HF-5 | SA banking-regulatory fluency | Substantive SARB PA / FSCA / FIC familiarity at the licence-application-engagement level. | Role-brief §3. |
| HF-6 | Companies Act s.69 disqualifier screen | Clean. | Companies Act s.69. |
| HF-7 | PA fit-and-proper screen | Clean. | Banks Act s.60(5)–(6) + PA fit-and-proper sub-instruments. |
| HF-8 | SA residence | Resident in SA. | Role-brief §9.1 (PAX recommendation accepted). |
| HF-9 | Capacity | 18–25 meeting-days/year available; ≤ 1 other listed-entity Chair role; ≤ 3 other NED roles. | Role-brief §3. |

**A candidate failing any HF dimension is eliminated.** No exceptions; no balancing-of-interests; no overall-score override. The hard-floor is the binding gate, full stop.

### 5.2 Strong-fit scoring (1–5)

| # | Dimension | Scoring anchor |
|---|---|---|
| SF-1 | SA-licensed-bank Board experience | 1: none; 3: NED at SA-listed financial-services entity; 5: NED at SARB-licensed bank with substantive PA engagement. |
| SF-2 | AC chairmanship depth | 1: 2 years at one entity; 3: 4–6 years across one or more entities; 5: AC chair through external-auditor change or SARB supervisory engagement. |
| SF-3 | S&E / sustainability oversight | 1: none beyond minimum; 3: prior S&E Committee membership; 5: S&E Committee Chair tenure with B-BBEE / ESG substance. |
| SF-4 | Banking-regulatory fluency | 1: financial-services-generic; 3: substantive SARB / FSCA engagement; 5: prior PA / FSCA / FIC officer or BASA committee tenure. |
| SF-5 | Financial-statements literacy | 1: non-CA, BA-return self-taught; 3: CA(SA); 5: CA(SA) + prior CFO or audit-partner tenure at regulated FSI. |
| SF-6 | Technology / cyber awareness (JS 1/2024) | 1: minimum-credible; 3: prior tech-committee membership; 5: tech-savvy NED with practitioner background. |
| SF-7 | Director duties baseline | 1: no IODSA credential; 3: CD(SA); 5: IODSA Fellow + teaching/writing. |
| SF-8 | Capacity headroom | 1: at the cap (18–25 days, 1 other Chair, 3 NEDs); 3: comfortably below cap; 5: meaningful availability for unscheduled engagement. |

### 5.3 Differentiating-fit scoring (1–5)

| # | Dimension | Scoring anchor |
|---|---|---|
| DF-1 | Prior Chair role at SA bank/insurer | 1: none; 3: insurer-Chair tenure; 5: SA-licensed-bank Chair tenure. |
| DF-2 | B-BBEE Codes contribution | 1: no contribution to mix; 3: positive contribution on one dimension; 5: material advancement on multiple dimensions. |
| DF-3 | Markets / trading-bank exposure | 1: none; 3: institutional FSI Board exposure; 5: prior trading-bank Board / Markets-Committee tenure. |
| DF-4 | International / cross-border experience | 1: SA-only; 3: one cross-border NED role; 5: substantive international-bank or cross-jurisdictional regulatory exposure. |
| DF-5 | Regulator-alumni credibility | 1: none; 3: BASA committee tenure; 5: former PA / FSCA / FIC senior officer (subject to PA cooling-off where applicable). |

### 5.4 Composite scoring and shortlist composition

- **Hard-floor:** all 9 must pass. Failure on any one eliminates.
- **Strong-fit composite:** weighted average of SF-1 to SF-8, with SF-2 (AC chairmanship depth) and SF-5 (financial-statements literacy) double-weighted because they are the load-bearing AC-chair dimensions. Threshold for shortlist consideration: **≥ 4.0 weighted average**.
- **Differentiating-fit composite:** simple average of DF-1 to DF-5; used for shortlist *ordering*, not entry. Tiebreak in favour of candidates who materially advance the B-BBEE Codes mix per role-brief §7.4.

The shortlist target is **3–5 candidates** (role-brief §7.2): at most 2 candidates at "ideal fit" (SF ≥ 4.5, DF ≥ 4.0); at least 1 candidate at "credible-alternate-shape" (SF ≥ 4.0, DF different shape) to give the CEO a genuine choice; no candidate below the SF ≥ 4.0 threshold enters shortlist regardless of DF score.
