Operating launcher. Dashboards, reports, decisions, registers, and substrate operations all start here. Every tile is a query over the canonical sources — events, registers, procedures.

Dashboards

Live

Continuously re-derived from the event log and canonical registers. Numbers reflect the bank now.

Reports

Point-in-time renderings — board packs, regulator returns, financial statements, audit reports. Generated, never assembled (Principle 6 downward chain).

Decisions

Live

CEO and Board decisions. Each decision carries options, recommendation, and a citation chain back to the governance framework.

Registers

Canonical

The single citable sources. Obligations, policies, procedures, agents, regulations.

Live

Compliance
