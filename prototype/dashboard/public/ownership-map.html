Ownership Map

Agent ⟺ Domain ⟺ Obligation. Every obligation is classified to a normalised domain (domain-ownership-map.ts, D-DOMAIN-OWNERSHIP-MAP) and routed to its accountable agent. Coverage is enforced on two axes: every obligation owned, every domain owned (recon:domain-ownership-coherence). Owner-drift = obligations whose authored owner disagrees with their domain's accountable seat (remediation pending).
