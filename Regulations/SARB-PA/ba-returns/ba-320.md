## Purpose of BA 320

Form BA 320 is **the** prudential return for the bank's **market-risk (position-risk) capital charge**. Market risk is a **Pillar-1** risk: the bank must hold capital against the risk of loss in on- and off-balance-sheet positions arising from movements in market prices. The economic perimeter is the four risk classes of Reg 28(3) — interest-rate, equity, foreign-exchange (incl. gold), commodity. Under the BCBS market-risk framework as transposed into Reg 28, the charge is computed under the **FRTB Standardised Approach** (SBM + DRC + RRAO), with a **simplified standardised approach** available for banks with small / non-complex trading books (and an internal-models approach subject to prior PA approval — not elected). The market-RWA contribution (× 12.5) integrates into the BA 700 capital-adequacy aggregate via Reg 38.

The **simplified standardised approach** is well-established in the Basel architecture: banks with **small or non-complex trading books** may apply a **reduced-sensitivity** treatment so the capital methodology is proportionate to the size and complexity of the market-risk exposure. Whether the bank applies the full FRTB-SA or the simplified SA within BA 320 (the in-corpus §2.1.13 routes the substantive rules to *"the relevant requirements specified in the Regulations"*) is counsel-gated (see Open items). Both calibrations report on the **same form, BA 320**.

## Binding obligations on the bank (summary)

1. **Measure trading-book (and in-scope banking-book FX/commodity) market risk** and hold capital per Reg 28 — on the FRTB-SA basis, or the simplified SA where the small-trading-book proportionality applies (`ORG-PR-RETURNS-012`; Reg 28; BCBS MAR 20.1).
2. **Determine eligibility for the simplified-SA calibration** — the small / non-complex trading-book proportionality test (counsel-gated thresholds).
3. **Capitalise FX positions in either book** — the FX NOP attracts market-risk capital whether held in the banking book or the trading book (Reg 28(3)(c)).
4. **Integrate the market-risk charge into the capital-adequacy aggregate** — feed the capital (× 12.5 → market RWA) into BA 700 (Reg 38, integrating Reg 28/29).
5. **Submit form BA 320** to the PA per Annexure 12A of D5/2025 and the Regulations, on the prescribed (monthly) cadence (D5/2025 §2.1.13; the FRTB-SA-vs-simplified-SA calibration + cadence counsel-gated).
6. **Operate the market-risk appetite limits above the regulatory floor** — MR-1-FX (1-day 99% VaR) and the per-currency NOP sub-limits (internal RAS; the appetite layer, not this catalogue).
